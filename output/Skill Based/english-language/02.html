Rules & Ideas

Skill: Rhymes — read carefully before the examples and quiz.

What is a rhyme?

Words rhyme when their ending sounds match: cat/hat, log/dog, pen/hen.

Listen to the ending

Say both words aloud. Clap on the part that sounds the same: cat-HAT.

Word families

Rhyming words often share letters at the end: -at (cat, hat, mat), -en (pen, hen, ten).

Rhyme in poems

Poems and songs use rhymes so lines sound musical and are easier to remember.
