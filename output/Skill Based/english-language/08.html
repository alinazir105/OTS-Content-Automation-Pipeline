Rules & Ideas

Skill: Grammar spot — read carefully before the examples and quiz.

Plurals often add -s

one cat → two cats, one book → many books.

Possessive with 's

Sara's bag = the bag belongs to Sara (one Sara).

Plural possessive

the boys' room = the room for many boys.

Common mistakes

The dogs bowl ❌ → The dog's bowl ✓ (one dog)

Key Words

  • plural
  • possessive
