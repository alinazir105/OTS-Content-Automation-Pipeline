📌 Introduction

Why do we need different tenses? Let's find out!

🌟 What are tenses?

In English, we use different tenses to talk about actions at different times. In this chapter, we will learn two important present tenses:

☀️

Present Simple

For habits, routines, and general truths

repeated actionsalways true
🔄

Present Continuous

For actions happening right now or planned

happening nowfuture plans

✅ Present Simple Tense

Used for repeated actions, habits, and general truths

📝 What is Present Simple?

The simple present tense talks about repeated actions and describes actions that are habitual or generally true.

Subject + Verb (1st Form) + Object

With he/she/it — add "-s" or "-es" to the verb

💡 Examples

✅ She reads every day.

❌ She doesn't read everyday.

❓ Does she read everyday?

✅ They play soccer on Sundays.

❌ They do not play soccer on Sunday.

❓ Do they play soccer on Sunday?

🔵 Stative Verbs

Verbs that show a state or condition, not an action

🔵 The Verb "BE"

The verb "be" is a stative verb to show a state or condition rather than an action.

🔄 Present Continuous Tense

Used for actions happening NOW or future arrangements

🔄 What is Present Continuous?

The present continuous tense describes an action happening right now.

Subject + is / am / are + Verb + ing + Object

DO YOU KNOW?

I = am  |  He, she, it, singular noun = is  |  We, you, they, plural = are

We also use the present continuous to talk about future plans and arrangements with other people:

"I'm having lunch at school tomorrow."

"Sami is playing tennis with Ali on Friday."

⏰ Time Expressions

Signal words that tell you which tense to use

🕐 Which time words go with each tense?

Certain time expressions are clues that tell us which tense to use!

🔵 Present Continuous
for NOW / around now

now today at the moment this week

🔴 Present Continuous
for FUTURE arrangements

tomorrow next Friday on Tuesday this evening

☀️ Present Simple time expressions:

every day always usually sometimes never on Sundays
  • ☀️ Present Simple: "She reads every day." — It happens regularly, it's a habit.
  • 🔄 Present Continuous: "She is reading at the moment." — It's happening right now.
  • 📅 Present Continuous for future: "Sami is playing tennis with Ali on Friday." — It's arranged.
  • 🌍 General truths: "Penguins live in Antarctica." — Always true, use Present Simple.
