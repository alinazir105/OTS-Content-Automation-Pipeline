Conjunctions

Conjunctions are words that join or connect words or sentences together. Learn how and, but, or, because, and so work — then practise with fun activities!

📖

Meet the Conjunctions

Conjunctions are connecting words. They join ideas in a sentence. There are five key conjunctions we'll learn today:

AND

Used to link two similar ideas in one sentence.

☀️ The sun is shining and it's 31°C.
🌡️ It's sunny and warm.
BUT

Used to contrast two different ideas in one sentence.

☀️ The sun is shining but it's −5°C.
❄️ It's sunny but cold.
OR

Used to talk about two different possibilities or options.

🏖️ Do you want to sit here or go inside?
🪑 We can sit outside or we can sit inside.
BECAUSE

Used to explain why something happens.

🌶️ I like it because it's hot and spicy.
🌧️ She stayed home because it was raining.
SO

Used to show the result of something.

🌧️ It was raining, so she stayed home.
😋 I was hungry, so I ate a snack.
