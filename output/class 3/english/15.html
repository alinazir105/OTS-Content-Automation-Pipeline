What Is "Be Going To"?

We use "be going to" to talk about plans or intentions for the future, or when something is very likely to happen based on present evidence.

📌 Formula

Subject + be (is / am / are) + going to + verb (1st form)
Planned Action

🗓 A Plan

When you have already decided to do something.

"I am going to visit my grandma tomorrow."
Intention

💡 An Intention

When you intend or mean to do something.

"She is going to start a new book tonight."
Future Plan

🌟 Future Plan

When a group has arranged to do something together.

"We are going to have a picnic on Sunday."
Prediction

🔮 Prediction

When you can see evidence that something will happen.

"Look at those clouds! It is going to rain."

📚 Subject + Be Verb Match

Subject Be verb Example
IamI am going to study.
He / She / ItisShe is going to buy a dress.
We / You / TheyareThey are going to play.

  • ✅I am going to study for my test. Affirmative
  • ✅She is going to buy a new dress. Affirmative
  • ❓Is she going to visit her friend? Question
  • ❓Are they going to come to the meeting? Question
  • 🚫They are not going to play outside. Negative
  • 🚫He is not going to join us for dinner. Negative

Will vs Be Going To

Both talk about the future — but they are used in different situations!

🗓 Be Going To

Use when you have already decided or made a plan, or when evidence shows something will happen.

Q: What are your plans for the weekend?
A: I'm going to tidy my bedroom. ✅
Look at those clouds! It is going to rain. ✅
They're going to get married. (decided) ✅

⚡ Will

Use for spontaneous decisions, offers, or promises made at the moment of speaking.

Q: Which notebook do you want?
A: Hmm... I'll have that one, please. ✅
I can't do this. — I'll help you! (offer) ✅
It's a secret. — I promise I won't tell anyone. ✅
