In this lesson, you will learn about adverbs — special words that give us more information about verbs. Just like detectives find clues, adverbs tell us the details of an action: how it was done, when it happened, and where it took place!

💡 What is an Adverb?

An adverb is a word that tells us more about a verb. It gives extra information about how, when, or where an action happens.

📌 Key Examples

She runs fast. (How does she run?)

He came yesterday. (When did he come?)

The children are playing outside. (Where are the children playing?)

📂 Types of Adverbs

⚡ Adverbs of Manner

Tell us HOW an action is done
slowly quickly loudly happily quietly beautifully

The cat walked slowly across the room.

She sang the song beautifully.

The dog barked loudly at the strangers.

He walked slowly to enjoy the cool breeze.

⏰ Adverbs of Time

Tell us WHEN an action happens
now later today yesterday soon tomorrow

I will finish my homework tomorrow.

I will start my homework now.

We went on a picnic yesterday.

She will call you tomorrow.
