📖 What is Composition?

📝 Composition Explained

A composition is a piece of writing where you use your own words to express ideas, tell a story, describe something, or share your feelings.


Good composition involves:

  • Choosing a topic and thinking about what to write
  • Organising your ideas in a clear order
  • Using correct grammar and punctuation
  • Including interesting details and examples
  • Writing a proper beginning, middle and end

💡 Remember: Every piece of writing tells the reader something. Think about who you are writing for (your audience) and what you want them to learn or feel.

📝 Types of Writing in This Chapter
Type 1

🐠 Descriptive Writing

You describe a person, place, animal or thing using vivid details. You tell the reader what it looks like, how it moves, and why it is special.

Example: Writing about your favourite sea creature.

Type 2

✉️ Letter Writing

You write a letter to a friend or family member. A friendly letter has a special format: address, date, greeting, body, closing, and signature.

Example: Writing about a day at the seaside.

Type 3

📚 Creative Story

You create an imaginary story with characters, a setting, a problem and a solution. Stories have a beginning, a middle and an end.

Example: The Kindness Chain story.

Type 4

💭 Reflective Writing

You think deeply about a topic — a festival, a person, or an experience — and share what it means to you personally.

Example: Reflecting on festivals in Pakistan.

Type 5

🌟 Informative Writing

You share facts and useful information about a topic clearly and in an organised way so the reader learns something new.

Example: Writing tips on how to stay healthy.

Type 6

🦸 Character Writing

You write about a real or imaginary person — what they do, what they look like, and why they are important or inspiring to you.

Example: Writing about a community helper.

🐠 Topic 23.1 – My Favourite Sea Creature

🌊 What to Write About

Choose a sea creature you love — a dolphin, an octopus, a clownfish, a sea turtle, or a whale!


Think about these questions as you write:

  • What does it look like? (colour, size, shape)
  • Where does it live in the ocean?
  • What does it eat?
  • What is interesting or special about it?
  • Why is it your favourite?

🐬 Sample Sea Creatures

🐬 Dolphin
Playful, intelligent mammal that leaps out of water.
🐢 Sea Turtle
Ancient reptile that swims slowly and lives for 100+ years.
🐙 Octopus
Eight-armed creature that can change colour to hide!
🐠 Clownfish
Bright orange fish that lives among sea anemones.

✨ Example Paragraph: My Favourite Sea Creature

My favourite sea creature is the dolphin. Dolphins are beautiful, silver-grey animals that live in warm oceans all around the world. They are very intelligent — they can even communicate with each other using special clicks and whistles! Dolphins love to swim in groups called pods and they often leap gracefully out of the water. I admire dolphins because they are friendly, playful and always seem to be happy. One day, I hope to see a real dolphin swimming in the sea!

✉️ Topic 23.2 – Letter Writing: A Day at the Seaside

A friendly letter is a letter you write to someone you know — like a friend or a family member. It has a special format that you must follow.

📋 Parts of a Friendly Letter

Part 1

📍 Address & Date

Write your address at the top right, then the date below it. Example: 12 Garden Street, Lahore. 10th June 2024

Part 2

👋 Greeting (Salutation)

Begin with "Dear [Name]," — always use a comma after the name.

Part 3

📝 Body

The main part of the letter — share your news, experiences, and what you want to say.

Part 4

💌 Closing & Signature

End with "Your friend," or "With love," then sign your name below.

📄 Sample Letter

Address: 5 Blue Wave Lane, Karachi.
Date: 15th July 2024

Greeting: Dear Fatima,

I hope you are well! I am writing to tell you about the most wonderful day I had at the seaside last weekend.

We arrived early in the morning when the sky was still a soft pink colour. The beach was covered in golden sand and tiny, shiny shells. I collected lots of shells and built a big sandcastle with my little brother. The waves kept crashing into it and we laughed every time!

We also saw colourful fish swimming near the rocks. Mum bought us coconut ice cream and we sat watching the sunset. It was truly magical!

I really wish you could have been there. Would you like to come with us next time? We could collect even more shells and play in the waves together. Please say yes!

Closing: Your loving friend,
Signature: Sara 💙
💚 Topic 23.3 – Staying Healthy

In this composition, you write a letter to a friend sharing fun, useful tips on how to stay healthy. Include activities, foods, and hygiene habits.

🏃

Play Outside

Running, cycling, swimming, and playing games keep your body strong and your mind happy.

🥗

Eat Healthy Foods

Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and dairy give your body the energy and nutrients it needs.

🧼

Wash Your Hands

Washing hands before eating and after using the toilet keeps germs away and stops you from getting sick.

Stay Happy

Spend time with family and friends, laugh, read books, and do things you enjoy.

🦸 Topic 23.4 – Community Helpers

Write about your favourite community helper — someone who works hard to make your community safe, healthy, and happy. Describe what they do, why you admire them, and give an example of them helping someone.

Helper 1

👮 Police Officer

Keeps people safe, helps in emergencies, directs traffic, and maintains law and order in the community.

Helper 2

🚒 Firefighter

Puts out dangerous fires, rescues people trapped in buildings, and teaches fire safety.

Helper 3

👨‍⚕️ Doctor / Nurse

Treats sick and injured people, gives medicines, and checks children's health at school.

Helper 4

👩‍🏫 Teacher

Educates children, helps them learn new skills, and prepares them for the future.

🌟 How to Write About a Community Helper

✨ Example: My Favourite Community Helper — The Doctor

My favourite community helper is the doctor. Doctors work very hard every day to keep people healthy and safe. They examine patients, give medicines, and help people recover from illnesses and injuries.

I admire doctors because they are always kind, calm and caring — even in very difficult situations. Once, when my little sister was very ill with a high fever, our family doctor came to see her late at night. She was so gentle and patient. She examined my sister carefully, gave her the right medicine, and told us what to do. By morning, my sister was already feeling better!

I think doctors are true heroes. They save lives every single day. When I grow up, I would love to become a doctor and help people in my community, just like she helped my sister.

Topic 23.6

🎉 Festivals Bring Joy & Unity

Reflect on what Pakistani festivals mean to your community:

  • 🤝 People come together and forget differences
  • 🏡 Neighbours share food and greetings
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Families reunite from far away
  • 🎊 Streets and homes are decorated
  • 🥁 Traditional music and cultural celebrations
  • 💖 Acts of generosity and giving to the poor
  • 🌟 A sense of shared identity and pride

📚 Key Festivals in Pakistan

🌙 Eid-ul-Fitr — The Festival of Breaking Fast ▼

Eid-ul-Fitr is celebrated at the end of Ramadan, the holy month of fasting. Families wake up early, dress in new clothes, and go to the mosque for special Eid prayers. Afterwards, everyone visits relatives and friends, exchanging sweets and gifts. Children receive Eidi — money given as a gift. Special foods like sheer khurma (sweet vermicelli pudding) and sewaiyan are prepared. The whole community gathers to celebrate with joy and gratitude.

🐑 Eid-ul-Adha — The Festival of Sacrifice ▼

Eid-ul-Adha commemorates the willingness of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) to sacrifice his son as an act of obedience to Allah. Families who can afford it perform Qurbani — the sacrifice of an animal such as a goat, cow, or camel. The meat is divided into three parts: one for family, one for relatives and friends, and one for those in need. This act teaches generosity and sharing. Eid prayers are offered in congregation and the whole community comes together to celebrate.

🎊 Independence Day — 14th August ▼

Pakistan's Independence Day is celebrated on 14th August every year. On this day in 1947, Pakistan became an independent country. Schools and public places are decorated with green and white flags. Children wear traditional clothes and paint their faces with the national flag's colours. Parades, cultural shows, and fireworks take place all over the country. It is a day of national pride and unity.

📚 Topics 23.7 & 23.8 – Creative Story Writing
Topic 23.7

🤝 The Kindness Chain

Hassan's act of generosity in "The Generous Boy and the Hungry Stranger" inspires his whole village. Imagine how one small act leads to a chain reaction of kindness.


Story ideas to include:

  • Hassan shares his food with a hungry stranger
  • The baker sees this and gives free bread to poor children
  • A farmer plants extra crops to donate to neighbours
  • Children help an old woman carry her shopping
  • The whole village becomes a happier, kinder place
Topic 23.8

🐦 The Little Bird's Adventure

A little bird is released back into the garden by the kind gardener. Now it must explore the world, meet new friends, and help others — just as it was helped.


Story ideas to include:

  • The bird flies across gardens and forests
  • It meets a lonely rabbit and sings to cheer it up
  • It warns animals about a coming storm
  • It befriends a wise old owl who teaches it about kindness
  • The bird learns that helping others makes you happy too

🏗️ How to Structure a Story

🌅

Beginning

Introduce your character, where they are, and what life is like. Set the scene!

⚡

Middle

Something happens — a problem, a challenge, an adventure. This is the exciting part!

🌈

End

The problem is solved. Your character learns something. The story ends happily.

💡 Top Writing Tips
Tip 1

🧠 Plan Before You Write

Before you start writing, spend a few minutes thinking about your topic. Write down a few key words or ideas. This makes your writing better organised and easier to read.

Tip 2

🎨 Use Describing Words (Adjectives)

Instead of "The fish was big," write "The enormous, silver fish glittered in the deep blue water." Adjectives make your writing come alive! Use colours, sizes, textures and feelings.

Tip 3

📐 Write in Paragraphs

Each paragraph should have one main idea. Start a new paragraph when you talk about something different. Use a topic sentence at the beginning of each paragraph.

Tip 4

✅ Check Your Work

Always re-read your writing when you finish. Check for spelling mistakes, missing capital letters, and incorrect punctuation. Reading your work aloud helps you find errors!

Tip 5

💬 Use Connectives

Connect your sentences and ideas using words like: because, however, therefore, firstly, then, finally, in addition, as a result. These make your writing flow smoothly.

📚 Key Vocabulary

These are important words for your composition writing. Click on any card to see an example sentence!

✏️ Composition
A piece of writing where you express ideas in your own words.
The teacher asked us to write a composition about our summer holidays.
📖 Descriptive
Writing that uses details to paint a picture with words.
Her descriptive writing made me feel like I was really at the beach!
📝 Paragraph
A group of sentences about one main idea, starting on a new line.
Each paragraph in your essay should have one clear main idea.
🎨 Adjective
A describing word that tells you more about a noun.
The enormous, silver fish swam in the crystal-clear water.
🔗 Connective
A word that joins sentences or ideas together.
I love swimming because the water is refreshing and cool.
✉️ Salutation
The greeting at the beginning of a letter, e.g. "Dear Ali,"
Always begin your friendly letter with a warm salutation.
🏗️ Structure
The organised way a piece of writing is arranged.
A good story has a clear structure: beginning, middle and end.
🌟 Generosity
The quality of being kind, giving, and willing to share.
Hassan's generosity inspired everyone in his village to help others.
🤝 Community
A group of people who live and work together in the same place.
The doctor plays an important role in keeping our community healthy.
🌙 Commemorate
To honour and remember an important event or person.
Eid-ul-Adha commemorates the faith of Prophet Ibrahim (AS).
💧 Hydrated
Having enough water in your body to stay healthy.
Drinking 8 glasses of water a day helps you stay hydrated.
🐬 Intelligent
Very clever and able to learn things quickly.
Dolphins are intelligent animals that can solve puzzles.
