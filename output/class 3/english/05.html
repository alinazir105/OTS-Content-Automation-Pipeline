Prepositions 🗺️

Learn how words like in, on, at, over, under, to connect nouns and pronouns to tell us where, when, and which direction things are!

💡 What is a Preposition?

A preposition is a word that connects a noun or pronoun to other words in a sentence. Prepositions help us know the place, time, and direction of things in a sentence.

📍

Place

Where something is
"The cat is under the table."

🕐

Time

When something happens
"I sleep at 9 o'clock."

🧭

Direction

Where something is going
"She walked to the market."

📍 Prepositions of Place

These prepositions tell us where something is located.

📚The book is on the table.
🐱The cat is hiding under the table.
🎈The balloon is above my head.
🚗The car is parked in front of the house.
🐦The birds are flying over the lake.

Key Place Prepositions

on under above over in front of behind next to between below opposite near in at

💡 Tip: Ask yourself "Where?" to find the preposition of place in a sentence.

🕐 Prepositions of Time

These prepositions tell us when something happens.

🍳I have breakfast before school.
🛝We go to the playground after lunch.
🎂My birthday is in October.
😴I sleep at 9 o'clock.
🙏We pray during the evening.

Key Time Prepositions

in on at before after during between until since for

💡 Tip: Ask yourself "When?" to find the preposition of time in a sentence.

🧭 Prepositions of Direction

These prepositions tell us where something is going.

🚶She walked to the market.
🪁The kite flew towards the sky.
🏃He ran into the classroom.
⚽The ball rolled across the floor.
🚗We drove from Karachi to Lahore.

Key Direction Prepositions

to towards into across from through up down along onto

💡 Tip: Ask yourself "Where is it going?" to find the preposition of direction.

📊 In / On / At — Place & Time

These three prepositions are very commonly used for both place and time. Here is how to use them correctly:

IN

UsePlace ExampleTime Example
Enclosed spacesThe cat is in the box.—
Countries, cities, regionsShe lives in Karachi.—
Large areas (parks, neighbourhoods)There are ducks in the park.—
Months, years, decades—We moved here in 2020. The event is in March.
Parts of the day / seasons—He wakes up in the morning. It gets cold in winter.

ON

UsePlace ExampleTime Example
SurfacesThe pen is on the desk.—
Streets, roadsOur house is on Tariq road.—
Transportation (buses, trains)She is on the bus.—
Technology (TV, phone, internet)He is watching a show on TV.—
Specific days and dates—The meeting is on Wednesday.

AT

UsePlace ExampleTime Example
Specific points or locationsShe is waiting at the entrance.—
AddressesHe lives at lane 2, DHA.—
EventsThey are at a concert.—
Specific times—The class starts at 9:00 am.
Expressions (at night, at noon)—We eat lunch at noon.
