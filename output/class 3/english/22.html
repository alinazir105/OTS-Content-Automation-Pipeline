City Aquarium

Come and explore an underwater world at City Aquarium! There are hundreds of amazing sea creatures for you to see.

🦈 Sharks

Sharks have lived in our oceans for over 400 million years. Some sharks are big, some are very small and some are very scary! Don't miss the sharks' feeding time at 3 p.m. every day in our shark zone.

🐧 Penguins

Most penguins are from the southern hemisphere, and some of them live in one of the coldest places on Earth: Antarctica. Watch our playful penguins dive and swim in the icy waters of our penguin zone!

🐢 Turtles

Turtles have been on planet Earth for over 200 million years. But today some species of sea turtle are endangered, because of hunters, fishing nets or plastic in the ocean. Come and see these beautiful creatures in our turtle zone.

🐙 Incredible Sea Creatures

There are so many other incredible sea creatures to discover at the City Aquarium, including octopuses, jellyfish, seahorses and starfish. Which is your favourite?

Handwashing

Washing your hands is an essential habit that helps keep you healthy by removing germs. Always wash your hands after using the toilet, before eating, and after playing outside.

How to Wash Your Hands Properly

  1. Wet hands with clean water
  2. Apply soap
  3. Scrub all parts — including between fingers and under nails — for at least 20 seconds
  4. Turn off the tap to save water
  5. Dry hands thoroughly with a clean towel

Why Does It Matter?

Practising good hand hygiene not only protects you from getting sick but also helps prevent the spread of germs to others.

🌟 Fun Fact: Singing "Happy Birthday" twice takes about 20 seconds — the perfect time to scrub your hands!

Community Helpers

Our community has many helpers who work hard to keep us safe and healthy. Three important community helpers are police officers, firefighters, and doctors.

👮 Police Officers

Police officers keep our neighbourhoods safe. They wear a special uniform and a badge. They help prevent crime, solve problems, and make sure that everyone follows the law. Police officers also help people find lost things and guide them if they are lost. In an emergency, call 15.

🚒 Firefighters

Firefighters are brave people who help us during fires and other emergencies. They wear special suits and helmets to protect themselves from the heat. They use water hoses and ladders to put out fires and rescue people. Firefighters are always ready to help, no matter the time of day or night.

👨‍⚕️ Doctors

Doctors help us stay healthy. They work in clinics and hospitals to take care of sick people. Doctors check patients, give them medicine, and tell them how to feel better. Some doctors also give vaccinations to protect people from diseases.

Our community is a safer and better place because of these community helpers. We should thank them for their hard work and respect what they do to help us all.

The Little Bird and the Helpful Gardener

One sunny morning, a small bird was flying through a beautiful garden filled with colourful flowers. Suddenly, the little bird accidentally pricked its wing on a sharp thorn. The bird was hurt and could not fly well. Feeling sad and helpless, it chirped softly for help.

A kind gardener, who was busy watering the plants, heard the little bird's cries. He gently picked up the bird, took out the thorn, and softly wrapped its wing with a small cloth. The bird felt much better and chirped happily. The gardener smiled and released the bird back into the garden.

The little bird wanted to thank the gardener, so it decided to stay near him. Every day, while the gardener worked, the bird sang cheerful songs to keep him happy. Over time, they became friends and helped each other in small ways. The gardener cared for the bird, and the bird filled the garden with its beautiful songs.

🌱 Moral: Kindness and gratitude create lasting friendships.

The Generous Boy and the Hungry Stranger

In a small village, there lived a young boy named Hassan. One day, Hassan found an old man sitting by the side of the road. The man looked tired and hungry, with no food or money. Hassan felt sad for him because he knew how hard it was to go without food.

Hassan only had a small piece of bread for lunch, but he thought, "I can share my food." He walked up to the old man, broke his bread in half, and gave one piece to him. The old man's face lit up with joy. He smiled and said, "Thank you, young boy. Your kindness means a lot."

When the people in the village heard about Hassan's generosity, they admired him. Many of them brought food and treats to thank Hassan for his kindness. The boy learned that when we are kind to others, it often comes back to us in wonderful ways.

🌟 Moral: When we are kind to others, kindness comes back to us in wonderful ways.
