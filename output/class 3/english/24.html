📚 What is a Poem?

A poem is a special kind of writing that uses beautiful words to describe feelings, ideas, and things around us. Let's learn about the important parts of a poem!

🎵 What Makes a Poem Special?
A poem is different from a story. In a poem:

• Words often rhyme at the end of lines
• Lines are arranged in groups called stanzas
• Poets use describing words to paint pictures
• Poems have a special rhythm — like music!
📝 Parts of a Poem
Line — one row of words in a poem

Stanza — a group of lines (like a paragraph)

Rhyme — when words end with the same sound

Rhythm — the beat or music of the poem

🔍 Stanza Breakdown — "Great, Glorious, Golden Sun"

Look at how the poem is organized into stanzas (groups of lines):

STANZA 1

Line 1
Great, glorious, golden Sun,
Line 2
Shine down on me today!
Line 3
You are the life of all this Earth,
Line 4
You and your magic ray.

STANZA 2

Line 5
You are the life of bird and plant,
Line 6
All must depend on you.
Line 7
Shine down, great sun, the whole day long!
Line 8
Shine from the heaven's blue.

🔤 Rhyme Scheme (which lines rhyme):

A: today B: ray C: you D: blue

Lines 2 & 4 rhyme (today / ray), and Lines 6 & 8 rhyme (you / blue)!

💡
A group of 4 lines in a poem is called a quatrain! All three poems in this chapter use quatrains.
🎯
Alliteration is when many words in a line start with the same sound. "Great, Glorious, Golden Sun" uses alliteration with the letter G!
🌟
When a poet speaks to something that can't talk (like the Sun), it's called personification — treating the sun as if it were a person!

1
Great, Glorious, Golden Sun
By Lenore Hetrick
☀️
Great, glorious, golden Sun, Shine down on me today! You are the life of all this Earth, You and your magic ray. You are the life of bird and plant, All must depend on you. Shine down, great sun, the whole day long! Shine from the heaven's blue.

🌟 Theme: The poem celebrates the importance of the Sun for all life on Earth.

🔡 Alliteration: "Great, Glorious, Golden Sun" — three words starting with G!

🗣️ The poet is talking directly to the Sun — this is called direct address.

🌿 The Sun gives life to: birds, plants, and all of Earth.

✨ Magic ray means the sun's light, which helps plants grow and keeps us warm.

Let's find the rhyming words in this poem:

Stanza 1:

Line 2: today

Line 4: ray

today 🔗 ray — both end in "ay"!

Stanza 2:

Line 6: you

Line 8: blue

you 🔗 blue — both end in "oo"!

🤔 Think about it:

❓ Why does the poet call the Sun "great, glorious, golden"?

💭 Because the Sun is huge (great), beautiful (glorious), and bright yellow (golden)!

❓ What does the poet mean by "magic ray"?

💭 The sunlight is "magic" because it makes plants grow and gives energy to all living things!

❓ Name two things that depend on the Sun, according to this poem.

💭 Birds and plants depend on the Sun!

2
Soccer Dreams
By Kenn Nesbitt
⚽
I kick the ball; it flies so high, Through the air and past the sky. With a goal, the crowd will scream, Soccer is my favourite dream!

🌟 Theme: The poet loves soccer and dreams of scoring goals!

📦 Stanzas: This poem has just 1 stanza with 4 lines — a single quatrain.

⚡ "flies so high" — exaggeration to show how powerfully the ball is kicked.

📣 "the crowd will scream" — shows the excitement of the game!

💭 "favourite dream" — soccer is not just a game, it's the poet's biggest wish!

This poem has a couplet rhyme scheme — pairs of lines rhyme:

Lines 1 & 2:

Line 1: high

Line 2: sky

high 🔗 sky — both end in "igh/y"!

Lines 3 & 4:

Line 3: scream

Line 4: dream

scream 🔗 dream — both end in "eam"!

Rhyme pattern: AABB (1&2 rhyme, 3&4 rhyme)

🤔 Think about it:

❓ What does the speaker do with the ball?

💭 The speaker kicks the ball and it flies high through the air!

❓ What happens when a goal is scored?

💭 The crowd screams — they cheer loudly with excitement!

❓ What is the speaker's "favourite dream"?

💭 Soccer is the speaker's favourite dream!

3
Winter
By Dorothy Aldis
❄️
The street cars are Like frosted cakes— All covered up With cold snowflakes. The horses' hoofs Scrunch on the street; Their eyelashes Are white with sleet. And everywhere The people go— With faces TICKLED By the snow.

🌟 Theme: The poem describes a cold, snowy winter day in the city.

📦 Stanzas: This poem has 3 stanzas with 4 lines each.

🎂 "Like frosted cakes" — a simile! The snow-covered streetcars look like cakes with white frosting.

🐴 "Scrunch on the street" — we can almost hear the horses walking on snow. This is called onomatopoeia!

😄 "TICKLED by the snow" — the capital letters show the word is special. Snow tickles people's faces like tiny touches!

Find the rhyming pairs in each stanza:

Stanza 1:

Lines 2 & 4: cakes 🔗 snowflakes

Both end in "akes"!

Stanza 2:

Lines 2 & 4: street 🔗 sleet

Both end in "eet"!

Stanza 3:

Lines 2 & 4: go 🔗 snow

Both end in "oh"!

🤔 Think about it:

❓ What do the street cars look like in the snow?

💭 They look like frosted cakes — all covered with white snow!

❓ What is white with sleet? (Whose eyelashes?)

💭 The horses' eyelashes are white because ice has formed on them!

❓ Why does the poet write "TICKLED" in capital letters?

💭 To emphasize the funny, playful feeling when snowflakes touch your face!

📖 Vocabulary

Learn these important words from the three poems. Read the meaning and example sentence!

☀️ Glorious
Very beautiful and wonderful
"It was a glorious sunny day."
✨ Magic Ray
The special light that comes from the sun
"The magic ray of the sun helps plants grow."
🌿 Depend
To need something to survive or live
"Plants depend on sunlight to grow."
⚽ Favourite
The one you like best of all
"Soccer is my favourite sport."
🎂 Frosted
Covered with a white layer (like icing on a cake)
"The frosted cake was delicious."
❄️ Sleet
A mixture of rain and snow that falls as ice
"The sleet made the road slippery."
🐴 Scrunch
To make a crunching sound when walking
"The snow scrunched under our boots."
😄 Tickled
Touched lightly, causing a funny sensation
"The feather tickled my nose."
🌩️ Snowflakes
Small, soft pieces of snow that fall from the sky
"Beautiful snowflakes fell on my hand."
