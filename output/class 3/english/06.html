📖 What Are Imperatives?

Definition

We use imperatives to order, request, suggest, and advise. Imperatives use the base form of the verb — no subject needed!

🛑 Eat this.
⚠️ Be careful!
🚶 Always cross the road carefully.
📚 Be quiet!
👑

Order

Used to command someone to do something firmly.

"Clean your room now!"
🙏

Request

Used politely with please or kindly.

"Please pass me the book."
💡

Suggestion

Used to propose an idea, often with Let's.

"Let's go to the park."
💬

Advice

Used to recommend something helpful.

"Always cross the road carefully."
🔤 Types of Imperatives

✅ Positive Imperatives

Start directly with the base verb.

OrderEat this.
AdviceBe careful!
AdviceAlways cross the road carefully.
RequestPlease come here.

❌ Negative Imperatives

Start with Do not / Don't or Never.

NegativeDon't say that!
NegativeDo not open this window.
NegativeNever touch that plant.
RequestDon't do that, please.

🎨 Visual Examples
📢 Order

"Clean your room now!"

The verb Clean comes first. It's a direct command — no 'please'.

🙏 Request

"Please pass me the book."

Please makes it polite. We use it to ask nicely.

💡 Suggestion

"Let's go to the park."

Let's includes the speaker too — we're suggesting together!

🩺 Advice

"You should drink more water."

This advises someone for their own benefit. It's helpful, not commanding.

🚫 Negative

"Don't touch that plant!"

Don't or Never begin negative imperatives to stop an action.

⚠️ Warning

"Never open this window."

Never is stronger than Don't — used for serious warnings.
