📌 What Are Plurals?

Words that show more than one of something

Definition

Plurals are words that show there is more than one of something. We add -s or -es to most singular nouns to make them plural.

📚
Book
→
Books
more than one book
🐕
Dog
→
Dogs
more than one dog
📦
Box
→
Boxes
more than one box

✅ Regular Plurals

Adding -s to most nouns

We add "s" to most nouns to form the plural. Here are some simple examples:

SingularPluralExample Sentence
TreeTreesThere are many trees in the park.
ShipShipsFive ships sailed the ocean.
FlowerFlowersShe picked ten flowers.
CatCatsTwo cats sat on the mat.
🏆

I have four shelves, six books and ten trophies

Notice how all three words are plurals!

📏 Spelling Rules

How to form plurals for different word endings

Rule 1: Add -es

Nouns ending in s, ss, sh, ch, x, o → add -es

bus → buses dress → dresses box → boxes potato → potatoes
Rule 2: Vowel + y → add -s

Nouns ending in vowel + y → just add -s

key → keys toy → toys day → days boy → boys
Rule 3: Consonant + y → -ies

Nouns ending in consonant + y → change y to -ies

pony → ponies family → families fly → flies city → cities
Rule 4: -f / -fe → -ves

Nouns ending in -f or -fe → change to -ves

wife → wives shelf → shelves knife → knives leaf → leaves

⚡ Irregular Plurals

Special plurals that don't follow the normal rules

Some nouns do not follow the regular rules of adding -s or -es to make them plural. These nouns are called irregular nouns, and their plural forms are unique.

Singular→PluralExample
Man→MenTwo men walked in.
Woman→WomenThe women sang.
Child→ChildrenHappy children played.
Tooth→TeethBrush your teeth!
Foot→FeetCold feet!
Mouse→MiceThree mice ran.
Goose→GeeseWhite geese swam.
Sheep→SheepTen sheep grazed.
Deer→DeerFive deer ran past.
Person→PeopleMany people came.

⭐ Remember!

Some irregular nouns like sheep and deer have the same form in both singular and plural. You can say "one sheep" and "many sheep"!
