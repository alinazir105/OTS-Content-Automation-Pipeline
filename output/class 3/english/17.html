👋 Introduction

What is "should" and why do we use it?

What is "should"?

We use should to give opinions and advice. It tells someone what you think is the right or best thing to do. When we want to say something is not a good idea, we use shouldn't.

✅

Should = Good Advice

Use should when you think something is a good idea or the right thing to do.

You should drink water. 💧
❌

Shouldn't = Bad Idea

Use shouldn't when you think something is a bad idea or the wrong thing to do.

You shouldn't eat too much. 🍬

📖 Concept: Grammar Rules

How to form sentences with should and shouldn't

Positive & Negative Sentences

Subject Should / Shouldn't Base Form (Verb)
I / he / she / it / we / you / they should tell him. / be here.
I / he / she / it / we / you / they shouldn't do that. / eat sweets.
Subject + should / shouldn't + Base Form of Verb

Example Sentences

We should get a taxi. 🚕
You shouldn't do that. 🙅
I think he should leave now. 🚶
I don't think you shouldn't eat a lot of sweets. 🍬

Forming Questions

Should Subject Base Form
Should I / he / she / it / we / you / they leave?
Should I / he / she / it / we / you / they open it?
Should + Subject + Base Form?
Should I tell Sami? → Yes, you should. ✅
What should I do? 🤔

Using "I think..." with Should

We often use I think... and I don't think... with should:

I think he should leave now. 🚶
I don't think you should eat a lot of sweets. 🍬

👁️ Real-Life Examples

See "should" and "shouldn't" in everyday situations

❄️

Situation 1

It's cold outside.

You should wear a hat. 🧢
🍫

Situation 2

You want to eat more chocolate.

I shouldn't eat any more chocolate. I'll feel sick! 🤢
📺

Situation 3

Sitting too close to the TV.

You shouldn't sit so close to the TV. It's bad for your eyes! 👁️
💌

Situation 4

Someone was very kind to you.

You should write a thank you letter. ✉️
🎨

Situation 5

The paint is not completely dry.

You shouldn't touch that! The paint's not dry yet. 🚫
🏊

Situation 6

Children in the swimming pool.

I don't think they should be in the swimming pool. 🏊
