📘 The Concept

Understanding the difference between subject and object pronouns

Subject pronouns replace the doer of the action:

I   You   We   He   She   It   They

Object pronouns replace the receiver of the action:

me   you   us   him   her   it   them

💡 Quick Tip

Ask yourself: "Is this pronoun doing the action?" → Use a Subject Pronoun.
Ask yourself: "Is this pronoun receiving the action?" → Use an Object Pronoun.

✍️ Example Sentences

See how subject and object pronouns are used in real sentences

1
🎁
He gave her a gift.

"He" = subject (the giver) · "her" = object (the receiver)

2
🗺️
She showed him the way.

"She" = subject (the one showing) · "him" = object (who was shown)

3
🤝
They helped us.

"They" = subject (the helpers) · "us" = object (who got help)

4
🎉
We invited them to a party.

"We" = subject (the inviters) · "them" = object (who was invited)

🎓 Guided Practice

See how nouns are replaced with the correct pronouns

Part A: Replacing with Subject Pronouns

The noun in bold is replaced by a subject pronoun.

🔸 Sami has a dog.
➡️
He has a dog.
🔸 Sarah and Sami are here.
➡️
They are here.
🔸 Sofia speaks Russian.
➡️
She speaks Russian.
🔸 The pens are new.
➡️
They are new.
🔸 David and I like tennis.
➡️
We like tennis.
🔸 The house is very old.
➡️
It is very old.
🔸 My sister is late.
➡️
She is late.

Part B: Replacing with Object Pronouns

The noun or noun phrase in bold is replaced by an object pronoun.

🔸 I can see Ali and Sofia.
➡️
I can see them.
🔸 I know that man.
➡️
I know him.
🔸 I work with David.
➡️
I work with him.
🔸 Suzy loved the flowers.
➡️
Suzy loved them.
🔸 You can come with Sami and me.
➡️
You can come with us.
🔸 This book is for you, Sami, and you, Sofia.
➡️
This book is for you.
🔸 You can open the letter.
➡️
You can open it.
