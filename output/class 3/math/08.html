What is Time?

Understanding Time

Time is measured in hours, minutes, and seconds. Clocks help us keep track of time with their hour, minute, and second hands.

⏰

Hour Hand

The shortest hand. Moves slowly, completing one full rotation every 12 hours.

🕐

Minute Hand

The longest hand. Moves faster, completing one full rotation every hour (60 minutes).

⚡

Second Hand

The thinnest hand. Moves the fastest, completing one full rotation every minute (60 seconds).

⏰ Hours to Minutes

To convert hours to minutes: Multiply by 60

1 HOUR = 60 MINUTES
4 HOURS = 4 × 60 = 240 MINUTES

💡 Remember

To convert hours → minutes: multiply by 60
To convert minutes → hours: divide by 60

🕐 Minutes to Hours

To convert minutes to hours: Divide by 60

60 MINUTES = 1 HOUR
180 MINUTES = 180 ÷ 60 = 3 HOURS

Step-by-step: Convert 180 minutes to hours

  1. 60 MINUTES = 1 HOUR
  2. 1 MINUTE = 1/60 HOURS
  3. 180 MINUTES = 180 × (1/60) HOURS
  4. = 180/60 HOURS
  5. = 3 HOURS ✅
