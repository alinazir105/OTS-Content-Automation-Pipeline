💡 Key Concepts

Understand what subtraction is and how borrowing works

1️⃣

1-Digit Subtraction

Subtract small numbers — no borrowing needed! Simply take away ones from ones.

Example: 7 − 3 = 4 ones
2️⃣

2-Digit Subtraction

Sometimes we need to borrow from the tens column when ones aren't enough.

Example: 56 − 29 = 27
3️⃣

3-Digit Subtraction

Borrow from tens AND hundreds when needed. Always work right to left.

Example: 634 − 275 = 359
🔑 Key Rule: Always subtract ones first, then tens, then hundreds. If a digit is too small, borrow from the next column!

📝 Word Problems

Apply subtraction to real-life situations

🍬

Q1: Sara's Candies

Sara has 64 candies. She gives 29 candies to her friends. How many candies does Sara have left?

🐦

Q2: Birds on a Tree

There are 58 birds on a tree. After some time, 32 birds fly away. How many birds are left on the tree?

👕

Q3: Shirts in a Store

A store has 76 shirts. They sell 48 shirts. How many shirts are left in the store?

🧸

Q4: Factory Toys

A factory makes 472 toys in a day. They sell 159 toys by the end of the day. How many toys are left?

🏫

Q5: Students in School

There are 685 students in a school. After summer break, 243 students move to a different school. How many students are left?

📚

Q6: Library Books

A library has 528 books. 134 books are donated to another library. How many books are left?
