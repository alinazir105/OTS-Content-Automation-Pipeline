What is Length?

Understanding the measure of how long or wide something is

📐

Definition

Length is the measure of how long or wide something is. We measure it from one end to the other.

🔍

How to Measure

Place one end of the object at 0 on the ruler. Read the number where the other end falls.

📏

Example: Eraser

The eraser is 5 cm from one end to the other. So the length of the eraser is 5 cm.

📎

Non-Standard Units

We can also measure using paperclips! Count how many paperclips fit along the object.

💡

Did you know? Before rulers were invented, people used body parts like feet and hands to measure! That's why we still say "foot" as a unit of measurement.

Units of Length

The four most common units we use to measure length

🌍

Kilometres (km)

Used to measure very long distances — like the distance between cities.

Biggest Unit
🏫

Metres (m)

The standard unit of length. Used to measure rooms, height of people, etc.

Standard Unit
📏

Centimetres (cm)

Used to measure smaller objects like pencils, erasers, and books.

Common Unit
🔬

Millimetres (mm)

The smallest unit. Used for very tiny measurements like the thickness of a coin.

Smallest Unit
Unit Symbol Size Used For
Kilometre km Biggest Distance between cities, countries

Conversions

Converting between metres and centimetres

➡️ Metres + cm → Centimetres

To convert metres to centimetres, multiply metres by 100, then add the centimetres.

cm = (metres × 100) + cm
✅ 1 m = 100 cm
✅ 2 m = 2 × 100 = 200 cm
✅ 2 m 12 cm = 200 + 12 = 212 cm

⬅️ Centimetres → Metres

To convert centimetres to metres, divide by 100.

m = centimetres ÷ 100
✅ 375 cm ÷ 100 = 3.75 m
✅ 3508 cm ÷ 100 = 35 m 8 cm
✅ 4000 cm ÷ 100 = 40 m

Word Problems

Apply your measurement skills to real-world problems

Q1: Ribbons Together

Sarah has a ribbon that is 12 cm long. She gets another ribbon that is 15 cm long. How long are the ribbons together?

Q2: Piece of Wood

A piece of wood is 1 metre long. If 30 cm is cut off, how much piece of wood is left?

Q3: Two Pieces of Rope

Noor has two pieces of rope. One is 2 metres long and the other is 50 cm long. How long are the ropes put end to end?

Q4: Race Track

A runner completes a race track that is 100 metres long. If she stops 25 metres before the finish line, how much has she run?

Q5: Two Shelves

Asma buys two shelves for her room; one is 85 cm long and the other is 95 cm long. What is the total length of both shelves combined?
