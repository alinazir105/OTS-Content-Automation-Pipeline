📖 Introduction to Division

Division is the inverse of multiplication — it splits a larger number into equal smaller parts

🔄 What is Division?

  • Division is the inverse of multiplication
  • It means splitting into equal parts or separating items into groups
  • We break down a larger number into smaller numbers
  • Division helps us share things equally and fairly
  • It is one of the four basic arithmetic operations

📥 Dividend

The number being divided. It's the total amount you start with. In 105 ÷ 8, the dividend is 105.

📤 Divisor

The number you divide by. It tells how many equal groups to make. In 105 ÷ 8, the divisor is 8.

✅ Quotient

The result of division — the answer. In 105 ÷ 8, the quotient is 13.

🔁 Remainder

What's left over after equal division. In 105 ÷ 8, the remainder is 1.

🔑 The Division Formula

Always verify your answer using this formula:

Dividend = Divisor × Quotient + Remainder
105 = 8 × 13 + 1 ✓

📋 How to Divide — Step by Step

Example: 413 ÷ 7

1

Divide the first digit(s)

Ask: how many times does 7 go into 41?

41 ÷ 7 → try 7 × 5 = 35
2

Subtract and Compare

Make sure the remainder is less than the divisor

41 − 35 = 6 (less than 7 ✓)
3

Bring Down the Next Number

Bring down the next digit and divide again

63 ÷ 7 → try 7 × 9 = 63
4

Subtract and Compare Again

Check that the final remainder is less than divisor

63 − 63 = 0 (no remainder!)
