Introduction to Numbers

Understanding how numbers are built using digits

Numbers are everywhere! We use them to count, measure, and describe quantities. In this chapter, we will learn how numbers are structured using place values — the position of a digit that tells us its value. Every digit in a number has a special place: Ones, Tens, Hundreds, Thousands, and Ten Thousands.

1️⃣

Ones (O)

The rightmost place. It tells how many single units are in the number. Example: In 47, the digit 7 is in the Ones place = 7.

🔟

Tens (T)

The second place from the right. Example: In 47, the digit 4 is in the Tens place = 40.

💯

Hundreds (H)

The third place from the right. Example: In 345, the digit 3 is in the Hundreds place = 300.

💎

Thousands (Th)

The fourth place from the right. Example: In 2,345, the digit 2 is in the Thousands place = 2000.

Expanded Form

Breaking a number into its place value parts

Expanded form shows each digit's value separately. We add up all the parts to make the whole number.

Examples

8,425 = 8,000 + 400 + 20 + 5
9,530 = 9,000 + 500 + 30 + 0
7,321 = 7,000 + 300 + 20 + 1
12,304 = 10,000 + 2,000 + 300 + 0 + 4
45,009 = 40,000 + 5,000 + 0 + 0 + 9

Predecessor & Successor

Numbers that come just before and after

⬅️ Predecessor

The number which comes just before another number is called the predecessor of that number.

89 is the predecessor of 90
999 is the predecessor of 1,000

➡️ Successor

The number which comes just after another number is called the successor of that number.

500 is the successor of 499
1,000 is the successor of 999

Knowledge Quiz

8 Questions — Test your understanding!

Question 1 of 8
In the number 679, what is the digit in the Tens place?
In 679: 6 is in Hundreds, 7 is in Tens, 9 is in Ones. The Tens position is the second digit from the right.
Question 2 of 8
What number is formed by: 4 hundreds, 4 tens and 3 ones?
4 hundreds = 400, 4 tens = 40, 3 ones = 3. So 400 + 40 + 3 = 443.
Question 3 of 8
True or False: The largest 3-digit number is 999.
The largest 3-digit number is indeed 999. The next number, 1000, is the smallest 4-digit number. ✅ True!
Question 4 of 8
What is the expanded form of 8,425?
8,425 = 8000 + 400 + 20 + 5. Each digit is written as its place value: 8 thousands, 4 hundreds, 2 tens, 5 ones.
Question 5 of 8
What is the predecessor of 30?
The predecessor comes just before a number. The number before 30 is 29. (30 - 1 = 29)
Question 6 of 8
In the number 37,159, what is the place value of the digit 7?
In 37,159: 3 is in Ten Thousands (30,000), 7 is in Thousands = 7,000, 1 is in Hundreds, 5 in Tens, 9 in Ones.
Question 7 of 8
True or False: The successor of 9,999 is 10,000.
The successor comes just after a number. 9,999 + 1 = 10,000. This is also the smallest 5-digit number! ✅ True!
Question 8 of 8
Which number is written correctly as "Six thousand and ten"?
Six thousand = 6,000. And ten = 10. So "Six thousand and ten" = 6,000 + 10 = 6,010.
