Multiplication Concept

🌟

What is Multiplication?

Multiplication is repeated addition. When you multiply, you are adding the same number again and again!

Example: 3 × 4 means adding 3 four times: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12

4 × 2 = 8. Write 8 under O.

2

✅ Thus, 24 × 2 = 48

📐 Example 3: 3-Digit Multiplication (246 × 2)

HTO
2 4 6
× 2
4 9 2
1

Multiply the ones: 6 × 2 = 12 = 1 ten + 2 ones.
Write 2 in ones column, carry over 1 to tens.

2

Multiply the tens: 4 × 2 = 8 tens.
8 + 1 (carry over) = 9 tens.

3

Multiply the hundreds: 2 × 2 = 4 hundreds.

✅ Final Answer: 246 × 2 = 492
