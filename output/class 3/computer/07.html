🖼️ Inserting Pictures in a Document

📁 From Your Computer

Follow these steps to insert a picture saved on your computer:

  1. Click on 'Pictures' then select 'This Device' option.
  2. A window will open, allowing you to browse your files.
  3. Navigate to the folder where your picture is saved.
  4. Click on the picture file you want to insert.
  5. Click 'Insert' or double-click the picture file to place it in the document.

🌐 From Online

Follow these steps to insert an image from the internet:

  1. Click on 'Pictures' then select 'Online Pictures'.
  2. A search box will appear — type a keyword (e.g., "computer").
  3. Press 'Enter' or click the search icon.
  4. A selection of images will appear — scroll and choose the one you like.
  5. Click on the image you want to insert.
  6. Click 'Insert' to place the image into your document.

📦 Inserting a Text Box in MS Word

Steps to Insert a Simple Text Box

  1. Open MS Word.
  2. Place the cursor where you want to insert the text box.
  3. Click on the Insert tab in the ribbon at the top.
  4. Click on Text Box in the Text group.
  5. A drop-down menu appears with different text box styles. Choose Simple Text Box.
  6. The text box appears — you can now type inside it or move it around!

🎨 Shape Format: Fill & Outline

🎨 Change Fill Colour of a Text Box

  1. Click on "Insert" and choose "Text Box" to add a text box.
  2. Click on the text box to select it.
  3. Go to the "Shape Format" tab at the top.
  4. Find and click "Shape Fill" option.
  5. Choose the colour you want from the colour menu.
  6. Remember to save your work after selecting the colour!
Shape Fill Colour Menu:

✏️ Change Outline Colour of a Text Box

  1. Select the text box by clicking on it.
  2. Go to the "Shape Format" tab at the top.
  3. Click on "Shape Outline" option in the toolbar.
  4. A colour menu will appear — choose your preferred colour.
  5. Your text box outline will change to the selected colour.
  6. Remember to save your work!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts in MS Word

Essential Keyboard Shortcuts

Function Shortcut Key Description
Copy Ctrl + C Copies selected text or object
Paste Ctrl + V Pastes copied or cut content
Cut Ctrl + X Cuts (removes) selected text or object
Undo Ctrl + Z Undoes the last action
Bold Ctrl + B Makes selected text bold
Save Ctrl + S Saves the current document
Select All Ctrl + A Selects all text in the document
Home Key Home Moves cursor to start of a line/page
End Key End Moves cursor to end of a line/page
Shift + Arrow Shift + → Selects text character by character

⌨️

Shift Key for Capitals

Hold the Shift key while typing a letter to write it in CAPITAL. For example: Shift + a = A. You can also hold Caps Lock to type all capitals!

🖱️

Arrow Keys for Selection

Hold Shift and press the arrow keys to select text. This works great when combined with Ctrl+C to copy selected text!

🔬 Lab Activities

💻 Activity 1: Changing Text Appearance

1

Open MS Word and type a sentence about your favourite activity.

2

Change the font size, colour, and style of the text.

3

Use the Delete key to remove any mistakes.

4

Try using the Shift key to write part of the sentence in uppercase.

🐘 Activity 2: My Favourite Animal

1

Open MS Word and type a paragraph about your favourite animal.

2

Change the font colour of the first sentence to make it stand out.

3

Bold any adjectives (e.g., "big," "furry," "fast") used in your description.

4

Underline the name of the animal each time you mention it.

5

Center-align the title "My Favourite Animal" at the top.

6

Go to the Insert tab, click on Pictures, and select This Device or Online Pictures to add a picture of your favourite animal.

🌳 Activity 3: A Day at the Park

1

Type a paragraph about a day you spent at the park.

2

Highlight and italicize any verbs (action words) in your paragraph.

3

Change the font style for the entire paragraph to something fun (like Comic Sans).

4

Add a bullet point list of three things you enjoyed doing at the park at the end.

5

Go to the Insert tab, click on Pictures, choose This Device or Online Pictures, and insert a park-related image.

🏫 Activity 4: My Dream School

1

Type a paragraph describing your dream school.

2

Add a title "My Dream School" and change its font size to make it stand out.

3

Use different colours for the names of subjects or activities you want at your dream school.

4

Underline the most exciting part of your dream school.

5

Go to the Insert tab, select Pictures, click This Device or Online Pictures, and search for a school-related image to insert.
