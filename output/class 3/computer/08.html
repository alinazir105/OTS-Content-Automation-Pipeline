💡 What is MS PowerPoint?

MS PowerPoint is a software that helps us create slideshows. We can use it to make presentations for school projects, stories, or any other information we want to share. 🎉

💡 Did you know? PowerPoint saves files with the extension .pptx and you can start a slideshow by pressing F5 on your keyboard!

➕ Inserting a New Slide

To add a new slide in PowerPoint, follow these steps:

  1. Open PowerPoint on your computer.
  2. Click on "Blank Presentation" to start a new project.
  3. Click on the "New Slide" button in the Home tab.
  4. A menu will appear — choose slide types like Title, Title and Content, or Blank.

🖼️ Inserting Pictures in Slides

We can add pictures to our slides to make them more interesting. There are two ways to do this:

Method 1: Using the Insert Tab

  1. Open PowerPoint and start a new presentation.
  2. Click on the "Insert" tab in the ribbon at the top.
  3. Click on "Pictures" and select "This Device" to choose a picture saved on your computer.
  4. Browse your files, select the picture, and click "Insert."
💡 Tip: You can also choose Stock Images or Online Pictures from the same menu!

Method 2: Using the Picture Icon on a Slide

  1. When creating a new slide, choose a layout that has a picture icon in the center.
  2. Click the picture icon on the slide (it looks like a small mountain image).
  3. Select a picture from your computer or online and insert it.
💡 Tip: Layouts with picture icons make it easy to add images in the right spot automatically!
↔️ Resizing a Picture

How to Resize a Picture

  1. Click on the picture you want to resize to select it.
  2. Click and drag the small circles (dots) around the picture to make it bigger or smaller.
  3. Release the mouse button to set the new size.

🔀 Moving Between Slides

How to Switch Slides

To switch between slides, click on the slide thumbnails on the left side of the screen. This helps you easily move from one slide to another while editing your presentation.

🔑 Keyboard Shortcuts:
• Page Down → Next slide
• Page Up → Previous slide
• F5 → Start slideshow from beginning

✨ Adding Transitions Between Slides

Transitions are animated effects that play when you move from one slide to the next. They make your presentation look more exciting!

🌊

Morph

Smoothly animates changes between two slides with similar layout elements.

⬅️

Push

Moves the old slide out as the new slide pushes in from a direction.

🔲

Wipe

The new slide wipes over the old one from a specified direction.

🌫️

Fade

The current slide fades out and the next slide fades in smoothly.

⚡

Split

The slide splits open from the center or closes from the edges.

🎲

Random

PowerPoint picks a random transition effect for each slide automatically.

How to Add Transitions

  1. Open your presentation and navigate to the slide where you want to add a transition.
  2. Click on the "Transitions" tab in the ribbon. A variety of transition effects will appear.
  3. Choose a transition by clicking on the desired effect. You can see a preview on your slide.
  4. Customize the transition using:
    • Effect Options — change the direction of the transition
    • Duration — adjust how long the transition takes
    • Sound — add a sound effect during transition
    • Apply To All — apply to every slide at once

🔬 Lab Activities
Lab Activity 1

🖥️ Create a Simple Presentation

Objective: Learn how to create a new presentation with 3 slides.

  1. Open PowerPoint and start a new presentation.
  2. Create three slides:
    • Slide 1: Title slide with your name.
    • Slide 2: Insert a picture of your favourite animal.
    • Slide 3: Add text explaining why you like this animal.
  3. Save your presentation as "My Presentation."
Lab Activity 2

🖼️ Inserting and Resizing Pictures

Objective: Practise inserting and resizing images.

  1. Open your saved presentation from Activity 1.
  2. On Slide 2, add another picture of an animal.
  3. Resize the picture to make it smaller than the first one.
  4. Save your work.
Lab Activity 3

🎨 Creating a Themed Presentation

Objective: Make a short presentation on a topic of interest.

  1. Open PowerPoint and start a new presentation.
  2. Choose a theme (e.g., "All About Cats or My Pet," "My Favourite Hobby," or "The Solar System").
  3. Create 4 slides, each with a different layout and content related to your theme.
  4. Insert at least two pictures and one piece of text in each slide.
  5. Practise switching between slides to review your presentation.
  6. Add Transitions between your slides.
