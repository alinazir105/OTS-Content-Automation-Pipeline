📦 1.1 Types of Materials

Materials are substances used to make objects. Some are natural, like wood and cotton, while others are man-made, like plastic and metal. Understanding different types helps us choose the right materials for different purposes.

1. Natural Materials

These are materials that come from nature. They are found in the earth, plants, or animals. People do not make them.

  • 🌳 Wood — from trees
  • 🌸 Cotton — from plants
  • 🐑 Wool — from sheep
  • 🪨 Rocks — from earth
  • 💧 Water — from nature
  • 🌱 Rubber — from rubber trees
✅ Natural
🏭

2. Man-Made Materials

These are materials made by people using natural materials or chemicals. They are changed or created in factories.

  • 🧴 Plastic — made in factories
  • 🪟 Glass — from sand + heat
  • 📄 Paper — from wood pulp
  • 🧱 Bricks — from clay + fire
  • 🔩 Steel/Metal — from ore
🏭 Man-Made

⚙️ 1.2 Properties of Materials

Different materials have different properties. A property tells us what a material is like — how it feels, behaves, or what it can do.

🪨

Hard or Soft

Some materials are hard, like metal, while others are soft, like cotton.

🔩 Metal = Hard 🧶 Cotton = Soft
💪

Strong or Weak

Some materials can hold heavy things without breaking, like metal, while others cannot.

🔩 Metal = Strong 📄 Paper = Weak
🌀

Flexible or Rigid

Some materials bend easily, like rubber, while others do not, like glass.

🌱 Rubber = Flexible 🪟 Glass = Rigid

🌍 1.3 Uses of Materials in Daily Life

We use different materials to make everything we see around us — clothes, shoes, toys, books, furniture, and more. Understanding the different types of materials helps us choose the right ones for different purposes.

👗 Clothes & Fashion

Cotton (natural) is soft and comfortable — perfect for making clothes, towels, and bedsheets.

🚗 Transport & Tools

Metal is strong and durable — used for cars, tools, bridges, and appliances that need to withstand force.

🪟 Windows & Jars

Glass is transparent and rigid — ideal for windows, mirrors, jars, and drinking glasses.

🧴 Bottles & Toys

Plastic is lightweight and waterproof — great for bottles, bags, toys, and food containers.

🛋️ Furniture & Buildings

Wood is strong and easy to shape — used for furniture, doors, floors, and paper.

🚗 Tyres & Erasers

Rubber is flexible and bouncy — used for car tyres, pencil erasers, and rubber bands.
