2.1 Heating and Cooling

🔥

Heating

Heating a material means making it warmer. When materials are heated, they can change their shape or even become a different kind of material.

Makes materials softer or liquid
🔨

Metal Rods

When a metal rod is heated, it can bend or become softer. This is why blacksmiths heat metal to shape it into tools or other objects.

Blacksmith example
🕯️

Wax (Heating)

Wax is soft when it's warm. When you light a candle, the heat makes the wax melt into a liquid.

Candle wax melting
❄️

Cooling

Cooling a material means making it colder. Cooling can make some materials become hard or change their state.

Makes materials harder or solid
🕯️

Wax (Cooling)

After a candle is blown out, the melted wax cools and hardens again into a solid.

Wax solidifying
🧊

Water

When water is cooled, like in a freezer, it turns into ice. This is an example of cooling changing a liquid to a solid.

Water → Ice
🤸

2.2 Stretching and Bending

↔️

Stretching

Stretching means pulling on a material to make it longer or wider. Some materials can stretch without breaking.

Pulling → longer or wider
🔴

Rubber Bands

Rubber bands can stretch and then go back to their original shape when you let go. This is because rubber is a flexible material.

Elastic → returns to shape
👖

Elastic in Clothes

The elastic in clothes stretches so it can fit us comfortably. This is why waistbands can adjust to different sizes.

Clothing waistbands
↩️

Bending

Bending means changing the shape of a material by pressing or pushing it. Some materials bend easily, while others cannot.

Pressing → changes shape
🏺

Clay

Clay can be bent and shaped into different forms, like a pot, cup, or sculpture. It's easy to mold because it's soft and flexible.

Pots, cups, sculptures
🧴

Plastic

Some plastics can bend a little but return to their original shape. Others will stay in the shape you bend them into.

Two types of plastic behavior
💡

2.3 Examples of Changing Shape

🧊 1. Melting

Melting happens when something solid turns into a liquid because it's heated.


Example: Ice melts into water when it's left in a warm place. We see this in summer when ice cubes melt in a drink.

❄️ 2. Freezing

Freezing is when a liquid becomes solid because it's cooled down.


Example: Water turns into ice when placed in a freezer. Ice cubes stay in one shape until they melt.

♨️ 3. Boiling

Boiling happens when a liquid is heated to a very high temperature and turns into a gas (steam).


Example: When we boil water in a kettle, it gets so hot that it bubbles and releases steam. Steam is water in gas form.

Why is Changing Shape Important?

Understanding how materials change shape helps us in our daily lives. It shows us how to use different materials in different ways!

🛠️

How to use different materials in different ways — rubber for stretchy things, metal for strong objects.

🌡️

How things around us react to temperature changes — ice melts in summer, water freezes in winter.

🏗️

Helps scientists and engineers design tools, buildings, and items we use every day.

🍳

Useful in cooking utensils, toys, and clothes — all designed using knowledge of materials.
