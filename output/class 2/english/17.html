🕐 Prepositions of Time

We use on, at, and in to talk about times and dates.

🗓️ ON + days / dates ⏰ AT + exact times 📅 IN + periods of time
on Monday at 2:00 in 2030
on my birthday at lunch time in the Spring / Summer / Autumn / Winter
on New Year's Day at half past four in July / March / October
on the twelfth of April at break time in the morning / afternoon / evening
📌 Remember:
✅ on Thursday morning / afternoon / evening
✅ in the morning, afternoon, evening
✅ at night
✅ at the weekend
