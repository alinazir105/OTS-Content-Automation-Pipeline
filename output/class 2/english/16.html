💡 What Are They?

What is an Irregular Verb?

Most verbs add "-ed" to show the past. But some verbs are special — they change their whole form! These are called Irregular Verbs.

go → went

We don't say "goed". We say went!

eat → ate

We don't say "eated". We say ate!

see → saw

We don't say "seed". We say saw!

write → wrote

We don't say "writed". We say wrote!

drink → drank

We don't say "drinked". We say drank!

fly → flew

We don't say "flyed". We say flew!
