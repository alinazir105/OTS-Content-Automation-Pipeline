Past Tense of "Be"

Learning Was and Were 🌟

Class 2 · English · Lesson 10
📖

What Are Was & Were?

The verb "be" in the past simple has two forms: was and were. We use them to talk about states — the existence or condition of something or somebody in the past.

💡

Know This! We often use the past simple with certain time expressions:

yesterday yesterday morning yesterday afternoon yesterday evening last night last week last month last year on Monday on Tuesday this morning this afternoon this evening
🧑
WAS

I, he, she, it, Ali

singular subjects

👨‍👩‍👧
WERE

We, you, they

plural / you subjects

📝

The Forms

✅ Affirmative (Positive)

💡

Examples

Affirmative ✅

  • Neil Armstrong was the first man on the moon. 🌙
  • It was cold this morning! 🥶
  • She was at home all day today.
  • They were very tired last night.
  • My parents were at the supermarket this morning.

Negative ❌

  • I wasn't at school last week.
  • Sami wasn't happy yesterday.
  • My friends weren't in the park this afternoon.

Questions ❓

  • Was he ill? — Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't.
  • Were they hungry? — Yes, they were. / No, they weren't.
  • Was it sunny last Saturday? — Yes, it was.
