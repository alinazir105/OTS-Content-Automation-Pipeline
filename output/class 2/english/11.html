📗 Class 2 — English Grammar

Use of "Had"

Learn how to talk about the past using "had" 🎉

💡

What is "Had"?

We use "had" as a state verb to talk about things that were true in the past. We use it to show:

🤒
Illness
"She had fever."
🎒
Possession
"I had money."
👨‍👩‍👧
Relation
"They had a friend."

🗓️ "Had" always tells us about something that happened in the PAST!

✅

Affirmative Sentences

Subject + had + extra words

For all subjects (I, you, we, they, he, she, it), we use "had" — it doesn't change!

SubjectHelping VerbExtra Words
I / you / we / theyhadfever
He / she / ithadfever

Examples:

✅ Affirmative She had a cough.
✅ Affirmative I had money.
✅ Affirmative He had a great time at the amusement park.
❌

Negative Sentences

Subject + didn't have + extra words

To make a sentence negative, we replace "had" with "didn't have" (did + not + have).

SubjectHelping VerbExtra Words
I / you / we / theydidn't havefever
He / she / itdidn't havefever

Examples:

❌ Negative She didn't have a cough.
❌ Negative I didn't have money.
❌ Negative He didn't have a great time at the amusement park.
❓

Question Sentences

Did + Subject + have + extra words?

To ask a question, we put "Did" at the beginning, then the subject, then "have".

Helping VerbSubjectHave + Extra words
DidI / you / we / theyhave fever?
DidHe / she / ithave fever?

Examples:

❓ Question Did she have a cough?
❓ Question Did I have money?
❓ Question Did he have a great time at the amusement park?
