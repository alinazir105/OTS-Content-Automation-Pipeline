Learning Articles: A, An & The

Special little words that go before nouns — and make a big difference!

A
An
The
💡
What Are Articles?
Let's find out what articles do!
Articles are special words that we use before nouns to talk about something specific or general.
I saw a dog in the park.
He ate an apple for lunch.
The dog I saw in the park was very friendly.
A
Before consonant sounds
a chair · a dog · a book
An
Before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u)
an apple · an elephant · an owl
The
For specific or unique things
the Sun · the Eiffel Tower · the sky
🅰️
When to Use "A"
Before consonant sounds, for one non-specific thing
"A" is used before words that begin with a consonant sound (like b, c, d, f, g…) and talk about one thing that is not specific.

Think of it as "any one of these" — not a particular one!
✏️ I have a pencil.
🐦 She saw a bird in the tree.
🥭 He is eating a mango.
🐱 There is a cat under the chair.
🐕 A dog is barking outside.
🎈 I want to buy a red balloon.
🌸 She found a flower in the garden.
⭐ Can you draw a star?
🐸 There is a frog in the pond.
📚 He has a book in his bag.
🔤
When to Use "An"
Before vowel sounds (a, e, i, o, u)
"An" is used before words that begin with a vowel sound — A, E, I, O, U — and talk about one thing that is not specific.

Remember: it's about the sound, not just the letter!

🎯
When to Use "The"
For specific, known, or unique things
"The" is used when we are talking about something specific, something we already know about, or something that is the only one of its kind.
1
Talking About Something Specific
Use "the" before something that both the speaker and listener know about.

💬 "I saw the teacher in the classroom."
(A specific teacher we both know.)
2
Unique Things
Use "the" before something that is the only one of its kind.

🌞 "The Sun is bright today."
(There is only one sun!)
3
Mentioning Something Again
When you mention something for the second time, use "the".

🐦 "I saw a bird. The bird was flying high."
