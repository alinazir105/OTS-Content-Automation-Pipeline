This, That, These & Those

Learn when to use the right word — near or far, one or many!

👆 👋 📚 👟
💡
The Big Idea
Near vs Far · Singular vs Plural

We use demonstrative pronouns to point to people and things.
The word we choose depends on two things:
1️⃣ Is it near us or far from us?
2️⃣ Is it one thing (singular) or more than one (plural)?

👆🧒
This
Singular · Near
🧒 "This is my brother, Sami."
(One person, right next to you)
👉🌳
That
Singular · Far
🌳 "That's my brother."
(One person, far away)
📚📚
These
Plural · Near
📚 "These books are mine."
(Many things, close to you)
👟👟👟
Those
Plural · Far
👟 "I like those shoes."
(Many things, far away)
Word Singular / Plural Near / Far Example
This Singular 📍 Near "This is my cat."
That Singular 🔭 Far "That is a big tree."
These Plural 📍 Near "These are my books."
Those Plural 🔭 Far "I like those shoes."
