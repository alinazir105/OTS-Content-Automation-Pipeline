📖 Class 2 · English · Lesson 9

Use of "Have"

Learn how to use "have" and "has" to talk about things you own, how you feel, and your family!

🏠 I have a house
🤒 She has fever
👨‍👩‍👦 We have uncles
🚗 He has a car

📌 What does "Have" mean?

We use "have" as a state verb to show three things:

🤒 Illness

When someone is sick or not feeling well.

Example
I have fever.
Example
She has headache.
🎒 Possession

When someone owns or has something.

Example
He has a new car.
Example
They have a doll.
👨‍👩‍👧 Relation

When talking about family members or relationships.

Example
We have two uncles.
Example
I have three cousins.

✅ Affirmative Sentences

Positive sentences — saying what someone HAS.

📋 The Form

Subject + have/has + extra words

Subject Verb Extra Words
I / you / we / they have fever
He / she / it / Ali has fever
💡
Use have with I, you, we, they.
Use has with he, she, it, and names like Ali or Sana.

❌ Negative Sentences

Negative sentences — saying what someone does NOT have.

🚫 The Form

Subject + do not / does not + have + extra words

❌ With I / you / we / they

I / you / we / they don't have fever

❌ With He / she / it / Ali

He / she / it / Ali doesn't have fever
🧠
Do not = don't (used with I, you, we, they)
Does not = doesn't (used with he, she, it, names)

❓ Question Sentences

How to ask if someone has something.

🙋 The Form

Do/Does + Subject + have + extra words + ?

❓ With I / you / we / they

Do + I / you / we / they + have fever?

❓ With He / she / it / Ali

Does + he / she / it / Ali + have fever?

✂️ Short Forms (Contractions)

We can make short forms by joining the subject with "have" or "has"!

🔗 Long Form → Short Form
I have→I've
You have→You've
He has→He's
She has→She's
It has→It's
We have→We've
They have→They've
