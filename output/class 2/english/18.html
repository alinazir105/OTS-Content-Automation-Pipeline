What are Adjectives?

Adjectives are words that describe nouns or pronouns. They tell us more about a person, place, or thing — such as size, colour, shape, and more!

👉 The big dog barked loudly.

👉 She wore a red dress.

The adjective "big" describes the noun "dog"

⚖️

Comparative Adjectives

Comparative adjectives are used to compare two things or people. They are formed by:

Add -er
big → bigger
tall → taller
fast → faster
Use more
intelligent → more intelligent
interesting → more interesting

👉 This book is bigger than that one.

👉 She is more intelligent than her brother.

🏆

Superlative Adjectives

Superlative adjectives are used to compare more than two things or people and show the extreme quality of one. They are formed by:

Add -est
tall → tallest
big → biggest
hot → hottest
Use most
interesting → most interesting
funny → funniest

👉 He is the tallest boy in the class.

👉 This is the most interesting story I have read.
