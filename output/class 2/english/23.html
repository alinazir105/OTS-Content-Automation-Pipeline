What Is Composition?

A composition is when we write sentences or a short story about something — like a picture, a person, or a place. Good writers always look carefully, think, and then write clearly!

💡 Tips for Great Writing

  • Look at the picture carefully before you write.
  • Use the word bank to get ideas.
  • Start each sentence with a capital letter.
  • End each sentence with a full stop (.) or question mark (?).
  • Try to write interesting sentences, not just "I see a dog."
