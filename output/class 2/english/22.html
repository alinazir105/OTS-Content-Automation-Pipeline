Sad Dad

Story 6: A Day with Grandpa

Tom and Grandpa have a wonderful day on the farm.

6
A Day with Grandpa

Tom spent the day with his Grandpa on the farm. In the morning, they fed the chickens and collected eggs. "These eggs are fresh!" said Grandpa. Tom held the basket carefully. After that, they planted seeds in the garden. Tom dug small holes while Grandpa dropped in the seeds. "One day, these will grow into big plants," Grandpa explained. In the afternoon, they sat under a big tree and ate sandwiches. Grandpa told Tom stories about when he was a boy. Tom loved hearing about the old days. "Can we do this again tomorrow?" Tom asked. Grandpa smiled and said, "Of course, Tom."

✏️ Answer the Questions
Q1. Where did Tom spend the day?
Click to answer...
Choose the best answer:
Tom spent the day on the farm with his Grandpa. Tom spent the day at school. Tom spent the day at the park.
Q2. What did Tom and Grandpa do in the morning?
Click to answer...
Choose the best answer:
They fed the chickens, collected eggs, and planted seeds in the garden. They ate sandwiches under a big tree. They played in the park.
Q3. What did Grandpa tell Tom while they were eating?
Click to answer...
Choose the best answer:

Q4. What did Tom ask Grandpa at the end of the day?
Click to answer...
Choose the best answer:
Tom asked: Can we do this again tomorrow? Tom asked: Can we go to school? Tom asked: Can I have cake?
Q5. What do you think Tom enjoyed the most about his day with Grandpa?
Click to answer...
Choose the best answer:
