My Flower

A poem about growing a flower with love and patience

My Flower
By Margaret Schroder
  • I had a little seed,
  • And I put it in a pot.
  • I put it in the sunshine,
  • And I watered it a lot.
  • I waited and I waited.
  • I watched it grow and grow.
  • And then one day a flower
  • Came out to say hello.
🌱🌸
💡 What happens in this poem?
  1. A seed is planted in a pot 🪴
  2. It gets sunshine ☀️ and water 💧
  3. After waiting, a flower blooms! 🌸

Rain

A poem about rain falling everywhere — on land and sea

Rain
By Robert Louis Stevenson
  • The rain is raining all around,
  • It falls on field and tree;
  • It rains on the umbrellas here,
  • And on the ships at sea.
🌧️⛵🌲
💡 Where does it rain?
  • On fields 🌾
  • On trees 🌳
  • On umbrellas ☂️
  • On ships at sea ⛵

Rhyming Words

Poems use rhyming words to make them fun to say and easy to remember. Can you spot the rhymes?

too ↔ do ✏️
