Question Words

Learn when to use Who, Whose, What and Which to ask great questions!

📖

Let's Learn!

There are two kinds of questions:

1. Yes / No Questions

Do you like chocolate? → Yes, I do.

Did Tahir enjoy the party? → No, he didn't.

2. Wh- Questions

These begin with question words like who, whose, what, and which.

WHO

Questions about People

Use who when you want to know about a person.

🗣️ Who sits next to you in class?
Sami. 👋
🗣️ Who are those people?
My friends! 😊
WHOSE

Questions about Possessions

Use whose when you want to know who owns something.

🗣️ Whose book is this?
It's mine. 📚
🗣️ Whose CDs are those?
They're Ali's. 💿
WHAT

Questions about Things

Use what when you want to know about something.

🗣️ What are you doing?
I'm doing my homework. 📝
🗣️ What's that?
It's an MP3 player. 🎵
WHICH

Questions about Choices

Use which when choosing between people or things.

🗣️ Which boy is your brother? (I can see several boys.)
That boy, over there. 👦
🗣️ Which jacket is yours? (I can see several jackets.)
The blue one. 🧥
