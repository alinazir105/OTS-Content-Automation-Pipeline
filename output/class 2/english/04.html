Learning About Pronouns!

Discover how pronouns replace nouns to make our sentences shorter and smoother 🎉

🙋

Subject Pronouns

Subject Pronouns replace the subject of a sentence — the person or thing doing the action. They include: I, you, we, he, she, it, they.

Singular

I
You
She
He
It

Plural

We
You
They

Examples:

  • Ali likes chocolate. → He likes chocolate.
  • Hira and Sami live in London. → They live in London.
  • Sara is drawing a picture. → She is drawing a picture.
  • The cat is sleeping. → It is sleeping.
  • Ali and I are playing football. → We are playing football.
  • The birds are flying. → They are flying.
  • My brother goes to school. → He goes to school.
  • The book is on the table. → It is on the table.
  • My parents are cooking dinner. → They are cooking dinner.
  • The dog is barking. → It is barking.
  • Hassan is eating an apple. → He is eating an apple.
🎯

Object Pronouns

Object Pronouns replace the object of a sentence — the person or thing receiving the action. They include: me, you, us, him, her, it, them.

me
you
us
him
her
it
them

Examples:

  • The teacher called him.
  • Can you help us?
  • Ali gave me a book.
  • I will help you with your homework.
  • Our teacher told us a story.
  • Sara called him on the phone.
  • The dog followed her to the park.
  • I found it under the bed.
  • We saw them at the playground.
💎

Possessive Pronouns

Possessive Pronouns show that something belongs to someone. They include: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.

mine
yours
his
hers
ours
theirs

Examples:

  • This book is mine.
  • The toys are theirs.
  • This pencil is yours.
  • The bag is his.
  • The dress is hers.
  • This ball is ours.
  • These shoes are mine.
  • The kittens are its.
  • The house is theirs.
