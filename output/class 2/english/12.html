Uses of the Present Simple

We use the present simple to talk about three main things:

🌍

General Truths

Facts that are always true about the world around us.

🗣 "They speak English in Australia."
🗣 "The sun rises in the East."
📅

Habits and Routines

Things we do regularly — every day or every week.

🗣 "I go to school every day."
🗣 "Sami plays football on Saturdays."
🏠

Permanent Situations

Things that stay the same for a long time.

🗣 "We live in London."
📝

How to Form the Present Simple

📐 Formula: Subject + Verb (1st Form) + Object

SubjectVerb FormExample
I / We / You / They work They work hard.
He / She / It / Zain works She works hard.
💡
With he, she, it and singular nouns, add "s / es / ies" to the 1st form of the verb.
Example: go → goes | study → studies | play → plays
❌

Negatives and Questions

Negatives
I / we / you / they → don't work
He / she / it / Zain → doesn't work
💡
The main verb does NOT change — no "s/es/ies" in negatives!
Questions
Do I / we / you / they work?
Does He / she / it / Zain work?

Questions and Short Answers

Do I go?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Do you go?Yes, you do. / No, you don't.
Does he go?Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
Does she go?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Does it go?Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.
Do we go?Yes, we do. / No, we don't.
Do they go?Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
