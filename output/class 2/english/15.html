🔵 Present Continuous Tense Happening Now!

We use Present Continuous for actions happening right now.

📌 How to form it?

Subject + is/am/are + verb + -ing

I am playing She is running They are eating

The action is happening right now as you speak! 🕐

🏃‍♂️ 🎨 📖 🎵 🍎

Look at these people — they are all doing something right now!

🟠 Present Simple Tense Every Day!

We use Present Simple for habits, routines and facts.

📌 How to form it?

Subject + verb (add -s for he/she/it)

I play She plays They run The dog barks

Used for things that happen every day / always / usually ☀️

☀️ 🏫 🍎 🎶 🐕

These things happen every day — that's Present Simple!

🟣 Past Simple Tense Already Done!

We use Past Simple for actions that are already finished.

📌 How to form it?

For regular verbs: add -ed to the verb.

played cooked watched walked

Some verbs are irregular: sleep → slept, draw → drew

Signal words: yesterday, last night, last weekend, ago ⏰

⏮️ 📅 🕰️ 📼 🔙

Past Simple = something that happened before now!
