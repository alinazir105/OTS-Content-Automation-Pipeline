Question Words

Learn when to use Where, When, Why, and How to ask questions!

🗺️ Where
⏰ When
🤔 Why
✨ How
📋 Quick Overview

Each question word has a special job. Look at the table to see what each one asks about!

Word Asks About Example Question Example Answer
Where 📍 Place Where do you live? In London.
When ⏰ Time When does class end? At twelve o'clock.
Why 🤔 Reason Why are you laughing? Because this book is funny.
How ✨ Manner How do you go to school? I walk.

🗺️ WHERE — Questions about Place

We use Where when we want to know about a place or location.

🧑
Where do you live?
👧
In London.
🧑
Where's the cat?
👧
Behind the chair.
👨‍🏫
Where's your homework?
👧
It's in my bag!
⏰ WHEN — Questions about Time

We use When when we want to know about a time.

🧑
When does this lesson end?
👧
At twelve o'clock.
🧑
When did you go to Seaview?
👧
Last summer.
🧑
When did you get up?
👧
At seven o'clock!
🤔 WHY — Questions about Reason

We use Why when we want to know the reason for something. Answers often start with Because.

🧑
Why are you laughing?
👧
Because this book is funny!
🧑
Why do you need an umbrella?
👧
Because it's raining!
👨‍🏫
Why are you late?
👧
Because I missed the bus.
✨ HOW — Questions about Manner

We use How when we want to know the way something is done.

🧑
How do you go to school?
👧
I walk.
🧑
How did he know the answer?
👧
He used the internet.
🧑
How did you get here?
👧
By bus!
💬 Expressions with "How"

Many common expressions start with How. Learn them all!

How are you?
"I'm fine, thanks!" / "I'm very well."
How old are you?
"I'm seven." / "I'm eleven years old."
How do you spell your name?
"S-o-f-i-a"
How about playing a game?
"Yes, good idea!" / "No, thanks."
Same as: What about / Let's
