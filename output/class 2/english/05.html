What are Possessive Determiners?

We use possessive determiners before nouns. They give information about possession (who owns something) or relation (who belongs to whom). They always come before a noun!

📌 Remember:

Possessive determiners come before a noun. They tell us who something belongs to. For example: "my bike", "his name", "their garden".
