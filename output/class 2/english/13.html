What is the Present Continuous?

We use the Present Continuous Tense to talk about things that are happening right now or around now.


🏃
She is running.
🎨
He is drawing.
🌧️
It is raining.
🎵
They are singing.
🧮

The Formula

Subject  +  Helping Verb (is / am / are)  +  Verb¹ + ing  +  Object

We add -ing to the main verb. The helping verb changes based on who is doing the action.


I → am You / We / They → are He / She / It → is
  • ✅ I am walking.
  • ✅ You are eating.
  • ✅ She is reading.
  • ✅ They are playing.
✅

Affirmative Sentences

Positive statements — saying what IS happening.

Subject Helping Verb Verb + ing
I am walking.
You / We / They are walking.
He / She / It / Zain is walking.
❌

Negative Sentences

We add not after the helping verb.

Subject Helping Verb + Not Verb + ing
I am not walking.
You / We / They are not (aren't) walking.
He / She / It / Zain is not (isn't) walking.
❓

Interrogative (Questions)

For questions, the helping verb comes first!

Helping Verb Subject Verb + ing?
Am I walking?
Are you / we / they walking?
Is he / she / it / Zain walking?
💬

Questions and Short Answers

When someone asks you a question, you can give a short answer!

Am I going? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Are you going? Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
Is he going? Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.
Is she going? Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Is it going? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
Are we going? Yes, we are. / No, we aren't.
Are they going? Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
