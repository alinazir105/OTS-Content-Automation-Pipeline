Past Simple Tense 🕐

Learn how to talk about things that already happened! We'll practise with fun activities.

📖

What is the Past Simple?

We use the past simple to describe completed actions and situations in the past — things that are already finished!

⏰ → ✅

Think of it like this: something happened before now and it is done. For example, if you ate breakfast this morning, that is past simple!

✏️

Examples

It was 9 o'clock. Sami opened the door and walked to the bus stop.

Last night I finished my homework and watched TV.

We went to France last month. We stayed in Paris.

I climbed the Eiffel Tower and I took a lot of photos. It was great.

Last Sunday, I tidied my room, cleaned the house, cooked a meal and washed the dishes.

Last Sunday, I visited my aunt, helped my mum, phoned my grandma and posted some letters.
