Can and Could: Ability

Time ✅ Affirmative ❌ Negative
Present I can play the drums. I can't play the piano.
Past Ali could speak German and English. He couldn't speak Japanese.
📌 Remember the Rule!

We use can / can't + base verb to talk about ability now (present).
We use could / couldn't + base verb to talk about ability before (past).
The verb form stays the same for I, you, he, she, it, we, they.

🎨 Examples

🚴

When I was young, I could ride a bicycle.

🎹

Sarah can play the piano very well now.

🗣️

They could speak French when they lived in Paris.

🔑

Permission & Requests

Give or Refuse Permission

✅
Give Permission
"You can come in now!"
❌
Refuse Permission
"You can't go out now."

Ask for Permission

🙋 "Can I see the menu, please?"
🎩 "Could I see the menu, please?"

Make Requests

💬 "Can you tidy your room now, please?"
🎩 "Could you open the door, please?"
💡 Could is more formal and politer than Can.
