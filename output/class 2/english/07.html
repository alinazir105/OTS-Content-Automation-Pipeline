Use of There Is / There Are

Learn how to talk about things and people that exist around us!

📝
Affirmative and Negative
When do we use "There is" and "There are"?

We use there is and there are to say that something or somebody exists. We use there is for one (singular) person or thing, and there are for more than one (plural) people or things. We often shorten there is to there's.

✅ Singular — There is / There's
🎬 There's a cinema in our town.
🏊 There isn't a swimming pool.
✅ Plural — There are / There aren't
👨‍👩‍👧 There are three people in the garden.
👥 There aren't four people.
💡 Remember: We often say there's (= there is), but in short answers we always say Yes, there is — never Yes, there's!

Yes/No Questions and Answers
How do we ask and answer with "Is there" and "Are there"?
🙋 Question
Is there a bus?
Is there a train?
Is there a van?
Are there lots of people?
💬 Short Answer
Yes, there is.
No, there isn't.
Yes, there is.
No, there aren't.
⚠️ In short answers, we do NOT say "Yes, there's" — always say "Yes, there is."
