🐶

Common Nouns

Chapter 1 · Nouns

Common nouns are the names of items, places, or people in general. They do not refer to anything specific.
🐕 dog
🏙️ city
📕 book
🍎 apple

These words name things in general — not a specific dog or a specific city!

🏛️

Proper Nouns

Specific names — always with a Capital Letter!

Proper nouns are specific names of people, places, or things. They always start with a capital letter.
🕌 Quaid's Tomb
🗼 Eiffel Tower
🇵🇰 Pakistan
🕍 Taj Mahal

Notice how every proper noun starts with a Capital Letter! 🎯

👥

Collective Nouns

Words for groups of things or people

Collective nouns are words used to describe a group of things or people.
🤝 team of players
🐑 flock
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 family
🍇 bunch

📋 List of Collective Nouns

1🐄 herd of cattle
2🐺 pack of wolves
3🐝 swarm of bees
4🚢 fleet of ships
5🐟 school of fish
6🍇 bunch of grapes
7🎵 choir of singers
8👨‍👩‍👧 crowd of people
9🪵 bundle of sticks
10⚔️ troop of soldiers
