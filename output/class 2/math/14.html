What is Time?

A clock helps us tell the time. It has numbers from 1 to 12 arranged in a circle. There are two hands on a clock that point to the numbers to tell us what time it is.

🕐
Short Hand
Tells us the hour
⏱
Long Hand
Tells us the minutes
🖐

The Clock Hands

⬆️

O' Clock

Minute hand points to 12. It is exactly the hour!

3 o'clock

⬇️

Half Past

Minute hand points to 6. 30 minutes past the hour!

Half past 3

➡️

Quarter Past

Minute hand points to 3. 15 minutes past the hour!

Quarter past 3

⬅️

Quarter To

Minute hand points to 9. 15 minutes before the hour!

Quarter to 4

Key Concepts

⏰

Hour Hand

The short hand — tells us the hour. It moves slowly around the clock once every 12 hours.

⏱

Minute Hand

The long hand — tells us the minutes. It moves around the clock once every 60 minutes.

🔢

Clock Numbers

A clock has numbers 1–12. Each number represents 5 minutes on the minute scale.

🌅

AM & PM

AM = morning (midnight to noon). PM = afternoon/evening (noon to midnight).
