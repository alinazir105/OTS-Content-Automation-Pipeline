What is Place Value?

Every digit in a number sits in a special spot called a place. The value of a digit depends on its position in the number. The three places we study are Ones, Tens, and Hundreds.

8

Ones Place

The rightmost digit. It tells how many single units there are. Example: in 128, the digit 8 is in the ones place.

7

Tens Place

The middle digit. It tells how many groups of ten there are. Example: in 278, the digit 7 is in the tens place.

2

Hundreds Place

The leftmost digit in a 3-digit number. It tells how many groups of hundred. Example: in 278, the digit 2 is in the hundreds place.
