What Are Even and Odd Numbers?
Let's understand the two types of numbers!
All numbers are either Even or Odd. You can tell them apart by looking at the last digit of the number!

Even Numbers
Numbers that can be shared equally into two groups
Even numbers end in: 0 2 4 6 8

Examples: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 ... 100, 102 ... 200, 202 ... 300

💡 Tip: If the last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 — it is Even!
