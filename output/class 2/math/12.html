What is Subtraction?

Subtraction means taking away a smaller number from a bigger number to find out how many are left. We use the minus sign ( − ) for subtraction.

97 minus 74 equals 23!

How to Subtract

We write numbers in a Tens (T) and Ones (O) table. Always subtract the Ones column first, then the Tens column.

  • 1Write the bigger number on top in the T–O table.
  • 2Write the smaller number below it with a minus sign (−).
  • 3Subtract the Ones (O) column: 7 − 4 = 3
  • 4Subtract the Tens (T) column: 9 − 7 = 2
  • 5Write the answer: 23
