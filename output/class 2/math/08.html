What Are Ordinal Numbers?
Numbers that tell us the position or order of something

Ordinal numbers tell us where something is in a list or order. We use them every day — like who came first in a race, which is the second floor of a building, or what is the third item in a line!

1
Cardinal numbers count: 1, 2, 3...
2
Ordinal numbers show position: 1st, 2nd, 3rd...
3
They have special word endings!
1st = First
2nd = Second
3rd = Third
4th = Fourth
5th = Fifth

Special endings to remember:

1st → st
2nd → nd
3rd → rd
4th onwards → th
