Comparing Numbers

Learn to use >, <, and = to compare numbers — just like a hungry crocodile choosing the bigger meal! 🐊

🐊 < = > 🐊

🔣

The Three Comparison Symbols

When we compare two numbers, we use one of three special symbols. Think of the symbol as a crocodile mouth — the crocodile always wants to eat the bigger number!

>
Greater Than
The left number is bigger
=
Equal To
Both numbers are the same
<
Less Than
The left number is smaller
🐊➡️
Greater Than (>)
Mouth opens left → bigger left
🐊🤝
Equal To (=)
Same on both sides
⬅️🐊
Less Than (<)
Mouth opens right → bigger right

How to Compare Numbers

Follow these steps to compare any two numbers:

  1. 1
    Count the digits. A number with more digits is always bigger. For example, 1000 > 999 because 1000 has 4 digits and 999 has 3.
  2. 2
    Compare Hundreds. If both numbers have the same number of digits, look at the hundreds place first. The bigger hundreds digit wins.
  3. 3
    Compare Tens. If hundreds are the same, look at the tens place.
  4. 4
    Compare Ones. If tens are also the same, look at the ones place.
  5. 5
    Use the right symbol. Write >, <, or = between the two numbers based on what you found.

📊 Example: Compare 525 and 495

NumberHundredsTensOnes
525 5 2 5
495 4 9 5

Hundreds: 5 > 4, so 525 > 495 ✅
