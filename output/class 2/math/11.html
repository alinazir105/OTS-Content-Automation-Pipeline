What is Addition?
Understanding the idea of putting numbers together

Addition means putting two or more numbers together to get a bigger number. The answer we get is called the sum or total. We use the + (plus) sign to show addition!

2 + 2 = 4 ⬆

The arrow points to the sum (the answer)!

🔢 Ones & Tens (2-digit)

When we add two numbers with tens and ones, we line them up carefully and add from right to left — ones first, then tens!

🏛️ Hundreds, Tens & Ones (3-digit)

Bigger numbers have a hundreds place too! We still add from right to left — ones, then tens, then hundreds.

📦 Carrying Over

When ones add up to 10 or more, we "carry" the extra ten to the tens column. This is called regrouping!

✅ Checking Your Answer

Always check your work by adding again from the top. If you get the same answer — you're correct!

📋
How to Add Step by Step
Follow these steps every time you add
  • Write the numbers one under the other, lining up ones under ones, tens under tens, and hundreds under hundreds.
  • Always start from the ones column on the right side.
  • Add the ones digits. If the answer is 10 or more, write the ones digit and carry the ten to the tens column.
  • Add the tens digits. Don't forget to include any number you carried!
  • Add the hundreds digits (for 3-digit numbers). Again, carry if needed.
  • Write your final answer under the line. That's your sum!
