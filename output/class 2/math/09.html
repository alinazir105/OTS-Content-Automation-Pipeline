What is a Fraction?
Understanding the parts

Fractions represent the parts of a whole or a collection of objects. A fraction has two parts separated by a line.

⬆️ Numerator

The number on top of the line. It tells how many parts we have.

3
—

⬇️ Denominator

The number below the line. It tells the total equal parts.
