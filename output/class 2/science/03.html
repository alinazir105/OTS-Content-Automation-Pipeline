❓ What is a Habitat?

Animals live in different places that provide them food, water, shelter, and a safe space to grow. These places where animals live are called habitats.

Some habitats are made by nature, while others are made by humans.

🗂️ Types of Habitats

There are two main types of habitats. Let's explore each one!

🌿

Natural Habitats

Found in nature — forests, grasslands, deserts, polar regions, bodies of water

🏗️

Man-made Habitats

Created by humans — farms, gardens, aquariums, zoos

🌿 Natural Habitats

Nature gives animals wonderful places to live and stay safe. Here are the five main natural habitats:

🌲

Forest

Densely covered with large trees and small plants. A rich, green home for many wild animals.

🐯 Tiger 🐒 Monkey 🦌 Deer 🐍 Snake 🦜 Parrot 🐛 Insects
🌾

Grassland

Covered with grass and small plants, with only a few scattered trees. Wide open spaces for large animals.

🦁 Lion 🐘 Elephant 🐆 Cheetah 🦘 Kangaroo 🦒 Giraffe
🏜️

Desert

Dry areas covered with sand, rocks, or even ice. Very few plants like cactus grow here.

🐍 Snake 🐪 Camel 🦎 Lizard 🦅 Birds 🐛 Insects
❄️

Polar Regions

Covered with ice and snow. Very few plants. Two regions: Arctic (North Pole) and Antarctica (South Pole).

🐻‍❄️ Polar Bear 🐧 Penguin 🦊 Arctic Fox
🌊

Bodies of Water

Includes oceans, rivers, lakes, and ponds. Animals live in or near water.

🐟 Fish 🐸 Frog 🐢 Turtle 🦀 Crab 🐋 Whale 🦈 Shark 🐬 Dolphin
🏗️ Man-made Habitats

Humans create places where animals can live and be protected. Here are some examples:

🚜

Farm

Places where cows, chickens, and sheep live and are taken care of by farmers.

🐄 Cow 🐔 Chicken 🐑 Sheep
🦒

Zoo

Places where different animals from around the world are kept and taken care of.

🦁 Lion 🐘 Elephant 🐍 Snake
🌸

Garden

Places where insects, birds, and small animals find food and shelter.

🦋 Butterfly 🐝 Bee 🐦 Bird
🐠

Aquarium

Water tanks where fish and water animals live safely.

🐠 Fish 🐙 Octopus 🐢 Turtle
💡 Why Are Habitats Important?

Habitats are important because they give animals everything they need to live. Without a good habitat, animals would not survive. That is why we must take care of their homes!

🍎

Food

Habitats provide food for animals to eat and grow strong.

💧

Water

Animals need water to drink and stay healthy.

🏠

Shelter

Habitats give animals a safe place to rest and sleep.

🛡️

Protection

Habitats protect animals from danger and bad weather.
