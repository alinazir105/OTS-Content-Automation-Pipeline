What Are Some Kinds of Animals?

There are many kinds of animals in the world!

Animals have different body coverings to help them live. For example, fur keeps otters warm and dry in cold water. Each animal group has special features that help it survive.

The Six Animal Groups

1
Mammals 🦁
🐴🐶🐱🐘
Features They have hair or fur on their bodies and they feed their babies milk.
Examples:
🐴 Horse 🐶 Dog 🐱 Cat 🐘 Elephant
2
Birds 🐦
🦜🕊️🦅🐦
Features They have feathers on their bodies and they lay eggs.
Examples:
🦜 Parrot 🕊️ Pigeon 🐦 Sparrow 🦅 Eagle
3
Reptiles 🦎
🐍🦎🐢
Features They have dry skin covered in scales. Their scales protect them and stop them from drying out.
Examples:
🐍 Snake 🦎 Lizard 🐢 Turtle
4
Insects 🐝
🐜🦋🐞
Features They have three body parts — head, thorax, abdomen — and six legs.
🔬 Three body parts: Head + Thorax + Abdomen
Examples:
🐜 Ant 🦋 Butterfly 🐞 Ladybug
5
Amphibians 🐸
🐸🐊
Features They can live both on land and in water. They have smooth, moist skin and lay eggs in water.
Examples:
🐸 Frog 🐸 Toad 🦎 Salamander
6
Fish 🐟
🐟🦈🐠
Features They live in water, breathe with gills, and have fins to swim.
Examples:
🐠 Goldfish 🦈 Shark 🐟 Salmon

Body Coverings

Animals have different body coverings that help them survive in their environment. Each covering serves a special purpose!

🦁

Fur / Hair

Keeps mammals warm and dry. Example: otters' fur keeps them warm in cold water.

🐦

Feathers

Help birds fly and keep them warm. Also provide colour and camouflage.

🦎

Scales

Protect reptiles and prevent water loss, keeping their skin from drying out.

🐸

Moist Skin

Helps amphibians breathe through their skin and stay hydrated on land.

🌱

What Do Animals Need to Live?

All animals need certain things to survive. Without these, they cannot live!

🍃

Food

Bears eat plants. Lions eat meat. Each animal eats what it needs.

💧

Water

All animals need fresh water to drink and stay alive.

💨

Air

Most animals breathe air. Fish use gills to get oxygen from water.

🏠

Safe Place to Live

Raccoons live in logs. Birds build nests. Every animal needs shelter.

🐻 Example: A bear eats plants and finds food in the forest. Raccoons live safely inside a hollow log!
