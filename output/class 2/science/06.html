What Are Natural Resources?

A natural resource is something from Earth that people use. Rocks, minerals, plants, soil, and water are natural resources. We use natural resources to make things we use every day!

🌟 Did You Know? Everything around you — your chair, your clothes, your food — comes from a natural resource!

Rocks and Soil

🪨 Rocks

Rocks break down over time and become part of the soil. We mix rocks, sand, and water to make concrete — used for buildings and sidewalks. We use minerals in sand to make glass!

🌱 Soil

Plants use soil to grow! We grow food in soil, and we use plants to make paper and clothing. Soil is one of our most important natural resources.

✏️ Parts of a Pencil — From Nature!

Look at all the parts of a pencil — they all come from natural resources!

🌳 Eraser Liquid from a rubber tree
🪵 Yellow Body Made of wood (from trees)
🪨 Gray Tip Made of graphite (a mineral)
💧

Water and Wind

💧 Water

We use water to drink, cook, and clean. We use it to help plants grow. Moving water over a dam can make electric power to light and heat our homes!

💨 Wind

Have you seen a flag wave in the wind? Wind can make things move! Windmills use moving wind to make electric power — just like water!

⚡ Fun Fact: When water flows over a dam very quickly, it spins huge machines called turbines that make electricity for thousands of homes!
🌾

Plant Resources

Many plants are used for food. Fruits, vegetables, and seeds come from plants. The seeds of some plants are called grains. Grains are crushed into flour to make cereal and bread!

🌾

Wheat

Seeds (grains) are ground into flour to bake bread and make cereal.

🌿

Cotton

Cotton from plants is used to make clothes and rugs.

🪵

Wood

Wood from trees is used to make buildings, furniture, and paper.

Animal Resources

🥛 Food from Animals

Many people eat chicken, fish, and other animals. Milk from cows, goats, and sheep is used to make butter and cheese!

👢 Materials from Animals

Leather is made from animal skins. Shoes and coats can be made from leather. Animals give us many useful things!

What Is Fuel?

A fuel is something that gives off heat when it burns. We burn wood, coal, and oil as fuels. Long ago, coal and oil formed underground from dead plants and animals. Now we use them to cook and heat our homes!

🪵

Wood

Burns to give heat. Used for cooking and warmth.

⚫

Coal

Formed underground. Used in power plants to make electricity.

⛽

Oil / Gasoline

Gasoline is made from oil. Cars and trucks burn it to move!
