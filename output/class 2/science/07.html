Position

Where is something?

Position is the place where something is. You can describe the position of an object by comparing it to something that does not move. You can use words such as in, on, under, next to, left, right, above, below, near, and far to describe position.

Distance

How far away is something?

Distance is how far away one thing is from another. There are different ways to measure distance. One way is by using a map.

📐 Standard Units

You can use units like:
  • Inches (in) – for small things
  • Feet (ft) – for rooms and heights
  • Miles (mi) – for long distances

📐 Metric Units

You can also use metric units like:
  • Centimetres (cm) – for small things
  • Metres (m) – for rooms and heights
  • Kilometres (km) – for long distances

🗺️ Measuring on a Map

Maps use a scale to show how far apart places are. For example, 1 cm on the map might equal 1 kilometre in real life!

When something changes its position!

Motion is a change in position. When something is moving, we say it is in motion. A diver starts at the top of a diving board, jumps into the air, and his body changes position — that is motion!

Speed

How fast does something move?

Speed is how far something moves in a certain amount of time. Most people can run half a mile in 5 minutes. But an Olympic runner can run 1 mile in just 5 minutes or less — they are much faster!

Pushes and Pulls

The forces that make things move!

Things cannot move by themselves. You have to use a force to make something move. A push or a pull is a force. When you play soccer, you kick the ball — your kick is a force!
👋➡️

Push

If you push something, it moves away from you. A kick is a kind of push. When you push a box, it slides away!

Pull

If you pull something, it moves closer to you. When you open a drawer, you pull it toward yourself!

Friction

The force that slows things down!

Friction is a force that slows down moving things. It happens when two things rub together. There is more friction on a rough surface than on a smooth one. Friction makes it harder to move something on a rough surface than on a smooth one.

✅ When Friction Helps

Running shoes have treads (patterns) that add friction. This keeps runners from slipping and falling!

👟
Shoe treads grip the ground!

🩰 When Less Friction Helps

The bottoms of ballet slippers are smooth so dancers can slide easily across the floor. Surfboards are also smooth to glide on waves!
