🌿 Plant Life Cycles

Discover how plants grow, make seeds, and keep our world green!

🌍 Unit 5 📚 Class 2 🔬 Science
🎯 What Will You Learn?
Learning Objectives for Unit 5
  • Identify roots, stems, leaves, flowers, fruits, and seeds — and describe what they do.
  • Explain how plants in rainforests, deserts, and other places change to survive.
  • Understand that flowers make seeds, fruits protect seeds, and seeds grow into new plants.
  • Explain how animals, wind, and water help move seeds to new places.
  • Describe how a plant grows from a seed, changes, and makes new plants.
  • Explain how young plants look like their parent plants.
  • Understand that plants need light, water, and soil to grow.
🌿 Parts of a Plant
Roots, Stems, and Leaves each have an important job!

Plants use their roots, stems, and leaves to get light and water. Each part has a very special job to help the plant live and grow.

☀️
🍃 Leaves
Use light to make food
🌿 Stem — holds plant up
🪱 Roots
Hold plant in ground.
Take in water from soil.
🪴

Roots

Hold the plant in the ground and take in water from the soil.

🌿

Stem

Holds the plant up and carries water from roots to leaves.

🍃

Leaves

Use sunlight to make food for the plant (photosynthesis!).

🌱 All About Roots
Roots come in many shapes and sizes!

Some roots are long and thin. Others are short and thick. The type of root depends on where the plant lives!

🏜️

Dry Places (Desert)

Plants have long roots that grow deep down to find water underground.

🌊

Wet Places

Plants like the banyan tree have roots above the ground so they don't get too much water.

🐻 Did you know? Animals like bears, raccoons, and porcupines eat roots! We eat roots too — carrots, radishes, and beets are all roots!
🥕

Carrots

A root we eat! It grows underground.

🫚

Beetroot

Another tasty root that grows in the soil.

🌳

Banyan Tree

Lives in a wet place — its roots grow above the ground!

🌸 Flowers, Fruit and Seeds
Flowers make seeds → Fruits protect seeds → Seeds become new plants!

Many plants have flowers. Flowers are the parts of plants that make seeds. Inside a flower is a sticky powder called pollen. Pollen helps flowers make seeds.

Plants that have flowers make fruit. Seeds grow inside the fruit. The fruit keeps seeds safe and helps them grow. Seeds can then grow into new plants!

🌸

Flowers

Parts of the plant that make seeds. They contain pollen.

✨

Pollen

A sticky powder inside flowers. Bees move pollen from flower to flower!

🍎

Fruit

Grows around the seeds to keep them safe. Apples are fruits!

🌰

Seeds

Grow inside fruit. Each seed can become a whole new plant!

🥦 Fun fact: We actually eat the flower of the broccoli plant! And bees are amazing helpers — they carry pollen from one flower to another.
🌰 How Do Seeds Look?
Seeds have many different shapes and sizes!

Seeds have many different shapes and sizes. But they all need the same things: light, water, and food to grow into new plants.

All seeds have covers that protect them and help keep them from drying out. Some seeds, like peanuts, even have shells!

🌼

Marigold Seeds

Small and thin seeds. They grow into beautiful yellow and orange flowers!

⭐

Star Anise Pod

A pod is a shell for seeds. The star anise pod has room for many seeds inside!

🥜

Peanuts

Peanuts are seeds! They grow underground. They have a hard shell and a thin dark cover.

🛡️ Seed Covers: Every seed has a cover (called a seed coat) that protects it and stops it from drying out. Some have extra shells too, like peanuts!
💨 How Do Seeds Move?
Seeds travel to new places in amazing ways!

Seeds need to move away from their parent plant to find space to grow. Animals, wind, and water all help seeds travel to new places!

🐿️

Animals

Squirrels bury seeds. Baboons eat fruit and leave seeds behind. Seeds stick to animal fur!

💨

Wind

Maple tree seeds spin like helicopters and fly far away in the wind!

🌊

Water

Seeds fall into rivers and oceans and are carried to new places!

🔄 The Plant Life Cycle
How plants grow, change, and make new plants!

A life cycle shows how a living thing grows, changes, and makes new living things. The plant life cycle begins with a seed and keeps going as plants make new plants.

Young plants look like their parent plants — same flower shapes, same leaf shapes. Some, like tulips, might look a little different (different colours!). Different plants have different life cycles — some live for a few weeks, others for many years!

1
🌰 Seed
The life cycle starts with a tiny seed. The seed has a protective cover.
2
🌱 Sprout / Germination
With water, light, and soil, the seed cracks open and a tiny shoot grows up!
3
🌿 Young Plant
The sprout grows bigger, growing leaves that use sunlight to make food.
4
🌸 Mature Plant with Flowers
The plant grows fully and makes flowers. Flowers make pollen to create new seeds!
5
🍎 Fruit and Seeds
Flowers turn into fruits with seeds inside. Seeds travel to new places and the cycle begins again!

Short Life Cycle

Some flowers complete their life cycle in just a few months!

🌲

Long Life Cycle

Redwood trees take more than two years just to make their cones!

🏡 Plants and Their Homes
Plants adapt to where they live!

Plants need light, water, and soil to grow. Different plants live in different places and have special features to help them survive!

🌴

Rainforest Plants

Have large leaves to take in lots of sunlight in the shady forest.

🌵

Desert Plants

Have few or no leaves. They store water in their thick stems!

🪷

Water Plants (Lily)

Roots in soil at pond bottom. Long stem reaches up. Leaf floats on top to get light!

🌿

Mimosa Plant

Closes its leaves when touched — this keeps animals from eating them!

🌞 Plants chase the light! Stems and leaves bend toward sunlight. Some flowers even turn to follow the sun as it moves across the sky! Strong winds can also change how a tree grows — bending it to one side.
