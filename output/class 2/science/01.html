Characteristics of Living Things

Unit 1 · Class 2 Science · Let's discover what makes something alive!

📚 What you will learn:

  • Understand what living things are with examples
  • Learn the six signs or characteristics of life
  • Tell the difference between living and non-living things
🌍
What Are Living Things?
Things that have life around us

There are many things around us. Some of them have life, such as birds, animals, plants, and humans. They are called living things.

The Six Signs of Life
What makes something living?

There are six signs of life that define living things. Let's learn them!

1
🍎
Need Food & Water
All living things eat food and drink water to survive and get energy.
2
🏃
Move
Living things move from one place to another to find food and shelter.
3
🌱
Grow
All living things grow — humans from babies, plants from seeds.
4
💨
Breathe
All living things breathe air to survive using lungs, gills, or stomata.
5
👁️
Have Senses
Living things react to changes. Humans use sight, hearing, taste, touch, smell.
6
🐣
Reproduce
All living things make more of their own kind — this is reproduction.
1
All Living Things Need Food and Water
Energy for life!

All living things eat food and drink water to survive. They get energy from food and water.

Humans get food from plants and animals. Animals get food from plants and other animals.

Plants Are Special!
Plants do not eat other living things. Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air.
2
All Living Things Move
Moving to find food and shelter

Living things move from one place to another to find food and shelter.

🐕
Dog runs with legs
🐟
Fish swims with fins
🦋
Butterfly flies with wings
🧑
Humans walk with legs
🌸
Plants Move Too!
Plants cannot move from place to place but they can move their parts — like the opening and closing of flowers!
3
All Living Things Grow
Getting bigger over time

All living things grow. Humans and animals are born as babies and grow as they get older. Plants grow from seeds in the ground.

⚠️
Did You Know?
Living things do not live forever. All humans, animals and plants die at some point.
4
All Living Things Breathe
Air is essential for life

All living things breathe air to survive. Different living things use different organs to breathe!

🫁
Humans
Breathe with Lungs
🐟
Fish
Breathe with Gills
🌿
Plants
Breathe through Stomata (tiny holes in leaves)
🤔
Critical Thinking
What do you think will happen to a plant if we forget to give it water for many days?
5
All Living Things Have Senses
Feeling the world around us

All living things react to changes around them. Plants can also feel changes — like how a sunflower turns toward the Sun!

Humans and animals use their five senses to understand and interact with their surroundings.
