📋 What is a Presentation?
Let's learn what a presentation is and what it contains.
A presentation is a document that has two or more slides. Each slide can contain text, images, videos, and music (sound). Microsoft PowerPoint 2019 is a popular presentation program.
📄

Presentation

A document with 2 or more slides shown on screen

🖼️

Slide

One page of a presentation with text, images, or more

💾

PowerPoint

Microsoft's presentation program (version 2019)

🎬

Slideshow

Playing your slides full screen one after another

👀 What can a slide contain?
📝
Text on a Slide
You can type titles, subtitles, and any words on a slide!
🖥️ Opening PowerPoint 2019
There are 3 ways to open PowerPoint. Let's learn them all!
1

Desktop Icon

🖥️

Double-click the PowerPoint icon on your desktop to open the program.

2

Taskbar Icon

📌

Click the PowerPoint icon pinned to the taskbar at the bottom of the screen.

3

Start Button

🪟

Click the Start Button, scroll to find PowerPoint in the menu, then click it!

🎯 After opening PowerPoint, you will see the welcome screen. Click on "Blank Presentation" to start a new, empty slideshow!

Follow these steps to add text to your first slide!
1

Open PowerPoint

Blank Presentation

▶️ View a Slideshow
There are 3 ways to play your slideshow full screen!
🗂️

Method 1

Click the "Slide Show" tab at the top → Click "From Beginning"

🖱️

Method 2

Click the Slide Show icon (📽️) at the bottom right corner of the screen

⌨️

Method 3

Press the F5 key on your keyboard to start the slideshow instantly!

🔙 To go back to PowerPoint: Press the Enter Key once — you'll see "End of slide show, click to exit." Press Enter one more time to return to the program.
