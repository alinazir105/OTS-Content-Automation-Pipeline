The Desktop

The desktop is the main screen on your computer where you can see and open your programs, files, and tools. It has pictures called icons that let you quickly start things, a bar at the bottom called the taskbar to manage and open programs, and a menu called the Start Menu to find and start new programs.

Drives

A drive is a storage location on a computer where files and folders are kept. There are different types of drives for storing your work.

🖥️

Local Drive

Stores files on the computer itself. It is built inside your computer.

💿

DVD RW Drive

Used for reading and writing CDs and DVDs.

🔌

Removable Drive

External storage, like a USB drive, that can be connected and removed easily.

Creating a Folder on the Desktop

Follow these steps to create a new folder on your computer desktop!

1
Right-click on a blank space on the desktop. A menu will appear!
2
Click on New in the menu that appears.
3
Click on Folder from the options.
4
A new folder will appear on the desktop. Click on the desktop OR press Enter on the keyboard to confirm.

Searching for Files and Folders

Sometimes you cannot find your file! Do not worry. You can use the Search button to find it quickly.

1
Click on the Start button (⊞) at the bottom of the screen.
2
Type the name of the file or folder you are searching for in the search box.
3
For example, type "cliparts" in the search box to find a file named cliparts.
4
Click on the file name in the results to open it.
