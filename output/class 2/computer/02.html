What is a Computer?

Definition

A computer is an electronic machine. It can store, organize and find information, do calculations and control other machines. Computers are available in different shapes and sizes.

Advantages of Using Computers

3 Key Advantages
1
⚡ Faster
Computers enable faster communication, like sending emails, compared to traditional mail. What used to take days now takes seconds!
2
🎥 Audio & Visuals
Computers support video calls and online meetings, allowing visual and audio interaction. You can see and hear people far away!
3
📄 Less Paper Work
Digital documents reduce the need for paper, making processes more environmentally friendly. We save trees by using computers!
