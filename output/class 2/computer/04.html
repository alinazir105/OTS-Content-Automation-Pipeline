What is MS Paint?

MS Paint is a fun and easy-to-use computer program that helps you draw and colour pictures. With MS Paint, you can use different tools to make shapes, add colours, and write words on your drawings.

It's like having a digital drawing pad where you can create your own art and make simple changes to pictures!

Lab Activity — Let's Draw in MS Paint!

Follow these steps to complete your lab activity in MS Paint. You will draw objects using different shapes and colour them!

1
Open MS Paint on your computer.
2
Select Your Shapes: Use the Shapes Toolbox to select basic shapes like the Oval, Rectangle, Triangle, and Line tools.
3
Create Your Object: Follow the instructions to draw each object. Combine different shapes to represent different parts of each object.
4
Add Colours: Use the Fill with Colour tool to colour each part of your drawing.
5
Save Your Work: Save each drawing with a different name (e.g., "My Clock," "My Ice-cream," etc.).
