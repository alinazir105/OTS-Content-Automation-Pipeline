What is Volume & Capacity?

📦 Volume and Capacity

Volume and Capacity measure how much liquid a container can hold.

  • Millilitres (mℓ) — used for small quantities (medicine, juice box)
  • Litres (ℓ) — used for larger quantities (water bottle, bucket)
  • Kilolitres (kℓ) — used for very large quantities (water tank, swimming pool)
🔑 Key Fact: 1 litre (ℓ) = 1,000 millilitres (mℓ)

💡 Quick Tips

  • Moving decimal 3 places left = ÷ 1000
  • When adding: carry over if mℓ ≥ 1000
  • When subtracting: borrow 1 ℓ = 1000 mℓ

Addition of Volume

Adding Volumes

1Write litres and millilitres in separate columns
2Add the millilitres first
3If total mℓ ≥ 1,000 → carry over 1 ℓ (subtract 1,000 from mℓ, add 1 to ℓ)
4Add the litres column (include any carry)

🔍 Worked Example: 17 ℓ 826 mℓ + 23 ℓ 578 mℓ

Step 1: Write in columns — ℓ | mℓ
   17 | 826
+ 23 | 578
Step 2: Add mℓ: 826 + 578 = 1,404 mℓ
Step 3: 1,404 ≥ 1,000 → carry 1 ℓ; remaining mℓ = 1,404 − 1,000 = 404 mℓ
Step 4: Add ℓ: 17 + 23 + 1 (carry) = 41 ℓ
✅ Answer: 41 ℓ 404 mℓ

Subtraction of Volume

Subtracting Volumes

1Write litres and millilitres in separate columns
2Subtract millilitres first
3If top mℓ < bottom mℓ → borrow 1 ℓ (add 1,000 to mℓ, reduce ℓ by 1)
4Subtract the litres column

🔍 Worked Example: 50 ℓ 100 mℓ − 25 ℓ 700 mℓ

Step 1: Write in columns — ℓ | mℓ
   50 | 100
− 25 | 700
Step 2: Subtract mℓ: 100 − 700 → not possible! Need to borrow.
Step 3: Borrow 1 ℓ from 50 → now 49 ℓ, and mℓ = 100 + 1,000 = 1,100 mℓ
Step 4: 1,100 − 700 = 400 mℓ
Step 5: Subtract ℓ: 49 − 25 = 24 ℓ
✅ Answer: 24 ℓ 400 mℓ

Word Problems

Solving Word Problems

Q1: Jug A has 45 ℓ 670 mℓ of orange juice, and Jug B has 67 ℓ 890 mℓ of apple juice. What is the total juice?
Add: 45ℓ 670mℓ + 67ℓ 890mℓ
mℓ: 670 + 890 = 1,560 → carry 1ℓ, remaining = 560mℓ
ℓ: 45 + 67 + 1 = 113ℓ
✅ Total = 113 ℓ 560 mℓ
Q2: Arif sells 35 ℓ 800 mℓ of milk in the morning and 24 ℓ 500 mℓ in the evening. How much milk does he sell in a day?
Add: 35ℓ 800mℓ + 24ℓ 500mℓ
mℓ: 800 + 500 = 1,300 → carry 1ℓ, remaining = 300mℓ
ℓ: 35 + 24 + 1 = 60ℓ
✅ Total milk sold = 60 ℓ 300 mℓ
Q3: Sara buys 500 mℓ of mustard oil, 250 mℓ of coconut oil, and 2 ℓ of refined oil. What is the total quantity of oil?
Convert refined oil: 2 ℓ = 2,000 mℓ
Add all mℓ: 500 + 250 + 2,000 = 2,750 mℓ
Convert to litres: 2,750 ÷ 1,000 = 2.75 ℓ
✅ Total oil = 2 ℓ 750 mℓ
Q4: A tank contains 478 ℓ 360 mℓ of water. After using 239 ℓ 125 mℓ. How much water is left?
Subtract: 478ℓ 360mℓ − 239ℓ 125mℓ
mℓ: 360 − 125 = 235mℓ (no borrowing needed)
ℓ: 478 − 239 = 239ℓ
✅ Water left = 239 ℓ 235 mℓ
Q5: Maria has 2 ℓ of oil. How many 250 mℓ bottles can she fill?
Convert: 2 ℓ = 2,000 mℓ
Divide: 2,000 ÷ 250 = 8 bottles
✅ Maria can fill 8 bottles of 250 mℓ each
Q6: A bucket holds 25 ℓ 750 mℓ of water. Another bucket has 13 ℓ 480 mℓ. How much more water does the first bucket hold?
Subtract: 25ℓ 750mℓ − 13ℓ 480mℓ
mℓ: 750 − 480 = 270mℓ
ℓ: 25 − 13 = 12ℓ
✅ First bucket holds 12 ℓ 270 mℓ more
