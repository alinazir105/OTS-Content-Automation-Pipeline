Place Value Charts

Every digit in a number has a special position called its place value. The value depends on where the digit sits.

🔑 Key Concepts
📍
Place Value: The value of a digit based on its position in a number.
🔢
Digit: Any single numeral from 0 to 9.
🗂️
Period: A group of digits separated by commas (Ones, Thousands, Lakhs, etc.)
🇵🇰
Pakistani System: Uses Ones → Thousands → Lakhs → Crores periods.
🌍
International System: Uses Ones → Thousands → Millions → Billions periods.
📖
Expanded Form: Writing a number as the sum of place values.

Expanded Form

Break a number into the sum of each digit's place value. This helps you understand what each digit is worth!

✏️ Find the expanded form of 478,923

Step 1: Identify each digit's position

Write each digit under its place value column: 4 (Hundred Thousands), 7 (Ten Thousands), 8 (Thousands), 9 (Hundreds), 2 (Tens), 3 (Ones)

Step 2: Multiply each digit by its place value

4 × 100,000 = 400,000  |  7 × 10,000 = 70,000  |  8 × 1,000 = 8,000

9 × 100 = 900  |  2 × 10 = 20  |  3 × 1 = 3

Step 3: Write the expanded form

478,923 = 400,000 + 70,000 + 8,000 + 900 + 20 + 3

✅ Final Answer:

478,923 = 400,000 + 70,000 + 8,000 + 900 + 20 + 3

📌 Expanded Form Rules
Formula: digit × place_value   Example: 654,321 6 × 100,000 = 600,000 5 × 10,000 = 50,000 4 × 1,000 = 4,000 3 × 100 = 300 2 × 10 = 20 1 × 1 = 1   = 600,000 + 50,000 + 4,000 + 300 + 20 + 1
⚠️ Watch Out!
If a digit is 0, its place value contribution is also 0. You may skip it or write it as 0.

E.g. 903,218 → the Tens Thousands place is 0, so: 900,000 + 0 + 3,000 + 200 + 10 + 8

Comparing 6-Digit Numbers

To compare numbers, start from the leftmost (highest) digit and work right until you find a difference.

📏 Rules for Comparison

1
Check from Left to Right: Start comparing the leftmost (highest place value) digit first.
2
Different digits found? The number with the bigger digit at that position is GREATER.
Example: 308,990 > 299,789 because 3 > 2
3
Equal digits? Move one position to the right and compare again.
Example: 786,995 < 790,012 because 8 < 9 (in ten-thousands)
4
Continue until you find a difference. The number with the higher digit first is greater.
