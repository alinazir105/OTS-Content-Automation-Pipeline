Units of Time

Units & Conversions

We measure time in seconds, minutes, and hours. Knowing how to convert between them is essential!

📏 Key Relationships

  • 1 minute = 60 seconds
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes
  • 1 hour = 3,600 seconds
Conversion Formulas
To convert minutes → seconds: multiply by 60 To convert seconds → minutes: divide by 60 To convert hours → minutes: multiply by 60 To convert minutes → hours: divide by 60

Time Arithmetic

Adding & Subtracting Time

Use these rules to add or subtract time correctly — including carrying and borrowing!

⚙️ Time Arithmetic Rules
1
Add or subtract hours, minutes and seconds separately.
2
If seconds exceed 60: carry 1 minute, subtract 60 from seconds.
3
If minutes exceed 60: carry 1 hour, subtract 60 from minutes.
4
For subtraction, borrow from the higher unit if needed.

📚 Worked Example: Adding Time

Step by Step
You start studying at 4:30 PM and study for 2 hours and 15 minutes. When will you finish?
Step 1 — Identify
Start time: 4:30 PM | Duration: 2 hours 15 minutes
Step 2 — Add Hours
4:30 PM + 2 hours = 6:30 PM
Step 3 — Add Minutes
6:30 PM + 15 minutes = 6:45 PM
Step 4 — Check
Minutes 45 < 60, so no carry needed. ✓
✅ Finish Time: 6:45 PM

📚 Worked Example: Finding Duration

Step by Step
Ali started homework at 5:30 PM and finished at 6:45 PM. How long did he spend?
Step 1 — Identify
End: 6:45 PM | Start: 5:30 PM. We subtract to find duration.
Step 2 — Subtract Minutes
45 − 30 = 15 minutes
Step 3 — Subtract Hours
6 − 5 = 1 hour
Step 4 — Combine
Duration = 1 hour and 15 minutes
✅ Ali spent 1 hour and 15 minutes on homework.
