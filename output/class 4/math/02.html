Understanding Addition

Addition means combining two or more numbers to find a total (sum).

Key Vocabulary
• Addend: Each number being added
• Sum: The result of addition
• Carry: When a column adds up to 10 or more, we carry to the next column

When adding large numbers, always line up the digits by place value — ones under ones, tens under tens, hundreds under hundreds, and so on.

Golden Rule: Always add from RIGHT to LEFT (ones → tens → hundreds → thousands → …)

🔢 Worked Example: Addition of 5-Digit Numbers

Find the sum of 25,416 + 10,934

✅ Final Answer: 25,416 + 10,934 = 36,350

Understanding Subtraction

Subtraction means taking one number away from another to find the difference.

Key Vocabulary
• Minuend: The number you subtract FROM
• Subtrahend: The number being subtracted
• Difference: The result of subtraction
• Borrowing/Regrouping: When a digit is too small, we borrow from the next column

🔢 Worked Example: Subtraction of 6-Digit Numbers

Find: 7,25,035 − 5,62,283

✅ Final Answer: 7,25,035 − 5,62,283 = 1,62,752

📌 Quick Facts

  • Always align digits by place value
  • Add/subtract from right to left
  • Carry when sum ≥ 10
  • Borrow when top digit is smaller
  • Check: use inverse operation!
