Core Concepts

What is an Angle?

📌 Definition of Angle

Two rays having a common endpoint are called an angle. The common endpoint is called the vertex, and the two rays are called the arms.

An angle formed by two rays sharing a common vertex point A

✏️ How to Read & Write an Angle

Read: angle A  |  angle CAB  |  angle BAC

Write: ∠A  or  ∠CAB  or  ∠BAC

The vertex letter always goes in the middle when using three letters!

Key Geometry Definitions

📏 Length

The measurement or extent of something from end to end.

📍 Vertex

A vertex is a point where two straight lines or rays meet.

〰️ Arms

The two sides of the angle, joined at a common endpoint (the vertex).

⬜ Right Angle

When two straight lines intersect each other at 90° or are perpendicular to each other at the intersection.

🎯 Naming Angles — Quick Rule

An angle is named using the vertex and the two arms. For angle with vertex Q and arms going to P and R: write ∠PQR or ∠RQP or simply ∠Q.

Types of Angles

Four Types of Angles

⬜ Right Angle

90°

When two lines are perpendicular. Shown with a small square at the vertex.

📐 Acute Angle

0° – 89°

Smaller than a right angle. A sharp, pointy angle.

↗️ Obtuse Angle

91° – 179°

Larger than a right angle but smaller than a straight angle.

↔️ Straight Angle

180°

Forms a straight line. Both arms point in opposite directions.

Counting Angles

How Many Angles in a Figure?

🔢 Formula for Counting Angles

When n rays share the same vertex, the total number of angles formed is:
Total Angles = n × (n–1) ÷ 2

For 4 rays: 4 × 3 ÷ 2 = 6 angles

📖 Guided Example: 4 Rays from Point A

Rays AB, AC, AD, AE all start from point A. How many angles are formed?

Step 1: Count the rays

We have 4 rays from vertex A: AB, AC, AD, AE  →  n = 4

Step 2: List adjacent angles

∠BAC, ∠CAD, ∠DAE — these are the 3 basic adjacent angles.

Step 3: List combined angles

∠BAD = ∠BAC + ∠CAD
∠BAE = ∠BAC + ∠CAD + ∠DAE
∠CAE = ∠CAD + ∠DAE
→ 3 more angles!

Step 4: Total count

3 adjacent + 3 combined = 6 angles total ✓
Using formula: 4 × 3 ÷ 2 = 6 ✓

Step 5: Name all 6 angles

∠BAC   ∠CAD   ∠DAE   ∠BAD   ∠CAE   ∠BAE

4 rays from vertex A forming 6 angles: ∠BAC, ∠CAD, ∠DAE, ∠BAD, ∠CAE, ∠BAE
