Section 1

Decimal Fractions

Decimal fractions are fractions where the denominator is a power of 10, like 10, 100, or 1000. Instead of writing these fractions as numbers over another number, we can use decimals.

Key Idea: A decimal fraction uses a decimal point (·) to separate the whole number part from the fractional part. Everything to the right of the decimal point is less than 1.

Examples

1️⃣

1/10 = 0.1

Zero point one
First decimal place

2️⃣

3/100 = 0.03

Zero point zero three
Second decimal place

3️⃣

5/1000 = 0.005

Zero point zero zero five
Third decimal place

Section 2

Tenths, Hundredths, Thousandths & Place Value

Decimals have place values just like whole numbers. Each place after the decimal point represents a fraction of a whole.

🟢

Tenths (0.1)

The 1st digit to the right of the decimal point.

Example: 0.3 = 3 tenths

🟠

Hundredths (0.01)

The 2nd digit to the right of the decimal point.

Example: 0.05 = 5 hundredths

🔵

Thousandths (0.001)

The 3rd digit to the right of the decimal point.

Example: 0.007 = 7 thousandths

Section 3

Mixed Fractions & Decimals

A mixed fraction combines a whole number and a fraction. We can convert it to a decimal by writing the fraction as a decimal.

Example: 2 and 3/10 = 2.3  |  The whole number stays, the fraction becomes the decimal part.

🔍 Guided Example: Mixed Fraction to Decimal

Convert 5 and 4/10 to a decimal.

Step 1
Identify the whole number part.
Whole number = 5
Step 2
Convert the fraction 4/10 to a decimal.
4/10 = 0.4 (one decimal place)
Step 3
Combine: whole number + decimal fraction
5 + 0.4 = 5.4
✅ Answer
5 and 4/10 as a decimal is:
5 4/10 = 5.4

Section 4

Adding & Subtracting Decimals

When adding or subtracting decimals, always align the decimal points vertically before performing the calculations.

Golden Rule: Line up the decimal points! Then add or subtract each column from right to left, carrying or borrowing as needed.

🔍 Guided Example: Adding Decimals

Solve: 5.25 + 3.75

Step 1
Write both numbers with decimal points aligned.
5 . 2 5
+ 3 . 7 5
Step 2
Add the hundredths column: 5 + 5 = 10. Write 0, carry 1.
5+5 = 10 → write 0, carry 1
Step 3
Add the tenths column: 2 + 7 + 1(carry) = 10. Write 0, carry 1.
2+7+1 = 10 → write 0, carry 1
Step 4
Add units: 5 + 3 + 1(carry) = 9. Write the decimal point.
5+3+1 = 9 → 9.00
✅ Answer
Final answer:
5.25 + 3.75 = 9.00

🔍 Guided Example: Subtracting Decimals

Solve: 10.6 − 2.34

Step 1
Write both numbers aligned, add a zero to 10.6 → 10.60
10 . 6 0
- 2 . 3 4
Step 2
Subtract hundredths: 0 − 4. Can't do it, borrow from tenths.
10 − 4 = 6 → write 6
Step 3
Subtract tenths: 6 − 1(borrowed) − 3 = 2. Write 2.
6 − 1 − 3 = 2
✅ Answer
Final answer:
10.60 − 2.34 = 8.26
