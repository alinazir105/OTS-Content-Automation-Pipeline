Introduction

What is MS PowerPoint?

MS PowerPoint is a powerful tool used to create presentations. A presentation is made up of multiple slides, where each slide can contain text, images, animations, and more.

Presentations are used for delivering information visually in schools, businesses, or events. Each slide acts like a page — you can add text, pictures, shapes, audio, and video.

📌 Key Definition A Presentation is a collection of slides created in MS PowerPoint. Each slide can hold different types of content like text, pictures, shapes, audio, and animations.
📄

Slides

Individual pages in a presentation. Each can have different layouts and content.

🖼️

Placeholders

Pre-defined text boxes on a slide for titles, subtitles, and content areas.

🎨

Design Themes

Ready-made visual styles that give your presentation a professional look instantly.

✨

Animations

Special effects applied to text or objects on a slide to make them move or appear.

Creating

How to Create a Presentation

Follow these steps carefully to build a complete presentation in MS PowerPoint from scratch.

Step 1
▶ Open MS PowerPoint

Click the Start menu or search for "PowerPoint" in the search bar. Click the PowerPoint App to open it. A blank presentation will appear on screen.

Step 2
▶ Create a New Slide

Click on "New Slide" in the Home tab toolbar to add a blank slide to your presentation. You can choose from different layouts like Title Slide, Title and Content, Blank, etc.

Step 3
▶ Add a Title and Text

Each slide has different placeholders for titles and text. Click on the text box that says "Click to add title" to write your slide title. Click "Click to add text" for body content.

Step 4
▶ Insert Images, Videos, and Shapes

Click on the "Insert" tab in the ribbon. From here you can add: Pictures (from file or online), Shapes (rectangles, circles, arrows), Videos and Audio files, Charts and SmartArt diagrams.

Step 5
▶ Design and Layout

Click on the "Design" tab to choose a theme. The Layout option (in Home tab) lets you change how content is arranged on each slide — try "Two Content", "Picture with Caption", etc.

Step 6
▶ Save Your Presentation

Click the "File" tab → "Save As" → choose a location and file name. PowerPoint files are saved as .pptx by default. Use Ctrl+S anytime to save quickly.

Step 7
▶ Run the Slideshow

Click the "Slideshow" tab → "From Beginning" to start the presentation from the first slide. You can also press the shortcut key F5 to start from the beginning instantly.

Transitions

Adding Transitions Between Slides

Transitions are visual effects that appear when you move from one slide to the next. They make your presentation look smooth and professional.

📌 What are Transitions? Transitions are animations that play when one slide changes to the next. They help guide the audience's attention and make the slideshow feel polished.

Popular Transition Effects

Fade
Push
Wipe
Split
Reveal
Morph
Random

🌊 Morph

Smoothly animates changes between two slides with similar layout elements. Great for zoom effects.

➡️ Push

Moves the old slide out as the new slide pushes in from a direction you choose.

🧹 Wipe

The new slide wipes over the old one from a specified direction — top, bottom, left, or right.

🌫️ Fade

The current slide fades out and the new one fades in — elegant and professional.

Customizing Transitions

After selecting a transition, you can customize it using options in the Transitions tab:

⚙️ Effect Options Change how the transition occurs — for example, the direction of a Push (from left, right, top, or bottom) or a Wipe.
⏱️ Duration Adjust how long the transition takes. A shorter duration (0.5s) feels snappy, while a longer one (2–3s) feels dramatic.
🔊 Sound Add a sound effect that plays during the transition — use sparingly to keep it professional.
📋 Apply To All Apply the selected transition to every slide in your presentation with one click.

🎯 Guided Example: Adding a Fade Transition

STEP 1 OF 5

Open your PowerPoint presentation. In the left panel, click on the slide you want to add a transition to (e.g., Slide 2).

STEP 2 OF 5

Click on the Transitions tab in the top ribbon. You will see a gallery of transition effects appear.

STEP 3 OF 5

From the gallery, click on "Fade". You will immediately see a preview of the fade effect on your slide.

STEP 4 OF 5

In the "Timing" group on the right, set the Duration to 01.00 second. You can also add a sound if you want.

STEP 5 OF 5 ✅

Click "Apply To All" to apply the Fade transition to every slide. Press F5 to run the slideshow and see the effect!

Animations

Adding Animations to Objects

Animations are effects applied to individual objects (text boxes, images, shapes) on a slide. They control how objects appear, disappear, or move during the slideshow.

📌 Transitions vs Animations Transitions = effects between slides. Animations = effects on objects within a single slide. Both are found in separate tabs in the ribbon.

Animation Categories

🟢

Entrance Effects

Bring objects onto the slide. Examples: Fade, Fly In, Float In, Appear, Bounce, Zoom.

🟡

Emphasis Effects

Highlight objects that are already visible. Examples: Pulse, Spin, Grow/Shrink, Color Pulse, Underline.

🔴

Exit Effects

Remove objects from the slide. Examples: Fade Out, Fly Out, Disappear, Shrink and Turn.

🔵

Motion Paths

Create a custom path that the object follows on the slide — Lines, Arcs, Turns, Loops, Custom Path.

Customizing Animations

⚙️ Effect Options

Modify how the animation behaves — change direction (e.g., Fly In from left), intensity, sequence.

▶ Start

On Click — starts when you click. With Previous — plays with the previous animation. After Previous — plays after it finishes.

⏱️ Duration

Adjust how long the animation takes to complete. Shorter = faster, Longer = slower and more dramatic.

⏳ Delay

Set a delay (in seconds) before the animation starts after it is triggered. Useful for sequencing effects.

🗂️ Animation Pane Open the Animation Pane from the Animations tab to see a list of ALL animations on the current slide. You can reorder, modify, or remove animations here. Use "Move Earlier" and "Move Later" to change the order.
Best Practices

Tips for Transitions & Animations

💼

Keep it Professional

Overusing animations and transitions can distract from your message. Use them to enhance your presentation, not overshadow it.

👁️

Test Your Presentation

Always preview your slides with transitions and animations to ensure they function as expected and look professional.

🎨

Use Consistent Themes

Apply the same transition style throughout your presentation for a cohesive, polished look.

🔤

Readable Text First

Make sure your text is large enough and contrasts well with the background before adding effects.
