📧 What is Email?

Electronic Mail

Email, which stands for electronic mail, is a method of exchanging digital messages over the internet. It allows users to send and receive messages, documents, images, and other files quickly and efficiently.

Email has become an essential communication tool in both personal and professional contexts, enabling instant communication across the globe.

💡 Key Definition

Email = Electronic Mail. With an email account, users can send messages to one or multiple recipients, organize their messages in folders, and access their email from various devices such as computers, tablets, and smartphones.

💻

Computers

Access your email from any desktop or laptop browser using Gmail.

📱

Smartphones & Tablets

Check and send emails on the go from your mobile device.

🌍

Global Communication

Send messages instantly to anyone, anywhere in the world.

📂

File Sharing

Attach and share documents, images, and other files easily.

🔧 Step by Step

How to Create a Gmail Account

Creating a Gmail account is simple and can be done in a few easy steps. Let's walk through the process together.

🛠️

Guided Walkthrough: Creating Your Gmail Account

Step 1 of 9
Visit the Gmail Website: Open your web browser and go to www.gmail.com. This is where you will set up your new account.
Step 2 of 9
Click on "Create Account": On the Gmail page, you will see the option "Create account." Click on it to begin the registration process.
Step 3 of 9
Fill in Your Information: Enter your first and last name. Then create a username that will be part of your email address (e.g., username@gmail.com).
Step 4 of 9
Create a Password: Choose a strong password using letters, numbers, and symbols. Confirm it by typing it again in the designated field.
Step 5 of 9
Complete the Form: Fill in required fields including your phone number, recovery email address, date of birth, and gender. Your phone number is essential for account recovery and security.
Step 6 of 9
Verify Your Phone Number: Google will send you a verification code via SMS to your phone. Enter the code to confirm your phone number.
Step 7 of 9
Agree to Terms and Conditions: Read through Google's privacy policy and terms of service. Click "I agree" to accept them and continue.
Step 8 of 9
Set Up Your Profile: You can optionally add a profile picture and complete additional settings. This step is optional but helps personalize your account.
Step 9 of 9
🎉 Your Gmail Account is Ready! Once you finish the setup, you will be taken to your new Gmail inbox. You can now start sending and receiving emails!

📥 Inbox & Compose

Checking & Sending Emails

Checking Email in Your Inbox

Once your Gmail account is ready, follow these steps to check your email:

Checking Email Steps
1
Log In: Go to www.gmail.com and enter your email address and password.
2
Access Inbox: After logging in you will see your inbox displaying all received emails. Newest emails appear at the top.
3
Identify Unread Emails: Unread emails appear in bold text, making them easy to spot. Click any email to read it.
📌 Remember

Unread emails are shown in bold in your Gmail inbox. Once you click and read them, they become normal (not bold).

Composing and Sending an Email

To write and send a new email in Gmail:

Composing & Sending Steps
1
Click "Compose": Find the Compose button in the upper left corner of your Gmail inbox.
2
Enter Recipient: In the "To" field, type the email address of the person you want to send your message to.
3
Add a Subject: In the "Subject" line, give a brief summary of your email's content.
4
Write Your Message: In the main text area, type your message.
5
Send the Email: Click the "Send" button at the bottom of the compose window.

👥 Multiple Recipients

Using CC and BCC

When you want to send an email to more than one person, Gmail provides two special fields: CC and BCC.

Feature CC (Carbon Copy) BCC (Blind Carbon Copy)
What it does Sends a copy of the email to others Sends a copy privately without revealing addresses
Visibility All recipients can see who was CC'd No one can see who was BCC'd
When to use When recipients can know about each other When you want privacy for recipients
Example Copying your teacher on a group project email Sending a newsletter without sharing everyone's address
👥

CC — Carbon Copy

The "CC" field sends a copy of the email and shows the email address to all recipients. Everyone can see who received the email.

🤫

BCC — Blind Carbon Copy

The "BCC" field sends a copy of the email to others without revealing their email addresses to anyone else. It's completely private.

📎 Attachments

Attaching a File to Your Email

You can send files like documents, images, and presentations along with your email. Here's how to attach a file in Gmail:

📎 Steps to Attach a File
1
Compose a New Email: Click on "Compose" to create a new email message.
2
Click the Paperclip Icon: At the bottom of the compose window, click the 📎 paperclip icon to attach files.
3
Select the File: A file browser window will open. Navigate to your file, select it, and click "Open."
4
Confirm the Attachment: Once uploaded, you will see the file name displayed below the subject line.
5
Finish Composing: Add recipients, a subject line, and message content as needed.
6
Send the Email: Click "Send" to deliver your email along with the attached file.
📎 The Paperclip Icon

The paperclip icon (📎) is the universal symbol used in email clients to attach files. Always check that your file has uploaded successfully before clicking Send!
