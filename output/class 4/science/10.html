Unit 01

Animal Classification

Animals can be classified into groups based on their features. Here are the key terms you need to know.

  • AnimalA living thing that moves and grows.
  • BackboneThe bones in the back that support the body.
  • FurSoft hair on some animal's bodies.
  • Warm-bloodedAnimals that keep their body warm all the time.
  • Cold-bloodedAnimals whose body temperature changes with the weather.
  • ScalesSmall, hard skin covering some animals like fish and snakes.
  • AdultA fully grown animal or person.
  • StingsA sharp pain caused by an insect or plant.

Unit 02

Life Cycles of a Butterfly and a Frog

Life cycles show how living things grow and change from birth to adult. Let's explore the butterfly and frog life cycles.

🦋 Butterfly Life Cycle
  1. Egg — laid on a leaf
  2. Larva (Caterpillar) — hatches, eats leaves
  3. Chrysalis (Pupa) — hard protective shell
  4. Adult Butterfly — emerges, flies, lays eggs
🐸 Frog Life Cycle
  1. Frogspawn — jelly-like eggs in water
  2. Tadpole — swims, breathes with gills
  3. Froglet — growing legs, still has tail
  4. Adult Frog — lives on land and water
  • LarvaA young insect that looks different from an adult.
  • ChrysalisThe hard shell where a caterpillar changes into a butterfly.
  • CaterpillarA small, worm-like insect that becomes a butterfly or moth.
  • FrogspawnA group of jelly-like eggs laid by a frog in water.

Unit 03

The Human Body

The human body is made up of many parts that work together to keep us alive and healthy.

  • Blood cellsTiny parts in blood that carry oxygen and fight germs.
  • MusclesBody parts that help us move and stay strong.
  • OrgansImportant body parts inside us, like the heart and lungs.
  • WindpipeThe tube that carries air to the lungs.
🫀 Key Organs
  • Heart — pumps blood around the body
  • Lungs — take in oxygen, release carbon dioxide
  • Brain — controls everything we do
  • Stomach — digests food
// How we breathe — step by step
1. Air enters → Nose/Mouth
2. Travels down → Windpipe
3. Reaches → Lungs
4. Oxygen → Blood cells
5. CO₂ → Exhaled out

Unit 04

Layers of Earth

The Earth is made of several layers. Each layer has unique properties and affects life on the surface.

  • EarthquakesThe shaking of the ground.
  • Volcanic eruptionsWhen a volcano throws out hot lava and smoke.
  • Molten metalMetal that is melted by heat.
  • GraniteA hard rock found in mountains.
  • Tectonic platesHuge pieces of Earth's crust that move slowly.
  • Ocean floorThe bottom part of the sea.
  • Solar radiationHeat and light coming from the Sun.

⚠️ Natural Disasters Linked to Tectonic Plates

When tectonic plates move and collide, they can cause earthquakes (ground shaking) and volcanic eruptions (lava escaping). This is why some regions experience more natural disasters than others.

Unit 05

Rocks

Rocks are classified into three main types based on how they are formed. Understanding rocks helps us learn about Earth's history.

🌋
Type 1

Igneous Rocks

Formed from cooled magma or lava. They are hard and crystalline.

Magma / LavaHot, melted rock from inside the Earth.
🏔️
Type 2

Sedimentary Rocks

Made from layers of sand, mud, and organic material cemented together over time.

FossilsOld remains of plants or animals found in rocks.
💎
Type 3

Metamorphic Rocks

Rocks that change form due to heat and pressure deep underground.

Metamorphic RocksRocks that change form due to heat and pressure.
  • UnderwaterSomething that is below the water.
  • Organic materialParts of living things, like plants and animals.
  • CementedStuck or glued together tightly.

Unit 06

Soil

Soil is more than just dirt — it's a complex mixture that supports all plant life on Earth!

  • Dead plantsPlants that are no longer alive.
  • Decayed plantsPlants that have rotted and broken down.
  • EarthwormsSmall worms that live in the soil and help it stay healthy.
  • Fertile soilSoil that is rich in nutrients and good for growing plants.
  • SiltVery tiny pieces of rock and soil that make the land soft.
🌱 What Makes Soil Fertile?

Fertile soil contains a mix of:

  • Decayed plants and animals (humus)
  • Tiny rock particles (sand, silt, clay)
  • Water and air spaces
  • Earthworms and other living things
// How earthworms help soil:
🪱 Burrow → create air spaces
🪱 Eat → break down dead plants
🪱 Excrete → add nutrients to soil
Result → richer, fertile soil!

Unit 07

Electricity and Magnetism

Electricity powers our world, while magnetism is a force that attracts certain materials.

  • ElectricityEnergy that makes lights and machines work.
  • MagnetismA force that pulls metal objects together.
  • CozyWarm, comfortable, and nice.
  • MetalsHard, shiny materials used to make things like coins and tools.
⚡ Electrical Circuit Basics

A simple circuit needs:

  • Battery — provides electrical energy
  • Wire — carries electricity
  • Bulb/Load — uses electricity
  • Switch — opens or closes the circuit
🧲 Magnetic Metals

Magnets attract metals like iron, steel, nickel and cobalt. They do NOT attract gold, silver, copper, or aluminium.

Unit 08

Mixtures and Solutions

Understanding how substances mix together — and how to separate them — is a key science skill!

  • MixtureTwo or more things mixed together but can be separated.
  • SolutionA mixture where one thing dissolves in another.
  • SoluteThe thing that dissolves in a solution, like sugar in water.
  • SolventThe liquid that dissolves something, like water for sugar.
🧪 Solution Formula

Solute + Solvent = Solution

Example: Sugar (solute) + Water (solvent) = Sugar solution

// Difference: Mixture vs Solution
Mixture: parts visible, CAN separate
Example: sand + water
Solution: parts invisible, DISSOLVED
Example: salt + water

The Solar System

Our solar system contains the Sun, eight planets, and many other objects including asteroids and comets.

  • PlanetsLarge round objects that move around the Sun.
  • AsteroidsSmall rocky objects that move in space.
  • CometsBright space objects with tails made of ice and dust.
🪐 8 Planets (in order from Sun)
  1. Mercury ☿
  2. Venus ♀
  3. Earth 🌍
  4. Mars ♂
  5. Jupiter ♃
  6. Saturn ♄ (with rings)
  7. Uranus ♅
  8. Neptune ♆

📖 Guided Example: Identifying Rock Types

Follow the steps to identify which type of rock is which — click "Next Step" to reveal each step.

Step 1 — Question

A rock was found near a volcano. It is very hard and sparkly. What type of rock is it?

Step 2 — Think

Near a volcano → magma or lava would have cooled. When lava cools, it forms a specific type of rock.

Step 3 — Identify

Cooled magma/lava = IGNEOUS ROCK. Examples include granite (forms deep underground) and basalt (forms from surface lava).

Step 4 — Answer

✅ The rock is an Igneous rock. It formed when hot magma from the volcano cooled and solidified into a hard, crystalline rock.
