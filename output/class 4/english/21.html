Passage 21.1
National Heroes of Pakistan

National heroes are people who work hard, make sacrifices, and inspire others to build a better nation. Pakistan has many heroes who have played a significant role in its history and development.

Born: December 25, 1876  |  Died: September 11, 1948

🇵🇰 Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah

Muhammad Ali Jinnah, known as Quaid-e-Azam (the Great Leader), is the founder of Pakistan. He worked tirelessly for the independence of Muslims in the Indian subcontinent. His leadership, honesty, and determination united the people to achieve freedom. Pakistan came into existence on August 14, 1947, because of his vision.

Born: November 9, 1877  |  Died: April 21, 1938

✍️ Allama Iqbal

Allama Iqbal, known as the Poet of the East, dreamed of a separate homeland for Muslims. His poetry inspired people to fight for their rights and work for a better future. He is also known as Mufakkir-e-Pakistan (the Thinker of Pakistan) and Shaair-e-Mashriq (the Poet of the East).

Born: July 31, 1893  |  Died: July 9, 1967

👩 Fatima Jinnah

Fatima Jinnah, the sister of Quaid-e-Azam, is called the Mother of the Nation. She supported her brother in the struggle for independence and worked to improve the lives of women in Pakistan. She is remembered for her strength and dedication to Pakistan.

Born: February 28, 1928  |  Died: July 8, 2016

🕊️ Abdul Sattar Edhi

Abdul Sattar Edhi was a humanitarian who devoted his life to helping the poor and needy. He founded the Edhi Foundation, which provides free hospital, orphanage, and ambulance services. He is known as "The Angel of Mercy" for his kindness and generosity.

Born: April 1, 1936  |  Died: October 10, 2021

⚛️ Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan

Dr. Abdul Qadeer Khan is known as the father of Pakistan's nuclear program. He worked hard to make Pakistan a strong and secure country. His contributions made Pakistan the first Muslim country to have nuclear power.

Passage 21.2
The Brave Little Girl

Read the story of a little girl who helps her village after a storm.

🌧️ Story

Once there was a little girl named Amina who lived in a small village. One day, a heavy storm hit the village, and many houses were damaged. Amina saw that her neighbours needed help.

She gathered her friends and started helping the villagers. They cleaned the streets, brought food to the elderly, and fixed broken roofs. Amina's bravery and kindness inspired everyone.

The villagers were so grateful that they organized a small party to thank her. Amina learned that even small actions can make a big difference.

Brave
Showing courage in difficult situations.
Grateful
Feeling thankful for something.
Inspire
To encourage others to do something good.

Step 1: Read the sentence carefully: "The villagers thanked Amina for her help."
Step 2: Remember — a noun is a person, place, or thing. Look for names of people or things.
Step 3: "villagers" — this is a noun (group of people). ✅
Step 4: "Amina" — this is a proper noun (name of a person). ✅
Step 5: "help" — this is a noun (a thing/action that can be given). ✅
✅ Final Answer: The nouns are villagers, Amina, and help.

Passage 21.3
Planet Earth 🌍

Read this informational passage about our home planet.

🌏 About Earth

Earth is the planet we live on and the third planet from the Sun. It is special because it supports life, providing air, water, and soil for plants, animals, and humans. Earth is called the "Blue Planet" as 70% of its surface is water. The rest is land, with mountains, forests, deserts, and plains.

Earth's atmosphere protects us from harmful rays and provides oxygen to breathe. It has one moon, which causes ocean tides and lights up the night. Earth rotates to give us day and night and orbits the Sun, taking 365 days to complete a year, which causes seasons.

To keep Earth healthy, we should plant trees, save water, and reduce pollution.

Planet
A large object in space that orbits a star.
Atmosphere
The layer of gases around a planet.
Orbit
To move in a path around something.

A Visit to the Zoo 🦁

🎢 The Story

One sunny morning, my family went to the zoo. I was very excited because it was my first time visiting the zoo. When we arrived, the first animals we saw were the lions. They were sitting under a tree and roaring loudly. It was a little scary but also fun!

We also saw monkeys swinging from tree to tree. They were playing and making funny faces. The colourful parrots were next. One of them even said, "Hello!" We then went to see the elephants. They were very big and were eating leaves. I liked the giraffes too; they had long necks and ate from tall trees. My brother and I also saw zebras with beautiful black and white stripes.

After seeing all the animals, we had ice cream. It was a wonderful day, and I can't wait to visit the zoo again!

Passage 21.5
Pakistan Armed Forces 🎖️

The Pakistan Armed Forces are the defenders of our country. They work tirelessly to protect Pakistan's borders and ensure the safety of its people. The armed forces are made up of three main branches.

Branch 1

🪖 Pakistan Army

The Pakistan Army is responsible for protecting the land borders. It is known for its bravery and discipline. The soldiers work in all kinds of environments — from deserts to mountains — to keep our country safe. They also help people during natural disasters like floods and earthquakes.

Branch 2

⚓ Pakistan Navy

The Pakistan Navy protects the country's waters. It has ships, submarines, and highly trained sailors who ensure that Pakistan's seas are safe. The Navy also helps in rescue operations and guards our coastline from threats.

Branch 3

✈️ Pakistan Air Force (PAF)

The Pakistan Air Force defends the skies of Pakistan. It has modern fighter jets and well-trained pilots who are ready to face any challenge. The PAF also plays an important role in delivering supplies and providing aid to remote areas.

Together!

Together, the Pakistan Armed Forces work to protect the nation and maintain peace. Their courage, hard work, and dedication make them a source of pride for all Pakistanis.

Defenders
Those who protect something from harm.
Borders
The edges or boundaries of a country.
Discipline
Being well-organised and following rules.
Submarine
A watercraft that can travel underwater.
Fighter Jet
A fast military aircraft used in combat.

Passage 21.6
Planning a School Project 📐

Ali and Sara, two Grade 4 students, were assigned a science project about "The Solar System." They had to make a model and write a report — and they needed teamwork!

💬 Dialogue

Ali: Hey Sara, have you started your part of the science project?

Sara: Not yet, Ali. I'm trying to understand how to draw the planets, but it's tricky.

Ali: We need to submit it by Friday! Have you gathered the materials for the model?

Sara: Yes, I have. But I still need to label the planets and finish the diagrams for the report.

Ali: I can help you with the drawings! I've already done something similar for my art class.

Sara: Really? That would be awesome! I also need to add some details to the planet descriptions. Can you help with that too?

Ali: Sure! Let's meet at my place after school and work together.

Sara: Thanks, Ali! With your help, we'll make it the best project in class!


Together, Ali and Sara decided to divide the work. Sara would focus on writing the details, while Ali would draw the planets and label them. By combining their strengths, they were confident their project would turn out amazing.
