All About Conjunctions

Conjunctions are the glue that holds our sentences together. Learn how words like and, but, because, although and more make your writing smoother and clearer!

What is a Conjunction?

A conjunction is a word that connects words, phrases, or sentences. Conjunctions make your writing smoother and easier to understand by linking related ideas together.

🔗

Connect Words

cats and dogs

🔄

🤔

Common Conjunctions

And, But, Or

And
Used to add similar ideas together.
I like reading and writing.
But
Used to show contrast or difference.
It was sunny but it was cold.
Or
Used to show a choice between two options.
Would you like tea or coffee?
Cause & Effect

Because and So

Because — Explains Reasons

Use because to explain why something happens.

I stayed at home because it was raining.
So — Shows Results

Use so to show the result of something.

It was raining, so I stayed home.
💡 Quick Tip: Because explains the reason (looks backward). So shows the result (looks forward). They are opposite sides of the same story!
More Conjunctions

When, While, Although, Unless, Until

When
Shows time. Can start or join a sentence.
I will call you when I get home.
When I get home, I will call you.
While
Shows two actions happening at the same time.
She was singing while he was playing the guitar.
Although
Shows a contrast — similar to "but" but stronger.
Although it was late, I stayed awake to read.
Unless
Means "if not" — sets a condition.
You can't go out unless you finish your homework.
Until
Means "up to the time of".
The store is open until 9 PM.
Guided Example

🔍 Choosing the Right Conjunction

"I was tired. I stayed awake to finish my book." — Join these with the correct conjunction.
Step 1 — Understand the relationship

Read both sentences carefully. The person was tired, yet they stayed awake. This shows a reason — being tired is the reason they might have slept, but they chose to stay awake. The second sentence explains why they stayed awake (to finish the book).

Step 2 — Choose the conjunction

We need to explain why they stayed awake. The conjunction "because" introduces reasons. So: "I stayed awake to finish my book" is the main action, and "I was tired" is background context.

Step 3 — Write the joined sentence

✅ "I stayed awake to finish my book because I was tired."
Or: "Although I was tired, I stayed awake to finish my book." (uses contrast!)

Step 4 — Check your answer

Does the sentence make sense? ✔ Does the conjunction correctly show the relationship? ✔ Is there a comma when the conjunction clause comes first? ✔ Well done!

Memory Tips

FANBOYS = coordinating conjunctions:
For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So
Because vs So: "It rained because of clouds" = reason. "It rained, so we got wet" = result.
Although + comma: Always add a comma when although starts the sentence!

Watch Out!

❌ "Although it was cold but we went out."
Don't use both although AND but!
✅ "Although it was cold, we went out."
Just use although alone.
❌ "While I arrived, she was cooking."
While = simultaneous actions. Use when for a single moment.
✅ "When I arrived, she was cooking."
