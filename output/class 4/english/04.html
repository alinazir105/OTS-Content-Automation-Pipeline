Possessive Adjectives

A possessive adjective is used with a noun to show ownership. It always comes before the noun it describes.

Definition: Possessive adjectives are placed before a noun to indicate who something belongs to.
✅ Correct
This is my book.
✅ Correct
That is her car.

📋 Quick Reference

I → my / mine
You → your / yours
He → his / his
She → her / hers
It → its / its
We → our / ours
They → their / theirs

💡 Key Tips

Possessive adjectives come before a noun. (my book)
Possessive pronouns stand alone — no noun after them. (The book is mine.)
Never say "the my book" — drop "the" with possessives!
2

Possessive Pronouns

A possessive pronoun is used without a noun. It replaces the noun entirely to show who something belongs to.

Definition: Possessive pronouns stand alone and show relation and possession without needing a noun after them.
✅ Pronoun Example
This book is mine.
✅ Pronoun Example
That car is hers.

🔍 How to Choose: Adjective or Pronoun?

1
Read the sentence carefully. Is there a noun after the blank?
2
Yes — noun follows? Use a Possessive Adjective. (my, your, his, her, its, our, their)
3
No — no noun follows? Use a Possessive Pronoun. (mine, yours, his, hers, its, ours, theirs)
4
Remember: Never use "the" before a possessive. ❌ "the my bag" → ✅ "my bag"

🆚 Adjective vs. Pronoun

Adjective (with noun):
This is my bag.
Pronoun (alone):
This bag is mine.

Both refer to the same ownership — just used differently!

🚫 Common Mistake

❌ Wrong
This is the my book.
✅ Right
This is my book.
❌ Wrong
That phone is the hers.
✅ Right
That phone is hers.
3

Apostrophe 's

We use an apostrophe to show possession with names and nouns. The rules depend on whether the noun is singular or plural.

Type of Noun Rule Example
Singular Add 's to show possession The cat's bowl is empty.
Plural (regular) Add ' to the end of the noun (already ends in -s) My parents' car is new.
Plural (irregular) Add 's — plural nouns not ending in -s The women's dresses are lovely.
Key Note: For names and singular nouns, always add 's.
For regular plural nouns ending in -s, just add ' at the end.
👜
This is my bag.
🏠
This house is ours.
🏍️
This is Sami's bike.

✏️ Apostrophe Rules

cat → cat's
Singular: add 's
parents → parents'
Regular plural: add ' only
women → women's
Irregular plural: add 's
children → children's
Irregular plural: add 's

⚠️ Watch Out!

Possessive pronouns never use an apostrophe:

  • ✅ yours (not your's)
  • ✅ hers (not her's)
  • ✅ ours (not our's)
  • ✅ theirs (not their's)
