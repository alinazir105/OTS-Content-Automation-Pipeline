Introduction

What is Composition?

Learning to express your ideas clearly in writing

📝 Definition

A composition is a piece of writing in which you organise and express your thoughts, ideas, or feelings clearly. Good compositions have a clear beginning, middle, and end.

✅ Why is Composition Important?
  • It helps you share your ideas with others.
  • It improves your vocabulary and grammar skills.
  • It teaches you to think in an organised way.
  • It is a key skill for school exams and everyday life.
📌 Key Elements of Good Writing
  • Clarity – Write clearly so the reader understands your message.
  • Organisation – Arrange your ideas in a logical order.
  • Detail – Add interesting details and examples to support your ideas.
  • Correct Language – Use proper grammar, spelling, and punctuation.
Types

Types of Composition

There are several types of compositions you will practise in this chapter.

📜

Paragraph

A group of sentences about one main topic or idea.

📖

Story / Narrative

A creative account of events with characters, setting, and plot.

📨

Letter

A written message to a friend, family member, or official.

📄

Application

A formal written request addressed to a principal or authority.

🌍

Descriptive Writing

Vivid description of a person, place, event, or object.

🔬

Narrative Report

Describing an experience like a science project or school trip.

🔍 Worked Example: Writing a Paragraph (Step by Step)

Topic: Your favourite national hero

STEP 1 — Choose your topic & brainstorm Think about who your national hero is.
Example: Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah — the founder of Pakistan.
Brainstorm: leadership, independence, vision, hard work, unity.
STEP 2 — Write your Topic Sentence Introduce your hero clearly.
✍️ "My favourite national hero is Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan."
STEP 3 — Add Supporting Sentences Give reasons why you admire them.
✍️ "He worked tirelessly for the rights of Muslims and led the movement for a free homeland."
✍️ "His famous motto 'Unity, Faith, Discipline' still guides Pakistanis today."
STEP 4 — Include Lessons Learned What can we learn from their life?
✍️ "From his life, we learn that determination and courage can help us overcome any challenge."
✍️ "He also teaches us that education and honesty are the keys to success."
STEP 5 — Write your Concluding Sentence Wrap up the paragraph neatly.
✍️ "Quaid-e-Azam will always be an inspiration to every Pakistani, and his legacy lives on in our hearts."

📄 Complete Sample Paragraph
My favourite national hero is Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, the founder of Pakistan. He worked tirelessly for the rights of Muslims and led the historic movement for an independent homeland. His famous motto — "Unity, Faith, Discipline" — still guides every Pakistani today. From his life, we learn that determination, courage, and education are the keys to success. Quaid-e-Azam will always be an inspiration to every Pakistani, and his legacy lives on in our hearts.
Letter Writing

How to Write a Letter

Letters have a special format. Learn the parts below!

✉️ Parts of a Friendly Letter
  • 1. Address & Date — Top right corner
  • 2. Salutation — "Dear [Name]," or "Dear Friend,"
  • 3. Body — The main message (opening, middle, closing thoughts)
  • 4. Closing — "Yours sincerely," / "With love,"
  • 5. Signature — Your name
House No. 5, Green Street
Lahore, Pakistan
15 November 2024

Dear Ahmed,


I hope this letter finds you in good health. I am writing to tell you about something very important — our planet Earth and why we must take care of it.


Earth is the only planet in our solar system that supports life. It gives us clean air, fresh water, fertile soil, and a perfect climate. Without Earth, there would be no plants, animals, or humans.


We must protect Earth by planting trees, saving water, and reducing pollution. Every small action counts!


Yours sincerely,
Ali

📋 Parts of a Formal Application
  • 1. The Principal, [School Name & Address]
  • 2. Subject: — Brief one-line purpose
  • 3. Respected Sir/Madam,
  • 4. Body — Explain your request clearly and politely
  • 5. Yours obediently,
       [Your Name], Class [X]
Writing Tasks

All 10 Composition Tasks

Here are all the writing tasks from Chapter 22. Click any card to expand tips!

Task 22.1 · Paragraph

My Favourite National Hero

Write a paragraph about your favourite national hero. Why do you admire them, and what lessons can we learn from their life?

💡 Tips:
  • Start with a strong topic sentence naming your hero.
  • Give at least 2–3 reasons for admiring them.
  • Mention 1–2 life lessons we can learn.
  • End with a concluding sentence.
Task 22.2 · Story

Helping During the Storm

Imagine you are one of Amina's friends. Write a story about how you helped your village during the storm.

💡 Tips:
  • Use first person ("I", "we", "my").
  • Describe the storm — rain, wind, darkness.
  • Explain what actions you took to help.
  • Include feelings of bravery and teamwork.
Task 22.3 · Application

Sick Leave Application

Write an application to your principal requesting two days of sick leave after helping clean up after rain in your neighbourhood.

💡 Tips:
  • Use formal application format.
  • Subject: Application for Sick Leave.
  • Explain that you helped clean up after rain and fell unwell.
  • Request exactly 2 days leave.
  • Sign off as "Yours obediently".
Task 22.4 · Paragraph

Why I Love Earth

Write a paragraph about Earth and explain why you love it.

💡 Tips:
  • Mention Earth's natural beauty — oceans, mountains, forests.
  • Describe what Earth provides for us.
  • Say what you can do to protect it.
Task 22.5 · Letter

Letter: Why Earth is Important

Write a letter to your friend explaining why the Earth is important.

💡 Tips:
  • Use friendly letter format with address and date.
  • Start with "Dear [Friend's name],".
  • Mention air, water, food, and biodiversity.
  • Urge your friend to protect the environment.
Task 22.6 · Narrative

My Day at the Zoo

Write about a day when you visited a zoo. Describe the animals you saw and which one was your favourite.

💡 Tips:
  • Describe your journey to the zoo.
  • Name at least 4–5 animals you saw.
  • Describe your favourite animal in detail.
  • Share how you felt at the end of the day.
Task 22.7 · Paragraph

My Favourite Branch of the Armed Forces

Write a paragraph about your favourite branch of Pakistan's Armed Forces and why you admire it.

💡 Tips:
  • Choose from Army, Navy, or Air Force.
  • Explain what that branch does to protect Pakistan.
  • Share why you find it inspiring.
Task 22.8 · Letter

A Letter to a Soldier

Write a letter to a soldier thanking them for their service and telling them how much you respect their dedication to protecting Pakistan.

💡 Tips:
  • Start with "Dear Brave Soldier," or a similar respectful salutation.
  • Express your gratitude and admiration.
  • Describe what their sacrifice means to you and Pakistan.
  • End with warm wishes and prayers.
Task 22.9 · Narrative

My Favourite Science Project

Narrate a project you worked on and why it was special. Include challenges you faced and how you overcame them.

💡 Tips:
  • Name the project and describe its purpose.
  • Explain what steps you took.
  • Describe at least one challenge and how you solved it.
  • Share what you learned from the experience.
Task 22.10 · Application

Application for a Science Exhibition

Write an application to the principal requesting permission to organise an educational science exhibition event.

💡 Include these 4 points:
  1. The purpose of the science exhibition.
  2. The suggested dates and themes for the event.
  3. How the exhibition will benefit the students.
  4. A polite request for approval and an offer of assistance.

📋 Helpful Writing Templates

Use these sentence starters to help you write your compositions!

✍️ Paragraph Template

Topic Sentence:"My favourite ___ is ___ because ___."
Reason 1:"One reason I admire them is ___."
Reason 2:"They also ___, which shows that ___."
Lesson:"From their life, we can learn that ___."
Conclusion:"___ will always be an inspiration because ___."

📨 Letter Template

Opening:"I hope this letter finds you in good health. I am writing to tell you about ___."
Main Body:"___ is important because ___. It also ___."
Call to Action:"I hope you will also ___ and help ___."
Closing:"Looking forward to your reply. Yours sincerely, ___."

📄 Application Template

To:The Principal, [School Name]
Subject:Application for ___ (brief purpose)
Request:"I most respectfully request that you kindly grant me ___."
Reason:"The reason for this request is that ___."
Closing:"I shall be very grateful for your kind consideration."

📚 Quick Reference

  • Paragraph — One central idea
  • Letter — Address, salutation, body, closing
  • Application — Formal request to authority
  • Story — Characters + Plot + Setting
  • Descriptive — Vivid sensory details

🔑 Key Words

🟢Topic Sentence — Introduces main idea
🟡Supporting Sentence — Gives detail/reasons
🔵Concluding Sentence — Wraps up the paragraph
📨Salutation — "Dear ..."
📝Application — Formal letter of request

✅ Writing Checklist

✔️Did you include a topic sentence?
✔️Did you add at least 2 supporting sentences?
✔️Did you write a concluding sentence?
✔️Did you check spelling and grammar?
✔️Did you use capital letters correctly?
✔️Is your letter/application in the correct format?

📖 Accordion Tips

Common Mistakes ▼
  • Starting every sentence with "I"
  • Forgetting to write a topic sentence
  • Using informal language in applications
  • Not writing the date in letters
  • Going off topic in the middle
Power Vocabulary ▼
  • Remarkable — very impressive
  • Dedicated — fully committed
  • Inspiring — motivating others
  • Extraordinary — very unusual
  • Perseverance — keep trying
Story Starters ▼
  • "It was a dark and stormy night…"
  • "I will never forget the day when…"
  • "The wind howled fiercely as…"
  • "Suddenly, everything changed when…"
