Past Simple Tense

Discover how to talk about actions, states, and habits that happened in the past!

✅ Affirmative ❌ Negative ❓ Interrogative 🔵 Stative Verbs
Section 1

Formation

How do we build sentences in the Past Simple Tense? There are three types: Affirmative, Negative, and Interrogative.

🟢 Affirmative (Positive Sentences)

Structure: Subject + past tense verb + object

Formula
Subject + past tense verb + Object

Example: She walked to school yesterday.

🔴 Negative Sentences

Structure: Subject + did not (didn't) + base form of verb + object

Formula
Subject + did not / didn't + base verb + Object

Example: She didn't walk to school yesterday.

🔵 Interrogative (Question Sentences)

Structure: Did + subject + base form of verb + object?

Formula
Did + Subject + base verb + Object?

Example: Did she walk to school yesterday?

Type Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + past verb + object She walked to school yesterday.
Negative Subject + didn't + base verb + object She didn't walk to school yesterday.
Interrogative Did + subject + base verb + object? Did she walk to school yesterday?
📌 Quick Reference

Subject Forms

I→ walked / didn't walk / Did I walk?
She/He/It/Ali→ walked / didn't walk / Did she walk?
We/You/They→ walked / didn't walk / Did they walk?

⚠️ Remember!

In negative & interrogative sentences, always use the base form of the verb (not past tense).

The Past Simple Tense tells us about things that happened and are finished in the past.

Section 2

Uses of Past Simple

The Past Simple Tense is used in several important ways.

✅

Completed Actions in the Past

Actions that started and finished in the past.

I visited Lahore last week.
She finished her homework an hour ago.
🔗

A Series of Past Actions

Multiple actions that happened one after another.

He opened the door, entered the room, and sat down.
🔄

Past Habits or Routines

Things you used to do regularly in the past.

We always went to the park when we were kids.
💬

Stative Verbs (was / were)

The verb "be" shows state, condition, or existence in the past.

She was a teacher. (State)
We were ready. (Condition)
There was a book on the table. (Existence)
🤝

Possessions (had)

We use "had" to show possession, relationships, or illness in the past.

She had a beautiful dress. (Possession)
They had many friends. (Relationships)
I had a headache. (Illness)

🔵 Stative Verbs — Deeper Look

🟢 "Be" (was / were)

  • She was a teacher. ← State of Being
  • We were ready for the trip. ← Condition
  • There was a book on the table. ← Existence

🟠 "Have" (had)

  • She had a beautiful dress. ← Possession
  • They had many friends. ← Relationships
  • I had a headache. ← Illness

🔍 Guided Worked Example

Learn step-by-step how to change a sentence into Past Simple Negative and Interrogative.

Original Sentence: "She visits her grandparents every week."

Let's convert this to Past Simple in all three forms.

1

Identify the Verb

The verb is "visits" (present tense). We need its past form.

"visits" → Past form: "visited"
2

Write the Affirmative

Use the past tense verb directly after the subject.

She visited her grandparents last week. ✅
3

Write the Negative

Add didn't after subject, then use the BASE form of the verb.

She didn't visit her grandparents last week. ❌
4

Write the Interrogative

Start with Did, then subject, then BASE form of verb.

Did she visit her grandparents last week? ❓
5

🎉 All Three Forms Complete!

You've successfully converted the sentence into all three forms of Past Simple Tense!
