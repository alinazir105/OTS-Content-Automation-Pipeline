Introduction

What is a Preposition?

The word that connects everything together

📌 Definition

A preposition is a word that connects a noun or pronoun to other words in a sentence. Prepositions help us understand the place, time, direction, and movement of things in a sentence.

💡 Key Idea

Think of a preposition as a "position word" — it tells us where something is, when something happens, or how something moves!

The 4 Types of Prepositions

📍
of Place

Where something is

Tells us the location or position of a person or thing.

e.g., under, beside, in, behind, in front of

⏰
of Time

When something happens

Tells us the time when an event or action occurs.

e.g., at, in, on, during

🧭
of Direction

Where something is going

Describes where someone or something is moving towards.

e.g., to, towards, from, across, through, over, past

🏃
of Movement

How something moves

Describes the way someone or something moves from one place to another.

e.g., down, off, along, out of, around

🔑 Quick Reference

Place under, beside, in, behind, in front of
Time at, in, on, during
Direction to, towards, from, across, through, over, past
Movement down, off, along, out of, around

📝 Remember

A preposition always comes before a noun or pronoun. The noun that follows is called the object of the preposition.

Example: The cat is under the table.
"table" = object of preposition

Prepositions of Time

When Something Happens ⏰

These prepositions tell us when an event or action takes place.

PrepositionUsed forExample Sentence
atSpecific times (clock times)I finish my homework at 8 o'clock every night.
inMonths, years, seasons, parts of dayWe have exams in December. / I go for a walk in the morning.
onDays of the week, specific datesThe class will start on Monday.
duringThroughout an event or periodMy friend called me during the movie.
🕗

Use at for exact clock times

📅
in

We have exams in December. / I walk in the morning.

Use in for months, years, seasons

📆
on

The class will start on Monday.

Use on for days and dates

🎬
during

My friend called me during the movie.

Use during for events/periods

🧠 Easy Trick to Remember AT / IN / ON

AT a specific time (AT 5 o'clock) → ON a specific day (ON Monday) → IN a longer period (IN December, IN 2024, IN the morning)

Prepositions of direction help describe where someone or something is moving towards.

Prepositions of Movement

How Something Moves 🏃

Prepositions of movement describe the way someone or something moves from one place to another.

PrepositionMeaningExample Sentence
downMoving lower / descendingThe ball rolled down the hill. / The children slide down the slide.
offMoving away from a surfaceHe jumped off a cliff.
alongMoving in the same direction as somethingHe walked along the beach.
out ofExiting something / moving away from insideThe bird flew out of the cage.
aroundMoving in a circle or all sidesThe horse galloped around the field.

🔍 Direction vs Movement — What's the difference?

Direction tells us where something is going (towards a destination): She walked TO the park.

Movement tells us how something moves (the manner or path of motion): He walked ALONG the beach.

🔍 Guided Worked Example

Learn how to identify and use prepositions step by step.

Sentence: "The children ran across the field and climbed up the tree."

Step 1: Read the sentence carefully

Identify all the words and look for words that show position, time, or movement.

Step 2: Find the prepositions

In this sentence, the prepositions are "across" and "up". These are the connector words.

Step 3: Identify the type

"across" = Preposition of Direction (moving from one side to another)
"up" = Preposition of Movement (moving higher)

Step 4: Find the object

Each preposition has an object (a noun after it):
across the field → object = "field"
up the tree → object = "tree"

✅ Step 5: Confirm your answer

Always check: Does the preposition + object make sense? Does it tell us direction, place, time, or movement? ✅ Yes! We're done.
