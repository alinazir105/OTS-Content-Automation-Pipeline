Formation

How do we build sentences in the Future Simple tense?

📖 What is the Future Simple Tense?

We use the Future Simple tense to talk about things that will happen in the future. The key word is "will" — it goes between the subject and the base form of the verb.

STRUCTURE:
Affirmative: Subject + will + base verb + object
Negative: Subject + will not (won't) + base verb + object
Question: Will + subject + base verb + object?
⚠ Always use the BASE FORM of the verb — never add -s, -ing, or -ed!
Type Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + will + base verb + object She will walk to school tomorrow.
Negative Subject + will not (won't) + base verb + object She won't walk to school tomorrow.
Interrogative Will + subject + base verb + object? Will she walk to school tomorrow?

Examples by Subject

Subject Affirmative Negative Question
I I will call you later. I won't call you later. Will I call you later?
She / He / It / Ali She will walk to school tomorrow. She won't walk to school tomorrow. Will she walk to school tomorrow?
We / You / They They will come to the party. They won't come to the party. Will they come to the party?

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • ❌ She will calls you → ✅ She will call
  • ❌ Will they visits? → ✅ Will they visit?
  • ❌ She won't calling → ✅ She won't call
  • ❌ He will not finishes → ✅ He will not finish
  • ❌ Will she going? → ✅ Will she go?

💡 Remember

  • will is the same for ALL subjects
  • won't = will not (contraction)
  • Always use base form after will/won't
  • No -s for third person (he/she/it)

Uses of Future Simple

When do we use the Future Simple tense?

🌦️ Predictions about the future

We use will to talk about things we think or believe will happen.

– It will rain tomorrow.
– The team will win the match.

⚡ Decisions made at moment of speaking

When we decide something right now, at the moment of speaking.

– I will answer the phone.
– I will have the soup, please.

🤝 Promises, Offers & Threats

Will is used to make promises, offer help, or warn someone.

– I will help you with your homework. (Promise)
– I will carry that for you. (Offer)

🔮 General Future Facts

Things that are certain or expected to happen in the future.

– She will be 10 years old next month.
– The sun will rise tomorrow.

💡 How to choose: Will or Won't?

  • Use will for positive/affirmative future statements.
  • Use won't (will not) to say something will NOT happen.
  • Both are used for all the same situations — just with opposite meaning.

Stative Verbs: Will Be & Will Have

Special uses of future simple with stative verbs

📌 "Will Be" — State, Condition, Existence

The verb "will be" is a stative verb used to show a state, condition, or existence in the future.

  • She will be a teacher. State of Being
  • We will be ready for the trip. Condition
  • There will be a book on the table. Existence

📌 "Will Have" — Possession, Relationships, Illness

We use "will have" as a stative verb to indicate possession, relationships, or states such as illness in the future.

  • She will have a beautiful dress. Possession
  • They will have many friends. Relationships
  • She will have a fever. Illness

🧠 Stative vs Action Verbs

Stative verbs describe states (be, have, like, know) rather than actions. In the future simple, will be and will have are the most common stative verbs you'll use.

Knowledge Quiz

🎯 Future Simple Tense Quiz

10 questions · Choose the best answer for each question

Question 1 of 10
Which sentence is correct in Future Simple tense?
✅ Correct! In Future Simple, we use will + base verb. The base form of "walk" is just "walk" — no -s, -ed, or -ing!
Question 2 of 10
What is the negative form of: "He will finish his work."
✅ Correct! won't = will not. Always use the base form after won't: won't finish ✔
Question 3 of 10
Which sentence uses Future Simple to express a PREDICTION?
✅ "It will rain tomorrow" is a prediction — we believe something will happen in the future.
Question 4 of 10
How do you make a question in Future Simple?
✅ For questions in Future Simple, move Will to the front: Will + subject + base verb?
Question 5 of 10
Fill in the blank: "They ______ (be) at the airport tomorrow."
✅ will be — The base form of "to be" is just "be". We never say "will are" or "will is".
Question 6 of 10
Which sentence expresses a PROMISE?
✅ "I will help you with your homework" is a promise — the speaker is committing to doing something for another person.
Question 7 of 10
Which sentence has a MISTAKE?
✅ "Will they visits" is wrong. After "Will" in a question, the verb must be in BASE form: "Will they visit?"
Question 8 of 10
"She will have a beautiful dress." — What type of usage is this?
✅ "Will have" as a stative verb can show possession — the speaker owns something in the future.
Question 9 of 10
What is the correct question form of: "She will visit her grandparents next weekend."
✅ Will she visit... — Move "will" to the front, keep the base verb "visit" unchanged.
Question 10 of 10
"I will answer the phone." — Why is Future Simple used here?
✅ This is a decision made at the moment of speaking — the speaker decides right now, in this moment, to answer the phone.
