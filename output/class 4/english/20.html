What is an Adverb?
An adverb is a word that adds more information to a verb, an adjective, or another adverb. It answers the questions when, where, why, and how.

Examples

  • She sings beautifully. (How does she sing?)
  • He will come tomorrow. (When will he come?)
  • They played outside. (Where did they play?)
  • She always listens carefully. (How often?)
  • She is very happy. (Modifies adjective)
  • The room was too dark to see anything.
  • She runs very quickly. (Modifies another adverb)
  • The athlete jumped almost perfectly.

Types of Adverbs

Manner

How something happens

Tell us how an action is done.

  • slowly, quickly, loudly
  • quietly, happily, carefully
  • easily, sadly
📌 "The cat walked slowly across the room."
Time

When something happens

Tell us when an action happens.

  • now, later, today, soon
  • yesterday, tomorrow
  • early, tonight
📌 "I will finish my homework tomorrow."
Place

Where something happens

Tell us where an action happens.

  • here, there, outside
  • inside, everywhere
  • nearby, upstairs, downstairs
📌 "The children are playing outside."
Frequency

How often something happens

Tell us how often an action occurs.

  • always, never, often
  • sometimes, usually, rarely
  • frequently, occasionally
📌 "She always brushes her teeth before bed."

🔍 Worked Example

How to identify an adverb in a sentence

Sentence: "She always listens carefully."

1 Find the verb in the sentence. → listens
2 Ask "How often does she listen?" → always → This is an Adverb of Frequency
3 Ask "How does she listen?" → carefully → This is an Adverb of Manner
4 ✅ Both always (frequency) and carefully (manner) are adverbs in this sentence!

📖 Key Concepts

Definition

A word that modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb.

The 4 Types

🏃 Manner ⏰ Time 📍 Place 🔄 Frequency

Questions Answered

  • How? → Manner
  • When? → Time
  • Where? → Place
  • How often? → Frequency

💡 Quick Tip

Many adverbs of manner end in -ly: slowly, quickly, happily, carefully
But not all: fast, hard, well, late are also adverbs!

🔑 Formula

Subject + Verb + Adverb

Example: She sings beautifully.

Adverb + Verb + Subject

Example: Always be kind.

The 4 Types of Adverbs

Time → When: now, today, tomorrow
Place → Where: here, there, outside
