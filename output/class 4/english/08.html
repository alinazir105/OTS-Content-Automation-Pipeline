📘 Past Continuous Tense — Class 4 English
LESSON 8 · ENGLISH GRAMMAR

Past Continuous
Tense

Learn how to describe actions that were happening at a specific moment in the past!

She was walking to school.
They were playing football.
It was getting cold.

Formation

The Past Continuous Tense uses was/were + the verb with -ing. Look at the three types below:

Type Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + was/were + verb(-ing) + object She was walking to school at 8 AM.
Negative Subject + was/were + not + verb(-ing) + object She wasn't walking to school at 8 AM.
Interrogative Was/Were + Subject + verb(-ing) + object? Was she walking to school at 8 AM?
🔑 Key Formula:
Subject + was/were + verb(-ing) + object

🪜 Guided Example: Build a Sentence

Let's build: "They / play / football / 5 PM" → Past Continuous

1
Choose the subject

The subject is They — a plural subject. So we use were.

2
Add the auxiliary verb

Plural subject → were

3
Add verb + -ing

play → playing

4
Complete the sentence

✅ They were playing football at 5 PM yesterday.

🎉 Done!
📌 When to use WAS vs WERE:
  • WAS → I, She, He, It, Ali (singular)
  • WERE → We, You, They (plural)

⚡ Quick Reference

Affirmative: was/were + verb-ing

Negative: was/were + not + verb-ing

Question: Was/Were + subject + verb-ing?

🧩 WAS or WERE?

  • I was reading
  • She/He/It was reading
  • Ali was reading
  • We/You/They were reading

📝 Verb + -ing Rules

  • walk → walking
  • play → playing
  • run → running (double)
  • write → writing (drop e)
  • study → studying

Uses of Past Continuous

We use the Past Continuous in three main situations:

🕐 Actions in progress at a specific time

  • I was reading at 7 PM yesterday.
  • They were watching TV when I arrived.

⚡ Past Continuous + Past Simple

  • The sun was shining when I woke up.
  • She was cooking when the phone rang.
Longer/background action interrupted by a shorter action

📈 Changing & developing situations

  • She was getting bored.
  • It was getting cold in Quetta.

💡 Remember

  • When = past simple interrupts
  • While = both actions continuous
  • Signal words: while, when, at that time, at 7 PM
🔥 Interruption Pattern:
She was cooking (past continuous = longer action) when the phone rang (past simple = shorter interruption).
