Formation of Present Simple

The Present Simple has three types of sentences. Learn the structure for each one!

🔍 Step-by-Step: Building a Sentence

Verb: walk · Subject: She
Step 1 – Choose your Subject
Pick the subject: She (third person singular)
Step 2 – Affirmative
Subject + verb (+s/es for he/she/it): She walks to school every day.
Step 3 – Negative
Subject + does + not + base verb: She doesn't walk to school every day.
Step 4 – Interrogative
Does + Subject + base verb + ? → Does she walk to school every day?
📋 Structure at a Glance
Type Structure Example
Affirmative Subject + base verb (+s/es) + object She walks to school every day.
Negative Subject + do/does + not + base verb + object She doesn't walk to school every day.
Interrogative Do/Does + subject + base verb + object? Does she walk to school every day?

More Examples

Subject Affirmative Negative Interrogative
I I walk to school every day. I don't walk to school every day. Do I walk to school every day?
She / He / It / Ali She walks to school every day. She doesn't walk to school every day. Does she walk to school every day?
We / You / They They walk to school every day. They don't walk to school every day. Do they walk to school every day?
⚡ Key Rule – Adding -s or -es

With he, she, it (third person singular), add -s or -es to the verb in affirmative sentences.

walk → walks  |  go → goes  |  watch → watches  |  read → reads

In negatives and questions, always use the base form: She doesn't go / Does she go?

🗂 Quick Reference

Affirmative: S + V(+s) + Obj
Negative: S + don't/doesn't + V
Question: Do/Does + S + V + ?

📌 Remember!

Add -s/-es for he/she/it only in affirmative.
Use does not (doesn't) for he/she/it negatives.
Never add -s to the verb after do/does.
Time expressions: always, often, every day, sometimes

💡 Did You Know?

The Present Simple is also called the Simple Present. It is one of the most commonly used tenses in English! 🌟

Quick think: "Water boils at 100°C." — Why is Present Simple used here?
Because it's always true! ✅

Uses of Present Simple

The Present Simple is used in many different situations. Here are the main ones:

🌍 Things Always True

  • Water boils at 100 degrees.
  • The Sun rises in the East.
  • Ice melts in heat.

📅 Permanent / Regular Activities

  • I live in Karachi.
  • She goes to school every day.
  • He works in a hospital.

💬 Opinions and Thoughts

  • I like pizza.
  • He thinks the movie is boring.
  • She believes in hard work.

⏰ With Time Expressions

  • I often read books.
  • They always arrive early.
  • We sometimes eat out.
  • She never forgets her homework.
📊 Frequency Adverbs — From Most to Least Often

Stative Verbs

Some verbs describe states, not actions. These are called stative verbs.

What is a Stative Verb?

Stative verbs describe a state, condition, or existence rather than an action. The most common stative verb is "be" (is, am, are).

🧑‍🏫

State of Being

"She is a teacher."

Describes what someone is.

✅

Condition

"We are ready for the trip."

Describes a state or condition.

📚

Existence

"There is a book on the table."

Shows something exists.

💼 "Have" and "Has" as Stative Verbs

We use have and has to show possession, relationships, or states like illness.

Possession

I have a bicycle.

She has a beautiful dress.

Relationships

He has two sisters.

They have many friends.

Illness / State

She has a fever.

He has a headache.

Imperatives

Imperatives give instructions, commands, advice, suggestions, or requests. They usually begin with a verb!

📢 What are Imperatives?

Imperatives are sentences that tell someone to do something. They usually start directly with the verb — there is no subject needed because we are speaking to "you"!

🚨 Commands

  • Sit down!
  • Stop talking!
  • Don't touch that!

Direct and firm instructions.

💡 Suggestions

  • Let's go for a walk.
  • Let's try a different way.
  • Try reading this book.

Ideas offered to a group.

🌟 Advice

  • Drink more water.
  • Exercise regularly.
  • Always finish your homework on time.

Helpful guidance for wellbeing.

🙏 Requests

  • Please keep quiet.
  • Please help me carry this bag.
  • Please turn off the light.

Polite asks, often with "please".
