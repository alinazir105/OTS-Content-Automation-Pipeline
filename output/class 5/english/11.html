Background

What is an Interjection?

An interjection is a special kind of word or phrase that expresses a sudden feeling or emotion. They are powerful, short, and burst right out of us!

📌 Definition

An interjection is a word or phrase that expresses a sudden feeling or emotion. Interjections are often followed by an exclamation mark (!) to show strong emotions. They stand alone or appear at the beginning of a sentence.

STRUCTURE
Interjection + ! → Strong Emotion
Interjection + , → Mild Emotion

Example: Wow! That's amazing. (strong)
Example: Oh, I see now. (mild)

🔍 Quick Discovery

Interjections are unique because they don't connect grammatically to the rest of the sentence. They just pop out to show how you feel at that moment! They can be single words like "Wow" or short phrases like "Oh no!". They are the most emotional part of speech!

Core Concept

Types of Interjections

Different interjections express different emotions. Here is a complete guide to the most common ones:

Interjection Emotion / Use Example Sentence
Wow Surprise / Amazement Wow! This is an amazing painting.
Oh Realization / Understanding Oh, I forgot my homework!
Hooray Joy / Celebration Hooray! We won the game.
Ouch Pain Ouch! That hurt.
Oops Mistake / Accident Oops! I spilled the milk.
Hey Attention Hey! Watch where you're going.
Ah Relief Ah, this feels so relaxing.
Alas Sadness / Regret Alas! The cat ran away.
Hurrah Joy / Victory Hurrah! We have won the match.

Yes! Every Language Has Them!

Almost every language in the world has interjections. They are one of the most natural parts of human speech!

📌

No Grammar Rules!

Click to find out!

🎯

They Stand Alone!

Interjections do not connect grammatically to other words. They are independent expressions of emotion!

😮

Strong vs Mild?

Click to find out!

⚡

Two Strengths!

Strong interjections use "!" (Wow! Ouch!). Mild interjections use "," (Oh, I see. Ah, nice.).

📚

Part of Speech?

Click to find out!

🏆

The 8th Part of Speech!

Interjections are officially the 8th part of speech in English grammar, alongside nouns, verbs, adjectives and more!

Challenge

Rewrite with Interjections

Rewrite each sentence by adding a suitable interjection at the beginning. There may be more than one correct answer!

  • 1) The cat jumped off the high wall.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing surprise or amazement (e.g., Wow!)
  • 2) I forgot my umbrella at home.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing a mistake or realization (e.g., Oops! or Oh!)
  • 3) The children started clapping when the magician finished.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing joy or celebration (e.g., Hooray! or Wow!)
  • 4) I slipped on the wet floor.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing pain or accident (e.g., Ouch! or Oops!)
  • 5) My favourite team lost the championship.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing sadness or regret (e.g., Alas! or Oh!)
  • 6) I stumbled my toe on the table.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing pain (e.g., Ouch!)
  • 7) The new roller coaster looks exciting.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing amazement (e.g., Wow!)
  • 8) I spilled coffee on my new dress.
    💡 Hint: Use an interjection showing a mistake or sadness (e.g., Oops! or Alas!)
