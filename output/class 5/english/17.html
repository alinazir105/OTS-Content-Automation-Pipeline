Background

What is Comprehension?

Comprehension means fully understanding what you read — not just the words, but the ideas, feelings, and messages behind them.

📝

Literal Understanding

Finding information directly stated in the text. "What percentage of Earth is covered by water?" — the answer is written in the passage.

🧠

Inferential Understanding

Reading between the lines. Understanding ideas not directly stated, like the lesson a character learns from their experience.

💬

Critical Thinking

Evaluating the text — forming opinions about characters, events, and messages. Why are honeybees important? What would you do?

🔗

Making Connections

Linking what you read to your own life, other books, or the world around you to deepen understanding.

🔍 Discovery Zone: The 3-Level Reading Model

Core Concept

Reading Strategies

Use these proven strategies to tackle any comprehension passage with confidence.

The DEAR Strategy for Comprehension
D — Decode: Read the passage carefully, noting key words E — Examine: Identify topic, main idea, and supporting details A — Analyse: Think about the WHY and HOW, not just the WHAT R — Respond: Write clear, complete sentences using evidence from the text
Structure your answer like this:

1. Restate part of the question in your answer.
2. Use evidence from the passage (quote or paraphrase).
3. Explain your answer if it's an opinion or inference question.

Example: Q: "What lesson did the farmer learn?"
A: "The farmer learned that patience and care can lead to great rewards, because his mango tree only produced fruit after several years of daily care."
Factual Questions — Ask for specific information from the text. Look for numbers, names, and actions.

Explanatory Questions — Ask "How" or "Why". Explain a process or reason using the passage.

Vocabulary Questions — Ask about word meanings in context.

Moral/Lesson Questions — Ask what a character learned or what message the author is giving.

The Journey of Water

💧 Water is one of the most important natural resources on Earth. It covers about 70% of our planet, but only a small amount is fresh and drinkable. Water goes through a continuous cycle. First, it evaporates from rivers, lakes, and oceans due to the heat of the sun. The vapour rises into the sky, where it cools and forms clouds. These clouds eventually release the water as rain, bringing fresh water back to the land. This cycle is called the water cycle, and it is vital for all living things.

📌 Questions & Model Answers

1) What percentage of the Earth is covered in water? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerAbout 70% of the Earth is covered in water.
2) What happens to water when the sun heats it? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerWhen the sun heats water, it evaporates from rivers, lakes, and oceans, turning into water vapour that rises into the sky.
3) How do clouds form? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerClouds form when the water vapour rises into the sky and cools down, causing it to condense into clouds.
4) What is the water cycle, and why is it important? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerThe water cycle is the continuous process by which water evaporates, forms clouds, and falls as rain. It is important because it brings fresh water back to the land and is vital for all living things.

The Life of a Honeybee

🐝 Honeybees are small but very hardworking insects. They live in groups called colonies, which can have thousands of bees. Each bee in the colony has a specific job. The queen bee's job is to lay eggs, while the worker bees gather nectar from flowers. They bring this nectar back to the hive and turn it into honey. Honeybees communicate with each other through a special dance to tell others where flowers are. Without honeybees, many plants would not be able to grow, as they help in pollination.

📌 Questions & Model Answers

1) What is a group of bees called? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerA group of bees is called a colony.
2) What is the queen bee's main job? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerThe queen bee's main job is to lay eggs.
3) How do worker bees communicate with each other? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerWorker bees communicate with each other through a special dance, which tells other bees where flowers can be found.
4) Why are honeybees important for plants? Click to reveal ▾
AnswerHoneybees are important for plants because they help in pollination. Without them, many plants would not be able to grow.
