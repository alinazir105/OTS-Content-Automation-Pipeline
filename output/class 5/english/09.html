📖Introduction

A preposition is a word that connects a noun or pronoun to other words in a sentence. Prepositions help us understand the place, time, direction, and movement of things in a sentence.
💡

Did You Know?

Click to flip!

🤩

The word "preposition" comes from Latin praepositio meaning "placed before" — because prepositions are placed BEFORE a noun or pronoun!

🔍What is a Preposition?

SENTENCE STRUCTURE:
Subject + Verb + Preposition + Object
Example: The cat jumped [onto] the roof.
Key Roles of Prepositions
  • 📍 Place — Where something is: under, over, behind, above...
  • ➡️ Direction/Movement — Where something is going: into, onto, through, across...
  • 🎯 Destination — The endpoint of movement: to
  • ✍️ Agent — Who did the action: by
  • 🎁 Purpose/Recipient — For whom or what: for
  • 🔨 Instrument — How something was done: with

📍Place Prepositions

These prepositions tell us where something is located.

✈️
Over
Higher than something else.
The plane is flying over the mountains.
🗝️
Under
Lower than something else.
The keys are under the table.
🏞️
Between
In the middle of two things.
The park is between the two houses.
🐱
Behind
At the back of something.
The cat is hiding behind the tree.
🏫
Next to
Beside something.
The library is next to the school.
🕐
Above
Higher but not directly over something.
The clock is above the door.
🖼️
Below
Lower but not directly under something.
The picture is hanging below the shelf.
Above = higher in position but NOT directly on top.
Over = directly on top or covering something.

✅ "The clock is above the door." (higher, but to the side)
✅ "The plane flew over the mountains." (directly above, crossing)

➡️Movement Prepositions

These prepositions describe movement from one place to another.

🏙️
Across
From one side to another.
The park is across the street.
🌲
Through
Movement within or across an area.
He walked through the forest.
🏊
Into
Movement inside something.
He jumped into the pool.
🐈
Onto
Movement to a surface.
The cat jumped onto the roof.
Into = movement to the inside of something.
Onto = movement to the surface/top of something.

✅ "She jumped into the pool." (inside the water)
✅ "The cat jumped onto the table." (on top of the table)

🔗Other Prepositions

🏪
To
Indicates destination.
He is going to the market.
📚
By
Indicates the agent or doer of an action.
The book was written by J.K. Rowling.
🎁
For
Indicates purpose or recipient.
This gift is for you.
🔨
With
Indicates the instrument or accompaniment used.
He fixed it with a hammer.
