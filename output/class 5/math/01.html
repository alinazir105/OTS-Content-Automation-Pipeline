Introduction

What Are Numbers?

Numbers are everywhere! We use them to count, measure, and calculate. In this unit, you will explore large numbers, place values, and powerful arithmetic operations.

🔢

Numbers Up to Billions

We'll work with numbers up to 10 digits — from ones all the way to billions. Every digit has a specific place and value.

➕

Four Operations

Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division — these four operations are the building blocks of all maths calculations.

🧮

BODMAS Rule

When an expression has multiple operations, BODMAS tells us the exact order to solve them — Brackets first, then division, multiplication, addition and subtraction.

🌟

Real-Life Maths

Word problems help you apply maths skills to real situations — like calculating money, measuring distances, and sharing equally.

Did You Know?

The number 1,000,000,000 is called ONE BILLION. If you counted one number every second, it would take you about 31 years to count to a billion without sleeping!

The Four Operations

➕ Addition

Line up digits, add column by column right to left. Carry over when sum ≥ 10.

  638,941
+ 347,036
= 985,977

➖ Subtraction

Subtract column by column. Borrow from the next column when needed.

  871,032
- 430,912
= 440,120

✖️ Multiplication

Multiply by each digit, shift left, then add the partial products.

  527
   × 3
= 1,581

➗ Division

Long division: divide, multiply, subtract, bring down — repeat. Remainder < divisor.

643,185 ÷ 24
Quotient: 26,799
Remainder: 9

Worked Examples

Step-by-Step Solutions

Follow each example carefully and understand the reasoning behind every step.

📍 Example 1: Write 567,812 in Words

ThousandsOnes
H-ThT-ThThHTO
567812
✅ Answer: Five hundred sixty-seven thousand, eight hundred twelve

📝 Example 2: Expanded Form of 849,018

1

Identify each digit: 8 in H-Th, 4 in T-Th, 9 in Th, 0 in H, 1 in T, 8 in O.

2

Write value of each digit: 800,000 + 40,000 + 9,000 + 0 + 10 + 8

3

Drop the zero term (0 hundreds) and simplify.

✅ 849,018 = 800,000 + 40,000 + 9,000 + 10 + 8

➕ Example 3: Add 5,389,624 + 930,157

1

Align by place value. The second number has fewer digits — pad with leading zeros if needed.

2

Add ones: 4+7=11, write 1 carry 1. Tens: 2+5+1=8. Hundreds: 6+1=7. Thousands: 9+0=9. Ten-Thousands: 8+3=11 write 1 carry 1. Hundred-Thousands: 3+9+1=13 write 3 carry 1. Millions: 5+0+1=6.

✅ 5,389,624 + 930,157 = 6,319,781

➖ Example 4: Subtract 273,587 from 307,843

1

Ones: 3−7 can't do, borrow. 13−7=6. Tens: (4−1)−8 can't, borrow. 13−8=5. Hundreds: (8−1)−5=2. Thousands: 7−3=4 (but we need to borrow), 17−3−1=13? Check carefully: Result is 034,256.

2

Carry and borrow chain across all digits carefully, working right to left.

✅ 307,843 − 273,587 = 034,256 → 34,256

➗ Example 5: Divide 643,185 ÷ 24

1

64 ÷ 24 = 2 remainder 16 (24×2=48, 64−48=16). Write 2 as first quotient digit.

2

Bring down 3 → 163 ÷ 24 = 6 remainder 19 (24×6=144, 163−144=19). Write 6.

3

Bring down 1 → 191 ÷ 24 = 7 remainder 23 (24×7=168, 191−168=23). Write 7.

4

Bring down 8 → 238 ÷ 24 = 9 remainder 22 (24×9=216, 238−216=22). Write 9.

5

Bring down 5 → 225 ÷ 24 = 9 remainder 9 (24×9=216, 225−216=9). Write 9.

✅ Quotient = 26,799  |  Remainder = 9  (9 < 24 ✓)

🧮 Example 6: BODMAS — Solve 82 − 32 × 2 + (8 + 20 ÷ 4)

B

Work inside brackets: (8 + 20 ÷ 4). First divide: 20 ÷ 4 = 5. Then add: 8 + 5 = 13.

O

No "of" operation here.

D

No more division outside brackets.

M

Multiply: 32 × 2 = 64. Expression becomes: 82 − 64 + 13.

AS

Left to right: 82 − 64 = 18. Then 18 + 13 = 31.

✅ Answer = 31
