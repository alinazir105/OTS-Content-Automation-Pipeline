Background

Why Do We Measure?

Measurement is fundamental to science, engineering, cooking, sports, and everyday life. In this unit, you will learn how to convert between units of distance, work with units of time, and convert between Celsius and Fahrenheit temperature scales.

You'll use multiplication and division to switch between units within each category — and by the end of this unit, you'll be able to solve real-world word problems involving all three topics!

Did You Know?

The word "kilometre" comes from the Greek "khilioi" (thousand) + "metron" (measure). So 1 kilometre literally means 1000 measures!

Units of Length & Distance

Time

Units of Time

Time can be measured in seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, and years. Each unit has a specific relationship with the others.

Relationship Conversion To Larger Unit To Smaller Unit
60 seconds = 1 minute sec ↔ min ÷ 60 × 60
60 minutes = 1 hour min ↔ hr ÷ 60 × 60
24 hours = 1 day hr ↔ day ÷ 24 × 24
7 days = 1 week days ↔ week ÷ 7 × 7
4 weeks = 1 month weeks ↔ month ÷ 4 × 4
12 months = 1 year months ↔ year ÷ 12 × 12

Temperature

Measuring Temperature

Temperature is measured using a thermometer. There are two common scales: Celsius (°C) used in most of the world, and Fahrenheit (°F) used mainly in the United States.

🌡️ Celsius Scale (°C)

0°C = water freezes

100°C = water boils

37°C = normal body temp

🌡️ Fahrenheit Scale (°F)

32°F = water freezes

212°F = water boils

98.6°F = normal body temp

°C → °F Formula
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32 Example: 35°C = (35×9/5)+32 = 63+32 = 95°F
°F → °C Formula
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9 Example: 59°F = (59-32)×5/9 = 27×5/9 = 15°C

Worked Examples

Step-by-Step Examples

Let's work through carefully explained examples from each topic in this unit.

📏 Distance Conversions

Example 1 Convert 8 km 150 m to metres
  • 1
    Identify: we have 8 km and 150 m. Convert km to m by multiplying by 1000.
  • 2
    8 km = 8 × 1000 = 8000 m
  • 3
    Add the remaining metres: 8000 m + 150 m = 8150 m
✅ Answer: 8 km 150 m = 8150 m
Example 2 Convert 18450 m to km and m
  • 1
    Divide 18450 by 1000 to find km: 18450 ÷ 1000 = 18 km with remainder 450
  • 2
    The remainder (450) stays as metres: 450 m
  • 3
    Combined: 18 km 450 m
✅ Answer: 18450 m = 18 km 450 m
Example 3 Add 3 m 35 cm + 8 m 75 cm
  • 1
    Add the centimetres: 35 + 75 = 110 cm = 1 m 10 cm. Write 10 under cm, carry 1 to metres.
  • 2
    Add the metres: 3 + 8 + 1 (carry) = 12 m
  • 3
    Result: 12 m 10 cm
✅ Answer: 3 m 35 cm + 8 m 75 cm = 12 m 10 cm
Example 4 Subtract 4 m 80 cm from 11 m 15 cm
  • 1
    Subtract cm: 15 - 80. We can't do this! Borrow 1 m = 100 cm from the metres column.
  • 2
    Now: 100 + 15 = 115 cm. Then 115 - 80 = 35 cm
  • 3
    Metres: 11 - 1 (borrowed) - 4 = 6 m
✅ Answer: 11 m 15 cm − 4 m 80 cm = 6 m 35 cm

⏰ Time Conversions & Operations

Example 5 Convert 400 minutes to hours and minutes
  • 1
    Divide 400 by 60: 400 ÷ 60 = 6 remainder 40
  • 2
    6 complete hours, with 40 minutes left over
  • 3
    Check: 6 × 60 = 360, and 400 - 360 = 40 ✓
✅ Answer: 400 minutes = 6 hours 40 minutes
Example 6 Add 3 hours 55 minutes + 5 hours 40 minutes
  • 1
    Add minutes: 55 + 40 = 95 minutes = 1 hour 35 minutes. Write 35, carry 1.
  • 2
    Add hours: 3 + 5 + 1 (carry) = 9 hours
  • 3
    Result: 9 hours 35 minutes
✅ Answer: 9 hours 35 minutes
Example 7 Convert 81 months to years and months
  • 1
    Divide 81 by 12 to find years: 81 ÷ 12 = 6 remainder 9
  • 2
    6 complete years, 9 months remaining
  • 3
    Check: 6 × 12 = 72, and 81 - 72 = 9 ✓
✅ Answer: 81 months = 6 years 9 months

🌡️ Temperature Conversions

Example 8 Convert 59°F to Celsius
  • 1
    Use formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • 2
    Subtract 32: 59 − 32 = 27
  • 3
    Multiply by 5/9: 27 × 5/9 = (27÷9) × 5 = 3 × 5 = 15
✅ Answer: 59°F = 15°C
Example 9 Convert 35°C to Fahrenheit
  • 1
    Use formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • 2
    Multiply by 9/5: 35 × 9/5 = (35÷5) × 9 = 7 × 9 = 63
  • 3
    Add 32: 63 + 32 = 95
✅ Answer: 35°C = 95°F
The Celsius scale spans 100 degrees between freezing and boiling. The Fahrenheit scale spans 180 degrees for the same range. So the ratio is 180/100 = 9/5. That's why we multiply by 9/5 to "stretch" Celsius degrees into Fahrenheit degrees!

🏆 Challenge Problems

Ready for harder problems? These multi-step word problems will stretch your thinking!

01

Temperature Comparison

During summer, the temperature in Jacobabad was 113°F, and in Hyderabad was 40°C. Which city was hotter, and by how much (in °F)?

Convert 40°C to °F first. Then compare both in °F to find the difference.
40°C = (40×9/5)+32 = 72+32 = 104°F. Jacobabad (113°F) > Hyderabad (104°F). Difference = 113 - 104 = 9°F hotter in Jacobabad.
02

School Closure Days

School closes for summer for 2 months and 5 days. Other holidays last 27 days. How many total days will school remain closed?

Convert 2 months to days (×30), then add all the days together.
2 months = 60 days. Total summer = 60 + 5 = 65 days. Total closed = 65 + 27 = 92 days.
03

Ali's Fever

Ali has a fever of 104°F. Convert this to °C. Is this above or below the normal body temperature of 37°C?

Use: °C = (°F - 32) × 5/9. First subtract 32, then multiply.
104 - 32 = 72. 72 × 5/9 = 8 × 5 = 40°C. Yes, 40°C is above normal body temperature (37°C). Ali definitely has a fever!
04

Fatima's Homework

Fatima takes 1 hour 20 minutes for Maths homework and 50 minutes for English. What is the total time?

Minutes: 20 + 50 = 70 min = 1 hr 10 min. Hours: 1 + 1 = 2 hr. Total = 2 hours 10 minutes.
