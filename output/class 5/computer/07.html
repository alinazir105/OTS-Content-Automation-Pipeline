📖 Introduction

Multimedia, transitions, and animations add visual appeal and engage your audience. In this unit, you'll learn to insert images, videos, and audio, and create custom animations to make your presentations come alive!

What is Multimedia?

Multimedia refers to the combination of text, images, audio, video, and animations in a single presentation. PowerPoint allows you to blend all these elements to create a powerful, engaging slideshow for your audience.

🖼️ Adding and Adjusting Multimedia

Inserting and Formatting Images

1

Insert Images

  • Click on the Insert tab
  • Select Pictures
  • Choose an image from your device
2

Format Images

  • Go to the Picture Format tab
  • Add borders and effects
  • Make the image partially transparent
3

Cropping & Removing Backgrounds

  • Select Crop from the Picture Format tab
  • Choose Remove Background to erase the background
  • Adjust areas to keep or discard
Insert Tab → Pictures → Select Image from Device Picture Format Tab → Borders / Effects / Transparency Picture Format Tab → Crop (to trim the image) Picture Format Tab → Remove Background (to erase background)
Removing the background of an image helps it blend seamlessly with your slide design. For example, if you have a photo of a person, removing the background lets you place them on any coloured or patterned slide without an awkward white or grey box around them.

Embedding Videos

1

Insert a Video

  • Go to the Insert tab
  • Select Video
  • Choose This Device (for files on your computer) or Online Video
2

Adjust Playback

  • Right-click the video on the slide
  • Choose Playback
  • Set it to play automatically or on click
Insert → Video → This Device OR Online Video Right-click Video → Playback → Set to Automatically / On Click
🎬

Did You Know?

PowerPoint supports video formats like MP4, AVI, MOV, and WMV. For best results, always use MP4 files as they are widely supported and have good compression.

📺

Online Videos

Click to reveal!

You can embed YouTube videos directly into PowerPoint using the Online Video option. The video will play from YouTube during your presentation (requires internet connection).

Adding Audio Clips

1

Insert Audio

  • Select Insert > Audio
  • Choose Audio on My PC
  • Select your sound file
2

Adjust Audio Settings

  • Use audio playback settings to adjust volume
  • Set fade in or fade out timing
  • Choose when the audio starts playing
Insert → Audio → Audio on My PC → Select Sound File Playback Tab → Volume, Fade In, Fade Out settings

Multimedia Types at a Glance

Type ↕ How to Insert ↕ Key Setting ↕ Tab Used ↕
🖼️ Image Insert → Pictures Remove Background, Crop Picture Format
Images can be formatted with borders, effects, and transparency. Use Crop to trim unwanted areas and Remove Background to erase the image background for a cleaner look on your slide.
🎬 Video Insert → Video Autoplay / On Click Playback
Videos can come from your device or online sources. Right-click the video and choose Playback to configure whether it starts automatically when the slide appears or only when you click on it.

Audio files such as music or sound effects can be added to slides. Use the Playback tab to set the volume, add fade-in and fade-out effects, and decide when the audio starts playing.

✨ Advanced Transitions

Transitions are effects that happen between slides when you move from one to the next during a presentation.

1

Select a Transition

  • Go to the Transitions tab
  • Pick an effect like Morph, Push, or Wipe
  • A preview plays automatically
2

Customize the Transition

  • Use Effect Options for direction/style
  • Set Duration to control speed
  • Add Sound for an audio cue
3

Apply to All Slides

  • Select Apply to All
  • Same transition plays throughout the presentation
  • Keeps your presentation consistent
Transitions Tab → Select Effect (Morph / Push / Wipe / Fade) Effect Options → Direction / Style Duration → Speed setting Sound → Add audio cue to transition Apply to All → Same transition on every slide

The Morph transition is a special effect that smoothly moves and transforms objects between two slides. If you duplicate a slide and move an object to a new position, Morph will animate that object gliding into its new position — like magic!

🎭 Animations

Animations are effects applied to objects within a slide — like text boxes, images, or shapes. They control how objects appear, move, or disappear.

Animation Types

🟢

Entrance

Makes objects appear on the slide. Examples: Fade, Float In, Fly In

🔵

Emphasis

Highlights objects already on the slide. Examples: Spin, Grow/Shrink, Pulse

🔴

Exit

Makes objects disappear from the slide. Examples: Fade, Fly Out, Shrink

🟡

Motion Paths

Moves objects along a custom path on the slide. Draw lines, arcs, or loops.

Animation Settings

1

Choose an Animation Type

  • Go to the Animations tab
  • Select from Entrance, Emphasis, Exit, or Motion Paths
2

Animation Settings

  • Use Effect Options to change direction or style
  • On Click – plays when you click
  • With Previous – plays at the same time as the previous animation
  • After Previous – plays automatically after the previous one ends

Using the Animation Pane

What is the Animation Pane?

The Animation Pane is a panel that shows you all the animations on a slide in order. You can use it to:

  • See all animations on the slide at once
  • Change the order of animations
  • Adjust the duration (how long each animation lasts)
  • Add delays between animations
Animations Tab → Animation Pane (opens side panel) Start: On Click | With Previous | After Previous Duration: controls animation speed Delay: adds wait time before animation starts Drag items in Animation Pane to reorder
🎯

Did You Know?

Click to reveal!

Float In is one of the most used Entrance animations in professional presentations because it looks natural and elegant without being distracting.

⏱️

Timing Tip

Click to reveal!

Setting animations to After Previous creates automatic slide shows that run without any clicks — great for kiosk displays or timed presentations!

Animation Start Options Compared

Start Option When it Plays Best Used For
On Click When presenter clicks the mouse Manual control during presentation
Best for interactive presentations where the speaker wants to control exactly when each element appears, keeping the audience engaged without overwhelming them.
With Previous At the same time as the previous animation Multiple objects appearing together
Great when you want two or more objects to animate simultaneously — for example, a title and a subtitle appearing at the same time.
After Previous Automatically after previous animation ends Automated sequence of animations
Perfect for creating automatic sequences, like a bullet list where each point appears one after another without any clicks.

💡 Tips for Using Transitions & Animations

  • ✅Use animations to highlight important information, but avoid overuse — too many animations look unprofessional.
  • 🎨Stick to one or two types of transitions for consistency throughout your presentation.
  • 👁️Preview animations before presenting to ensure they enhance, not distract from, your content.
  • ⚡Keep animation durations short and snappy — animations longer than 1 second can feel slow.
  • 🎯Use Entrance animations for revealing new information, and Emphasis for drawing attention to key points.
  • 🔄The Morph transition is great for showing movement or transformation of objects between slides.

🏋️ Practical Activity

Follow these steps to create an enhanced PowerPoint presentation:

  1. Open a new presentation in MS PowerPoint and create at least 4 slides on a topic of your choice.
  2. Add two images to different slides using Insert → Pictures. Format them with borders and effects using the Picture Format tab.
  3. Insert a short video on one slide using Insert → Video → This Device. Set it to play automatically.
  4. Apply a transition effect to each slide. Use at least 2 different transitions (e.g., Fade and Morph).
  5. Choose an animation effect for at least two objects — one Entrance and one Emphasis animation.
  6. Open the Animation Pane and adjust the timing and order of your animations.
  7. Add a sound effect to one of your transitions using the Sound option in the Transitions tab.
  8. Preview your entire presentation and adjust any animations that feel too fast, too slow, or distracting.
  9. Present your slideshow to your class, explaining the choices you made for animations and transitions.
