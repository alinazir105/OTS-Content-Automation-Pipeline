What is MS Paint?

MS Paint is a simple computer program that lets you draw and paint pictures! 🖼️

You can use tools like brushes 🖌️, pencils ✏️, and erasers 🧹 to make your art.

You can also use it to colour shapes 🟡🟦 and add text to your pictures! ✍️

It is easy to use and fun for making different kinds of images! 😄

You can save your drawings 💾 or set them as your computer background! 🖥️

MS Paint is great for kids who love to create and play with colours! 🌈

🌟 Fun Fact!

MS Paint is like a digital colouring book! You can draw, colour, and create pictures using your mouse or touchpad! 🖱️

Activity: Draw & Write!

Follow these steps in MS Paint! 🖥️

  1. 1 Draw a shape (like a rectangle ▭) on the canvas!
  2. 2 Click on the Text Tool (A) 🅰️ in the toolbar.
  3. 3 Click on the blank space under the Rectangle shape.
  4. 4 Write the name of the shape you drew! ✍️
