Monitor

🖥️
What is a Monitor?

The monitor is like a TV screen for your computer!

📸It shows pictures on the screen.
📺It turns on and off like a TV.
🎬It helps us see and edit photos and videos.
🧠

CPU – The Brain! 🧠

🖨️
What is the CPU?

CPU stands for Central Processing Unit. It is the brain of the computer!

🧠It is the brain of the computer that solves many problems.
💡It helps the computer think, process data and perform calculations.
▶️It runs programs on the computer.
⌨️

Keyboard

⌨️
What is a Keyboard?

We use a keyboard to type letters, words and numbers!

✍️It types letters and words into the computer.
💬It helps us write and communicate with the computer.
🔤We use alphabet keys to type letters and numeric keys to type numbers.
🖱️

Mouse

🖱️
What is a Mouse?

The mouse helps you point and click on things on the screen!

👆It moves the pointer on the screen to click on things.
🔲It helps us select and move items on the computer.
🎮It controls the arrow on the screen to play games and click buttons.
🖐️It also helps us drag and drop objects.
✏️
