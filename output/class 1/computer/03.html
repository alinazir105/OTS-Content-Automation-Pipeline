Key Concepts

🖥️

Monitor

A monitor is like a TV screen for your computer. It shows you pictures, videos, and everything you do on the computer, helping you see and edit your work.

Watching cartoonsSeeing photosPlaying gamesTyping words
⌨️

Keyboard

A keyboard is a tool with many buttons, called keys, that lets you type letters, numbers, and symbols into the computer. It helps you write and communicate.

Typing your nameWriting a storySending messagesEntering numbers
🖱️

Mouse

A mouse is a small device that fits in your hand and helps you move a pointer on the screen. You can click on things, select items, and play games with it.

Clicking on an appDragging a pictureSelecting textPlaying a game
🧠

CPU (System Unit)

The CPU, or System Unit, is like the brain of the computer. It thinks, processes data, performs calculations, and runs all the programs to solve problems.

Making the computer thinkRunning a drawing programCalculating sumsOpening a video
💻

Computer System

A computer system is a group of parts like the monitor, keyboard, mouse, and CPU that work together to help you do many tasks, from playing to learning.

Doing homeworkWatching moviesDrawing picturesBrowsing the internet
✨

Functions

Functions are the specific jobs or tasks that each part of the computer system performs. Knowing their functions helps us understand how to use them.

Monitor shows picturesKeyboard typesMouse clicksCPU thinks
👆

Pointer

The pointer is a small arrow or icon that moves on the computer screen when you move the mouse. It helps you select and interact with items.

Clicking an iconSelecting a menu itemDragging a window

Story Explanation

Once upon a time, there was a friendly computer named Chip. Chip had a big family of parts that loved to work together! His shiny Monitor was like his eyes, showing all the colorful pictures. His busy Keyboard was like his fingers, typing out exciting stories. His quick Mouse was like his pointing finger, helping him click on adventures. And deep inside, his clever CPU was like his brain, thinking super fast to make everything happen. Together, they were the best computer system, ready for any task!

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing the monitor with the CPU.
  • Thinking the mouse types words instead of the keyboard.
  • Believing the keyboard moves the pointer on the screen.
