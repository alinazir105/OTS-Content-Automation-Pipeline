How to Open MS Paint? 🖥️

To open MS Paint, double click on the MS Paint icon on the computer. A big window will open — that's where all the fun happens! 🎉

The Canvas 🎨

The canvas is the big white space in MS Paint. It is like a blank piece of paper! You can draw, colour, and create anything you like on the canvas.

↩️

Undo and Redo Buttons 🔄

Made a mistake? No worries! 😊

👉 Click Undo to go back and remove your mistake.

👉 Click Redo to bring back something you removed.

⌨️

🔑 Shortcut Key for Undo: Ctrl + Z

🔑 Shortcut Key for Redo: Ctrl + Y

🧰

The Shapes Box 🔷🔴⭐

The Shapes Box has lots of shapes you can draw! Circles, squares, triangles, stars, hearts and many more!

How to use a shape:

  • Click on any shape from the Shapes Box to select it
  • Drag on the canvas to draw the shape

Activity – Draw Shapes! 🎯

Follow these steps to draw shapes in MS Paint:

  1. Double click on MS Paint to open it on the computer
  2. Click on any shape from the Shapes Box to select it
  3. Drag the shape on the canvas to draw it

You can draw a circle 🔵, a square 🟦, a star ⭐ and more! Have fun!
