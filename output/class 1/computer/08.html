FONT FORMATTING AND ALIGNMENT IN MS WORD

Let's explore how to make our words look super cool and neat in MS Word!

Learning Objectives

  • Type simple words and short sentences.
  • Change the size, style, and position of the text.

Key Concepts

📝

Font Formatting

Making text look different by changing its size, style, or color. It helps to make your words stand out and look good on the page.

Making a title biggerChanging text colorMaking words bold
📏

Font Size

How big or small your letters appear on the screen. You can choose different sizes to make important words stand out or fit more text.

Making a heading size 24Making body text size 12Shrinking text for a small note
✒️

Font Style

The way your letters look, like bold, italic, or underline. These styles help to emphasize words and add a special touch to your writing.

Making a word **bold**Writing a word *italic*Drawing a line under a word
🅱️

Bold

A font style that makes your text appear thicker and darker. It's used to highlight important words or titles, making them easy to spot.

**Important Notice****STOP****Hello World**
🇮

Italic

A font style that makes your text slant slightly to the right. It's often used for emphasis, titles of books, or foreign words.

*The Little Prince**Wow, that's amazing!**bonjour*
➖

Underline

A font style that draws a line beneath your text. It helps to draw attention to specific words or phrases, making them stand out.

Click hereImportantYour Name
↔️

Alignment

How your text is positioned on the page, such as left, right, or center. It helps to organize your writing and make it look neat.

Centering a titleAligning text to the left marginMoving text to the right
⬅️

Backspace Key

A key on the keyboard used to erase letters to the left of your cursor. It helps you correct mistakes quickly while typing.

Deleting a wrong letterFixing a typoRemoving extra spaces
⏎

Enter Key

A key on the keyboard used to move your cursor to the next line. It helps you start new paragraphs or create space between lines of text.

Starting a new paragraphAdding a blank lineMoving to the next bullet point
⬆️

Caps Lock Key

A key that, when turned on, makes all the letters you type appear in capital letters. It's useful for typing headings or emphasizing words.

Typing "HELLO"Writing "IMPORTANT MESSAGE"Making a word "BIG"
💾

Save Work

Storing your document on the computer so you don't lose it. It's important to save often so your hard work is safe and you can open it again later.

Saving a storySaving a drawingSaving your homework

Fun Facts

Some fonts are designed to be easier to read on screens, while others are best for printing.

Once upon a time, in a magical land called MS Word, lived little letters! They loved to play and change their looks. Sometimes, they wanted to be super BIG (like a title!), sometimes they wanted to lean a little (italic!), and sometimes they wanted to be super strong (bold!). They also learned how to line up neatly on the page, whether it was on the left, in the middle, or on the right. And when they made a mistake, a friendly Backspace key helped them erase it. When they wanted to start a new adventure on a new line, the Enter key was there. For big, important messages, the Caps Lock key made all letters capital! Most importantly, they learned to save their adventures so they could always come back and play another day.

Worked Examples

Example 1: You typed \'hello\' but you wanted it to be \'HELLO\' and very importa
  1. First, use the Backspace key to delete \'hello\'.
  2. Then, turn on the Caps Lock key.
  3. Type \'HELLO\'.
  4. Select \'HELLO\' and click the \'Bold\' button to make it thicker.
Final: **HELLO**
Why: Backspace fixes mistakes, Caps Lock makes capitals, and Bold makes text stand out.
Example 2: You want to write a title \'My Story\' and put it in the middle of the
  1. Type \'My Story\'.
  2. Select \'My Story\'.
  3. Choose \'Center Alignment\' to move it to the middle.
  4. Change the \'Font Size\' to a larger number (e.g., 24) to make it bigger.
Final: A large, centered title: \'My Story\'
Why: Center alignment positions text in the middle, and Font Size controls how big it appears.
