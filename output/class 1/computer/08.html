Activity Time!

Let's practice in MS Word! Follow these steps one by one. You can do it! 💪

1

🔡 Change Font Size

Open MS Word and type a short sentence. Then change the size of the text to make it larger or smaller. Try size 12, then size 36!

2

✏️ Apply Font Style

Select your sentence and apply bold, italic, or underline to see the different styles. Try all three!

3

📐 Adjust Alignment

Try aligning your sentence to the left, right, and center of the page. Watch it move!

4

💾 Save Your Work

After making these changes, save your work by clicking on the "Save" icon (the little floppy disk 💾 at the top)!
