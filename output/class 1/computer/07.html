Microsoft Word!

🖥️
Tap Me!
MS Word looks like a big blue "W" on your computer screen! It helps you type words and sentences! 📝
🖱️
Open Word!
Click TWICE (Double Click) on the MS Word icon to open it! Click… click! 🎉
✍️
The Cursor!
The cursor is a blinking line | that shows you WHERE your letters will appear when you type! 🌟
⌨️
Start Typing!
Click inside the blank white area of MS Word, then use the keyboard to type words and sentences! 🎊
⭐ 🌈 ⭐
1

Find the Icon 🔍

Look for the MS Word icon (big blue W) on your computer screen!

2

Double Click! 🖱️

Double click on the icon to open MS Word!

3

Click to Type! ✏️

Click inside the blank white area of MS Word to start typing!

4

Use the Keyboard! ⌨️

Press the keys on the keyboard to type your words and sentences!

The Keyboard!

The keyboard has many different kinds of keys! Let's learn about them! 🌟

🔢 Number Keys

  • 1 Type numbers!
  • 2 For counting!

🔤 Letter Keys

  • A Type letters!
  • Z All 26 letters!

🎛️ Special Keys

  • Space Make a space!
  • Enter New line!
  • Backspace Delete!
  • Shift Capital letters!
  • Esc Quit / Escape!

🎮 Function Keys

  • F1–F12 Special functions!

🏹 Arrow Keys

  • ↑ Move up!
  • ↓ Move down!
  • ← Move left!
  • → Move right!

🔢 Number Pad

  • 0–9 Numbers on the right!

⚡ Magic Ctrl Keys (Shortcuts!)

Ctrl+C = Copy 📋 Ctrl+V = Paste 📌 Ctrl+X = Cut ✂️ Ctrl+S = Save 💾 Ctrl+A = Select All 🌟 Ctrl+Z = Undo ↩️ Ctrl+P = Print 🖨️ Ctrl+B = Bold 𝐁 Ctrl+F = Find 🔍 Ctrl+N = New 📄
