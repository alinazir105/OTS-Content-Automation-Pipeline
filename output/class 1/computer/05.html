How to Hold the Mouse

✋🖱️
Tap me to see!
👆 TAP TO SEE!
1

👍 Put your THUMB on the LEFT side of the mouse.

2

💍 Put your RING FINGER and LITTLE FINGER on the RIGHT side.

3

☝️ Put your INDEX FINGER on the LEFT button.

4

🖕 Put your MIDDLE FINGER on the RIGHT button.

5

🤗 Hold the mouse GENTLY — don't squeeze it!

6

😴 When you are NOT using the mouse — let it go!

📋 Always put the mouse on a FLAT surface or a mouse pad!
