🤔 What is a Computer?

In our daily life, we use machines to make our work faster and easier — like fans, bicycles, and washing machines!

A computer is a smart electronic machine 🧠 that helps us do many tasks and solve problems.

❓ Questions & Answers

  • Q1: What is a machine?
    A machine is a tool or device that helps us do our work faster and easier. Examples include fans, bicycles, and washing machines.
  • Q2: What is a computer?
    A computer is a smart electronic machine that helps us do many tasks and solve problems. It works very fast! ⚡
