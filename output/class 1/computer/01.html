What Is A Computer?

🖥️

In our daily life, we use machines to make our work faster and easier, like fans 🌀, bicycles 🚲, and washing machines 🫧.

A computer is a smart electronic machine that helps us do many tasks and solve problems! 🧩

🖥️ Computer = Smart Electronic Machine that Helps Us! 🤖
