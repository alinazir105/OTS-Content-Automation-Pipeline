🌟 The Magic Symbols!

The crocodile always wants to eat the BIGGER number! 🐊
Look at these 3 special symbols:

🐊➡️ > Greater Than
Big number FIRST!
🐊😐 = Equal To
Both the SAME!
⬅️🐊 < Less Than
Big number LAST!
💡 Remember: The open mouth 🐊 always faces the BIGGER number! If you see 5 > 3, the mouth opens toward 5 because 5 is bigger!
