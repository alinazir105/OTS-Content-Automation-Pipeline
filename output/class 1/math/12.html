What is Multiplication?

Big Idea

Multiplication is fast adding! Instead of adding the same number again and again, we multiply! 🎯

🍎🍎🍎
3 + 3 = 6
✕
2 × 3 = 6

Both give us 6 — multiplication is just faster! 🏃‍♂️💨
