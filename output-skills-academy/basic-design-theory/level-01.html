The Concept

Design = Intentional Communication

"Design is not about making things look pretty. Design is about making things work — for real people, in real situations."

Every color, every shape, every size, every space on a label, a poster, or a screen was a choice. Design is the skill of making those choices on purpose — not by accident.

01

What is this for?

Every design has a job. A warning sign needs to be seen instantly. A restaurant menu needs to make food sound delicious. A medicine bottle needs to save lives.

02

Who is it for?

Design is always for someone. A sign for a child uses different words than one for a doctor. A brand for teenagers looks different from one for grandparents. Audience changes everything.

03

Does it work?

This is the only question that matters at the end. Can people use it? Do they understand it? Does it do its job? Good design always works. Great design is invisible.
