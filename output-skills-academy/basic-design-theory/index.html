Course Overview

Course Philosophy

Basic Design Theory is OTS's skill-based visual thinking course. It is not a software tutorial. It is not a Canva class. It is a course about how humans communicate, organise, express, and persuade through the visual world — and how to do it consciously, skillfully, and creatively.

The course has 12 Levels. It is designed for everyone — from a 10-year-old who wants to understand why some posters look amazing, to a teenager who wants to design for real, to an adult exploring creativity for the first time.

The Four Gaps This Course Solves

Seeing Gap

Students look but they do not see. They pass posters and screens every day without noticing what makes them work.

Vocabulary Gap

Students have opinions about design but no language to describe or defend them.

Making Gap

Students want to create but feel they lack talent. Design is a skill, not a gift.

Thinking Gap

Students copy trends but do not understand the principles behind great design.
