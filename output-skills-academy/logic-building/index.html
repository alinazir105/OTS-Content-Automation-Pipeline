Course Overview

Course Philosophy

Logic Building is OTS's skill-based thinking course. It is not a mathematics class. It is not a coding class. It is a course about how to think clearly, solve problems step by step, identify patterns, reason through challenges, and make decisions logically.

The course has 12 Levels. Level rank determines difficulty — not age or class. It is designed for complete beginners through to advanced learners.

Every level builds on the last — from basic structured thinking all the way to designing complete logical systems from scratch.

The Problem

The Four Gaps This Course Solves

Most students have been taught what to think — not how to think. This course closes four critical gaps.

🧠

Thinking Gap

Students react but do not reason. They answer without understanding why their answer is right or wrong.

طلباء ردعمل دیتے ہیں لیکن سوچتے نہیں۔
🔗

Pattern Gap

Students memorise but do not see connections. They store information in silos instead of building a mental map.

طلباء یاد کرتے ہیں لیکن رابطے نہیں دیکھتے۔
🧩

Problem-Solving Gap

Students give up when there is no obvious answer. They need a clear method to work through ambiguity.

طلباء مشکل سوالوں کے سامنے ہار مان لیتے ہیں۔
🎯

Confidence Gap

Students fear being wrong, so they avoid thinking altogether. Logic training builds the courage to reason out loud.

طلباء غلط ہونے سے ڈرتے ہیں، اس لیے سوچنے سے بچتے ہیں۔
