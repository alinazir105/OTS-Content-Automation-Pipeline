Core Concept

The Three Types of Patterns

Patterns come in three flavours. Once you know the flavour, you know the rule. Once you know the rule, you can predict anything. Let's break them down.

🔶
Visual Patterns

Shapes, colours, and arrangements that repeat in a predictable order. Your eyes spot these before your brain even tries.

🔵 🔷 🔺 🔵 🔷 🔺
بصری پیٹرن
🔢
Numerical Patterns

Numbers that follow a rule — add 2, multiply by 3, double each time. Find the rule, predict the next number.

2 → 4 → 8 → 16 → 32
عددی پیٹرن
🧩
Logical Patterns

Rules about relationships between ideas. "Every time it rains, the ground is wet." That's a logical pattern — cause and effect.

IF rain → wet ground
منطقی پیٹرن

The Famous One: Fibonacci Sequence

The Fibonacci sequence is one of nature's favourite patterns. Each number is the sum of the two before it. It appears in sunflower seeds, spiral shells, and even your social media feed algorithm.

Hover or tap any number to see how it's made. Rule: F(n) = F(n−1) + F(n−2)

Real-World Connection

Where Pattern Logic Lives

Pattern recognition isn't an abstract skill — it's the engine behind some of the most powerful technologies and natural systems on Earth. Here's where you'll find it in the wild.

🎵

Spotify Recommendations

Spotify analyses patterns in millions of songs: tempo, key, energy, danceability. When you listen to a song, it spots patterns in your taste and matches them to patterns in other songs.

IF user likes: fast tempo + minor key → RECOMMEND: similar energy songs
سپوٹیفائی پیٹرن سے گانے تجویز کرتا ہے۔
⛅

Weather Forecasting

Meteorologists don't guess — they look for patterns in pressure, temperature, and wind that they've seen thousands of times before. Pattern = prediction.

Low pressure + rising humidity → rain pattern detected → 80% chance of rain
موسم پیش گوئی پیٹرن پر مبنی ہے۔
🏏

Cricket Team Selection

Selectors in Pakistan look at batting patterns: does this batter score well under pressure? Against spinners? On slow pitches? Every match adds data to the pattern.

Averages 45+ in Test matches + performs in spin conditions → selected for tour
کرکٹ ٹیم سلیکشن میں پیٹرن کا استعمال
🚦

Karachi Traffic Management

Traffic engineers study congestion patterns: which roads jam at 8 AM, which routes get clear after school hours. Pattern recognition → smarter signal timing.

IF time = 8:00–9:30 AM AND location = Shahra-e-Faisal → extend green light by 15s
کراچی ٹریفک پیٹرن سے ٹریفک لائٹ کنٹرول ہوتی ہے۔
